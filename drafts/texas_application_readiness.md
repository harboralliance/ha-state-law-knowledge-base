# Texas Application Readiness

## Overview of Requirements for Texas OAG Application Submission

### Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging Employees

The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established specific requirements for human trafficking training. House Bill 390 mandates that all employees at commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms and all employees at any lodging establishment in the state complete annual training related to identifying and responding to human trafficking.

### Standards to Meet

1. **Annual Training Requirement**:
   - Employees of lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms must complete the required training annually.
   - All other lodging establishment employees must also complete the training, regardless of the number of rooms.

2. **Training Content**:
   - The training must cover topics such as recognizing signs of trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and understanding anti-trafficking signage requirements.

3. **Approved Training Materials**:
   - Employers are required to use approved training videos available online or from certain organizations.

### Materials Needed

1. **Human Trafficking Training Videos**:
   - Accessible through approved sources.

2. **Anti-Trafficking Signage**:
   - Required for all lodging establishments in the state, regardless of size.
   - Specifications and examples provided by the OAG.

3. **Training Documentation**:
   - Records of employee training completion, including dates and methods of training.

### Likely Review Risks/Gaps

1. **Compliance with Annual Training Requirement**:
   - Risk: Failure to provide annual training for all relevant employees.
   - Gap: Lack of documentation or evidence of annual compliance.

2. **Proper Use of Approved Training Materials**:
   - Risk: Using unapproved or outdated training materials.
   - Gap: Inconsistent or inadequate training content across the organization.

3. **Effective Anti-Trafficking Signage Placement**:
   - Risk: Insufficient or improperly displayed anti-trafficking signage.
   - Gap: Lack of compliance with signage requirements in all lodging establishments.

### Recommendations

1. **Ensure Annual Training Compliance**:
   - Schedule and conduct annual training sessions for all relevant employees.
   - Maintain detailed records of training completion.

2. **Verify Training Materials**:
   - Confirm that the training videos used are approved by the OAG or other authorized sources.
   - Regularly update training content to ensure relevance and effectiveness.

3. **Proper Signage Installation**:
   - Ensure all lodging establishments display anti-trafficking signage in designated locations.
   - Conduct regular inspections to verify compliance with signage requirements.

By addressing these risks and gaps, the Harbor Alliance can prepare for a successful Texas OAG application submission while ensuring compliance with state regulations on human trafficking training.
