# Texas Application Readiness

## Overview

The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established specific requirements for human trafficking training for workers in commercial lodging establishments. House Bill 390 mandates annual training on identifying, responding to, and reporting signs of human trafficking. This document outlines what Harbor Alliance must prepare to ensure compliance with these requirements.

## Materials Needed

1. **Approved Training Videos**:
   - Accessible online or through certain organizations.
   - Ensure the videos meet OAG's approved criteria for human trafficking training content.

2. **Signage**:
   - Anti-trafficking signage as required by law.
   - Ensure compliance with any specific requirements provided by the OAG or other regulatory bodies.

3. **Training Materials**:
   - Comprehensive training materials including handouts, guides, and any additional resources recommended by the OAG.

## Standards to Meet

1. **Annual Training Requirement**:
   - Provide annual human trafficking training to all employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms.
   - Ensure that all employees at lodging establishments receive mandatory training regardless of size.

2. **Training Content**:
   - Training must cover identifying signs of trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and understanding the requirements for posting anti-trafficking signage.

3. **Employee Interaction**:
   - Employees should be equipped to recognize human trafficking indicators and know how to report such incidents promptly.

## Likely Review Risks/Gaps

1. **Training Compliance**:
   - Verify that all required training sessions are completed by employees.
   - Ensure that the training content aligns with OAG-approved materials.

2. **Signage Availability**:
   - Confirm that anti-trafficking signage is placed in all designated locations within each lodging establishment.
   - Check that the signage complies with any additional requirements provided by the OAG or other regulatory bodies.

3. **Employee Awareness**:
   - Assess employees' understanding of human trafficking and their ability to report incidents.
   - Conduct a follow-up training assessment if necessary to ensure ongoing awareness and preparedness.

## Conclusion

Harbor Alliance must prepare for Texas OAG application submission by ensuring compliance with the annual human trafficking training requirement, obtaining approved training materials, and making sure all necessary signage is in place. Regular review and assessment of these requirements will help identify any gaps or risks that need to be addressed to ensure full compliance with the regulations.
