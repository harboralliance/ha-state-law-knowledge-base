# Texas Application Readiness

## Human Trafficking Training Requirement for Commercial Lodging Establishments in Texas

### Overview
The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established requirements for human trafficking training for workers in commercial lodging establishments. Specifically, House Bill 390 requires that such training be provided annually to all employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, and to employees at all lodging establishments in the state regardless of size.

### Key Points:
1. **Training Requirement**:
   - **Effective Date**: Ongoing requirement since HB 390 passed.
   - **Frequency**: Annual training for large establishments (10+ rooms), bi-annual or as needed for small establishments.
   - **Purpose**: To educate employees on identifying and responding to signs of human trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and understanding anti-trafficking signage.

2. **Applicability**:
   - **Businesses Covered**: All commercial lodging establishments in Texas.
   - **Employees Affected**: All employees within these establishments.

3. **Materials Needed**:
   - Harbor Alliance must provide or locate appropriate training materials approved by the OAG.
   - These may include training videos, manuals, and anti-trafficking signage.

4. **Standards to Meet**:
   - Training must cover:
     - Identifying signs of human trafficking
     - Reporting suspected incidents
     - Understanding requirements for posting anti-trafficking signage
   - The training duration can vary but should be effective and engaging.
   - Employers are responsible for ensuring compliance with the required frequency.

5. **Likely Review Risks/Gaps**:
   - Risk: Non-compliance with annual training requirements.
     - Gap: Lack of proper documentation or proof of completion.
   - Risk: Inadequate understanding of signs and reporting procedures.
     - Gap: Employees may not be prepared to take appropriate actions in real scenarios.
   - Risk: Insufficient anti-trafficking signage visibility.
     - Gap: Potential lack of accessibility to employees.

### Conclusion
Harbor Alliance must ensure that all commercial lodging establishments comply with the annual human trafficking training requirement as mandated by HB 390. This includes providing or acquiring approved training materials, conducting effective and engaging training sessions, maintaining proper documentation of employee completion, and ensuring adequate anti-trafficking signage visibility within the premises.

### Steps for Preparation:
1. **Identify Training Providers**: Contact Harbor Alliance to locate approved training videos, manuals, and other materials.
2. **Develop Training Schedule**: Plan annual training sessions for all employees in commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, and bi-annual or as needed for smaller establishments.
3. **Ensure Documentation**: Maintain detailed records of training completion for each employee.
4. **Anti-Trafficking Signage**: Purchase and strategically place anti-trafficking signage throughout the premises.

### Review and Compliance:
Regularly review the effectiveness of the training program and signage to ensure compliance with OAG requirements. Conduct assessments to identify areas for improvement and address any gaps promptly.

By following these guidelines, Harbor Alliance can effectively prepare for and comply with the Texas OAG's human trafficking training requirement.
