# Texas Application Readiness

## Overview
The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established requirements for human trafficking training for workers in commercial lodging establishments. Specifically, House Bill 390 requires that such training be provided annually to all employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, and to employees at all lodging establishments in the state regardless of size.

## Materials Needed
- Harbor Alliance must prepare a human trafficking training program that meets OAG's requirements.
- The training should cover topics related to identifying and responding to human trafficking, including recognizing signs of trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and understanding the requirements for posting anti-trafficking signage.
- Online or in-person training videos approved by the Office are available.

## Standards to Meet
- **Training Content**: The program must include:
  - Identifying signs of human trafficking
  - Reporting suspected incidents
  - Understanding the requirements for posting anti-trafficking signage
- **Frequency**: Employees at commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms must receive annual training. All employees in all lodging establishments must complete the required training.
- **Duration**: Training sessions should be effective and interactive.

## Likely Review Risks/Gaps
- **Compliance Verification**: There may be a need to verify that all employees have completed the required training.
- **Training Effectiveness**: The effectiveness of the training program needs to be assessed to ensure it meets the required standards.
- **Signage Compliance**: Ensure that anti-trafficking signage is properly posted and visible to employees.

## Conclusion
Harbor Alliance must prepare a comprehensive human trafficking training program that meets OAG's requirements. Key elements include effective content, regular frequency of training, and adherence to reporting guidelines. The team should also be prepared to review the training program for compliance and effectiveness.
