# Texas Application Readiness

## Human Trafficking Training Requirements for Commercial Lodging Establishments in Texas

The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established requirements for human trafficking training for workers in commercial lodging establishments. Specifically, House Bill 390 mandates that such training be provided annually to all employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms and to employees at all lodging establishments regardless of size.

### Materials Needed

- **Approved Training Videos**: Employers can access these videos online or through specific organizations.
- **Anti-Trafficking Signage**: Anti-trafficking signage must be posted in prominent locations within the establishment.
- **Training Completion Records**: Documentation that training has been completed by all employees.

### Standards to Meet

1. **Annual Training for Employees with 10 or More Rooms**:
   - Content: Topics related to identifying and responding to human trafficking, including recognizing signs of trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and understanding anti-trafficking signage requirements.
   - Duration: The training must be completed annually.

2. **Training for All Lodging Establishment Employees**:
   - Content: Similar to the annual training but tailored to all employees regardless of the size of the lodging establishment.
   - Frequency: While not specified, it is implied that this training should be provided as part of regular onboarding and refresher courses.

### Likely Review Risks/Gaps

1. **Training Coverage**:
   - Risk: Ensuring that the annual training covers all required topics comprehensively.
   - Gap: The need to verify that the chosen training videos meet the specific requirements outlined in House Bill 390.

2. **Employee Participation and Completion**:
   - Risk: Ensuring that all required employees complete the annual training.
   - Gap: Potential gaps in tracking employee attendance and completion records, leading to compliance issues.

3. **Signage Placement**:
   - Risk: Properly posting anti-trafficking signage in prominent locations throughout the establishment.
   - Gap: The possibility of forgetting or neglecting to post the required signage in all necessary areas.

4. **Documentation and Reporting**:
   - Risk: Maintaining accurate documentation of training completion and signage placement for potential audits by the OAG.
   - Gap: The need for a robust system to track and report on compliance with the requirements, particularly for establishments with multiple locations or franchises.

### Conclusion

To ensure readiness for the Texas OAG application submission, Harbor Alliance must:
- Identify and select appropriate human trafficking training videos that meet the specified requirements.
- Schedule annual training sessions for all relevant employees.
- Ensure proper posting of anti-trafficking signage in all designated locations.
- Maintain thorough documentation of training completion and signage placement to demonstrate compliance with House Bill 390.

By addressing these risks and gaps, Harbor Alliance can confidently prepare its application and meet the requirements set forth by the Texas OAG.
