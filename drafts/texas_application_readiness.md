# Texas Application Readiness

## Preparation for Texas OAG Application Submission

### Materials Needed:
1. **Human Trafficking Training Videos**: Access approved training videos online or in-person at certain organizations.
2. **Training Documentation**: Proof of completion for each employee who has received the annual human trafficking training.
3. **Anti-Trafficking Signage**: Ensure anti-trafficking signage is posted as required.

### Standards to Meet:
1. **Annual Training Requirement**: Provide human trafficking training annually to all employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, and to all employees at smaller lodging establishments.
2. **Training Content**: The training must cover identifying signs of human trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and understanding anti-trafficking signage requirements.
3. **Signage Compliance**: Post anti-trafficking signage in a visible location within the establishment.

### Likely Review Risks/Gaps:
1. **Training Coverage**: Verify that all required topics are covered in the training videos and documentation.
2. **Employee Participation**: Ensure all employees have completed the training and obtained necessary documentation.
3. **Signage Display**: Confirm that anti-trafficking signage is posted in a visible location and accessible to all employees.
4. **Compliance Verification**: Review the application for any gaps or missing information as per the Texas OAG guidelines.

### Additional Resources:
- **Texas Office of the Attorney General (OAG)**: For detailed requirements and application instructions specific to Texas.
- **Online Training Platforms**: Various platforms offering human trafficking training that meets the Texas OAG standards.
