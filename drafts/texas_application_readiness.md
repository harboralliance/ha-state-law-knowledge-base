# Texas Application Readiness

## Overview
The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established specific requirements for human trafficking training for workers in commercial lodging establishments. This document outlines what the Harbor Alliance must prepare to meet these requirements and includes necessary materials, standards, and potential review risks/gaps.

## Required Training for Commercial Lodging Establishments

### Standards to Meet
1. **Training Frequency**: 
   - All employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms: annually
   - Employees at all lodging establishments in the state regardless of size: annually

2. **Content of Training**:
   - Recognizing signs of human trafficking
   - Reporting suspected incidents
   - Understanding the requirements for posting anti-trafficking signage

### Materials Needed
1. **Training Videos**: Office of the Attorney General has approved several training videos that meet the requirements and can be accessed online or in-person at certain organizations.
2. **Anti-Trafficking Signage**: Employers must ensure that all relevant staff members have access to anti-trafficking signage.

### Potential Review Risks/Gaps
1. **Compliance Verification**: Ensuring that annual training is completed by all employees within the specified timeframe (e.g., 10+ room establishments annually, all establishments annually).
2. **Documentation**: Maintaining accurate records of training completion and anti-trafficking signage distribution.
3. **Signage Placement**: Verifying that anti-trafficking signage is prominently displayed in a location where all staff can easily see it.

## Compliance Timeline
- **Immediate Action**:
  - Identify all employees who will need annual training.
  - Schedule the initial set of training for all relevant employees.
  - Obtain and distribute the approved training videos.
  - Purchase or create anti-trafficking signage that meets the required specifications.

- **Ongoing Monitoring**:
  - Set up a system to track completion of annual training.
  - Ensure that anti-trafficking signage is updated annually as required.
  - Regularly review compliance with the training requirements and signage placement.

## Conclusion
The Harbor Alliance must ensure that all employees in commercial lodging establishments receive the required human trafficking training annually and that anti-trafficking signage is prominently displayed. By following these guidelines and maintaining detailed records of training completion, the Harbor Alliance can meet the OAG's requirements for Texas lodging establishments.
