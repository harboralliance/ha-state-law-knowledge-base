# Texas Application Readiness

## Overview

The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established requirements for human trafficking training for workers in commercial lodging establishments. The key requirement is that such training be provided annually to all employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, and to employees at all lodging establishments in the state regardless of size.

## Materials Needed

- **Training Videos**: Office-approved videos that meet the requirements can be accessed online or in-person at certain organizations.
- **Anti-Trafficking Signage**: Anti-trafficking signage must be posted within the lodging establishment.

## Standards to Meet

1. **Annual Training Requirement**:
   - Train all employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms annually.
   - Train all employees in any lodging establishment regardless of size annually.

2. **Training Content**:
   - Topics related to identifying and responding to human trafficking, including recognizing signs of trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and understanding the requirements for posting anti-trafficking signage.

3. **Training Format**:
   - Training must be completed online or in-person at certain organizations.
   - Duration: The specific duration is not detailed in the source material but should align with industry standards to ensure comprehensive coverage.

## Likely Review Risks/Gaps

1. **Compliance Verification**:
   - Ensure that the training videos are official and meet the OAG's requirements.
   - Verify that all employees have completed the annual training.
   - Confirm that anti-trafficking signage is prominently displayed within the establishment.

2. **Employee Training Records**:
   - Maintain records of completed training sessions for all affected employees, including dates and materials used.
   - Ensure that new hires receive the required initial training within six months of joining the role.

3. **Signage Compliance**:
   - Verify that the anti-trafficking signage is compliant with OAG standards.
   - Ensure that the signage is easily visible to all staff members at all times.

4. **Training Effectiveness**:
   - Assess the effectiveness of the training program by evaluating employee knowledge and understanding of human trafficking prevention measures.
   - Consider conducting follow-up assessments or refresher courses to reinforce learning.

5. **Ongoing Awareness**:
   - Implement an ongoing awareness campaign to keep employees informed about the latest developments in human trafficking prevention and response.

## Conclusion

To ensure readiness for the Texas OAG application submission, Harbor Alliance must focus on providing comprehensive annual training for all relevant employees, maintaining accurate records of completed training sessions, ensuring compliance with signage requirements, assessing the effectiveness of training programs, and implementing an ongoing awareness campaign. By addressing these risks and gaps, Harbor Alliance can demonstrate its commitment to meeting the OAG's requirements and promoting a safe working environment in commercial lodging establishments.
