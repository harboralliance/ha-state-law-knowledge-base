# Texas Application Readiness

## Overview
The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established requirements for human trafficking training for workers in commercial lodging establishments as outlined in House Bill 390. This document summarizes what Harbor Alliance must prepare for submitting an OAG application to Texas.

## Materials Needed
1. **Human Trafficking Training Videos**: Harbor Alliance must access and provide approved training videos that meet the requirements set by the Office of the Attorney General.
2. **Training Documentation**: Documentation of completed training sessions, including employee attendance records and completion certificates.
3. **Anti-Trafficking Signage**: Harbor Alliance needs to purchase or create anti-trafficking signage as required by the state law.

## Standards to Meet
1. **Annual Training Requirement**: Harbor Alliance must ensure that all employees in commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms complete annual human trafficking training.
2. **Signage Compliance**: All locations, regardless of size, must post anti-trafficking signage.
3. **Training Coverage**: The training must cover topics related to identifying and responding to human trafficking, including recognizing signs of trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and understanding the requirements for posting anti-trafficking signage.

## Likely Review Risks/Gaps
1. **Training Completion Records**: Harbor Alliance should ensure that all employee attendance records and completion certificates are accurate and up-to-date.
2. **Signage Placement**: Verify that anti-trafficking signage is properly displayed in all locations, ensuring it is visible to employees.
3. **Compliance Verification**: Regularly review compliance with the training requirements to ensure ongoing adherence.
4. **Employee Awareness**: While training covers reporting suspected incidents, Harbor Alliance should also reinforce the importance of immediate action and escalation procedures within their organization.

## Conclusion
Harbor Alliance must prepare comprehensive human trafficking training documentation, ensure signage compliance, and maintain accurate records to meet Texas OAG application requirements. By addressing these key areas, Harbor Alliance can demonstrate its commitment to protecting employees from human trafficking and meeting state compliance standards.
