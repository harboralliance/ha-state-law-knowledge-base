# Texas Application Readiness

## Overview
The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established requirements for human trafficking training for workers in commercial lodging establishments. This document outlines what Harbor Alliance must prepare for submitting an application to OAG.

## Materials Needed
1. **Training Curriculum**: Ensure that the curriculum meets the requirements set by the Office of the Attorney General.
2. **Employee Training Records**: Keep records of all employee training sessions, including dates and completion statuses.
3. **Anti-Trafficking Signage**: Obtain or create signage that complies with OAG guidelines for posting in all lodging establishments.

## Standards to Meet
1. **Annual Training**: Provide human trafficking training annually to all employees at commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, and to employees at all lodging establishments regardless of size.
2. **Training Content**: The training must cover topics related to identifying and responding to human trafficking, including recognizing signs of trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and understanding the requirements for posting anti-trafficking signage.
3. **Signage**: Ensure that anti-trafficking signage is prominently displayed in all lodging establishments.

## Likely Review Risks/Gaps
1. **Training Compliance**: Verify that the training materials are up-to-date and meet OAG standards.
2. **Record Keeping**: Ensure that all employee training records are accurate, complete, and properly documented.
3. **Signage Availability**: Confirm that anti-trafficking signage is available and placed in appropriate locations within all lodging establishments.
