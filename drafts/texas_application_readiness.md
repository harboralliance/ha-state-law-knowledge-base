# Texas Application Readiness

## Overview
Texas requires that commercial lodging establishments provide annual human trafficking training to their employees. This includes all employees at establishments with 10 or more rooms, as well as employees at all lodging establishments regardless of size.

### Materials Needed
- Harbor Alliance must obtain the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) approved training videos.
- They should also prepare internal documents outlining the training program, including employee records of completion and signage requirements.

### Standards to Meet
1. **Annual Training Requirement**: All employees at lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms must complete the annual human trafficking training.
2. **Training Content**: The training must cover identifying signs of human trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and understanding anti-trafficking signage requirements.
3. **Signage**: Employees must be trained on the proper posting of anti-trafficking signage in their workplace.

### Likely Review Risks/Gaps
1. **Compliance Verification**: Harbor Alliance must ensure that all employees complete the required training and maintain records to verify compliance.
2. **Training Effectiveness**: There is a risk that some employees may not fully understand the material, especially if the training videos are complex or poorly designed.
3. **Signage Compliance**: Risk of improper signage placement and insufficient understanding of its significance by employees.

### Additional Resources
- Harbor Alliance should consider partnering with local organizations such as the Texas Coalition Against Sexual Assault (TCASA) to obtain additional resources and expertise in human trafficking prevention.
- They can also refer to the National Human Trafficking Resource Center for further guidance on anti-trafficking training.
