# Texas Application Readiness

## Overview
The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established requirements for human trafficking training for workers in commercial lodging establishments. House Bill 390 mandates annual training for employees at lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, and all employees at other lodging establishments.

## Materials Needed
- Approved training videos from the Office of the Attorney General
- Documentation proving completion of the required training

## Standards to Meet
1. **Annual Training**: All employees must complete the required annual human trafficking training.
2. **Training Content**: The training must cover:
   - Identifying and responding to human trafficking
   - Recognizing signs of trafficking
   - Reporting suspected incidents
   - Understanding anti-trafficking signage requirements

## Likely Review Risks/Gaps
1. **Compliance Monitoring**: Ensure that all required training is completed annually.
2. **Signage Requirements**: Verify compliance with posting anti-trafficking signage.
3. **Employee Awareness**: Conduct periodic assessments to ensure ongoing awareness and retention of training content.

## Conclusion
To be ready for the Texas OAG application submission, the Harbor Alliance must ensure that all employees complete the required human trafficking training annually and comply with signage requirements. Regular reviews and assessments will help identify any gaps in knowledge or adherence to standards.
