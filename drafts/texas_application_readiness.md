# Texas Application Readiness

## Overview
The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas requires human trafficking training for workers in commercial lodging establishments. This requirement affects lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, as well as all lodging establishments regardless of size.

## Materials Needed
1. **Approved Training Videos**: Harbor Alliance must access and utilize the approved training videos that meet OAG requirements.
2. **Employee Records**: Maintain records of training completion for each employee, including dates and methods of training delivery.

## Standards to Meet
1. **Annual Training**: All employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms must receive annual human trafficking training.
2. **Comprehensive Content**: The training must cover identifying signs of human trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and understanding anti-trafficking signage requirements.
3. **Training Frequency**: Employees at all lodging establishments must complete the required annual training.

## Likely Review Risks/Gaps
1. **Timeliness of Training**: Ensure that all employees receive the training within the specified timeframe to avoid non-compliance penalties.
2. **Comprehensive Coverage**: Verify that the training materials cover all required topics thoroughly and comprehensively.
3. **Employee Engagement**: Assess whether employees are fully engaged and understand the importance of human trafficking training.
4. **Documentation Accuracy**: Maintain accurate records of training completion and ensure they meet OAG requirements.

## Next Steps
1. **Access Training Materials**: Harbor Alliance must log in to the provided platforms or organizations to access the approved training videos.
2. **Schedule Training Sessions**: Organize training sessions that align with employee schedules, ensuring all required staff participate.
3. **Document Participation**: Keep detailed records of each employee's completion of the training, including dates and methods used.
4. **Review and Validate**: Review training completion records to ensure they meet OAG requirements and identify any gaps or risks that need addressing.

## Conclusion
Harbor Alliance must be prepared to comply with Texas OAG's human trafficking training requirements for commercial lodging establishments. By accessing approved training videos, scheduling timely sessions, documenting participation accurately, and validating compliance, Harbor Alliance can ensure readiness for submission of their application.
