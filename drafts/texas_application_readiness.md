# Texas Application Readiness

## Human Trafficking Training Requirements for Commercial Lodging Establishments

### Overview
The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established requirements for human trafficking training for workers in commercial lodging establishments. Specifically, House Bill 390 mandates that such training be provided annually to all employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms and to employees at all lodging establishments regardless of size.

### Key Points:

1. **Training Requirement**:
   - **Effective Date**: Ongoing (annual)
   - **Duration**: Not specified in the source material
   - **Purpose**: To educate employees on identifying and responding to signs of human trafficking

2. **Applicability**:
   - **Businesses Covered**: All commercial lodging establishments in Texas
   - **Employees Affected**: All employees, including those at establishments with fewer than 10 rooms

3. **Exceptions**:
   - No specific exceptions are mentioned in the source material

### Training Content
The required training must cover topics related to identifying and responding to human trafficking, including:
- Recognizing signs of trafficking
- Reporting suspected incidents
- Understanding the requirements for posting anti-trafficking signage

### Materials Needed
1. **Approved Training Videos**: Employers can access approved training videos online or in-person at certain organizations.
2. **Anti-Trafficking Signage**: Employees are required to ensure that anti-trafficking signage is posted within their establishments.

### Standards to Meet
- Ensure compliance with the requirements set forth by the Texas OAG and House Bill 390.
- Maintain accurate records of training completion for all affected employees.

### Likely Review Risks/Gaps
1. **Training Content**: Verify that the chosen training materials cover all required topics comprehensively.
2. **Documentation**: Ensure that records of completed training are kept accurately and securely.
3. **Signage Compliance**: Confirm that anti-trafficking signage is posted in all relevant areas of the establishment.
4. **Employee Awareness**: Conduct a review to ensure that all employees have effectively absorbed the training content.
