# Harbor Alliance Action Items

## Texas

**Next Steps:**
- Coordinate with local lodging associations to schedule annual human trafficking training for employees.

**States Allowing Immediate Market Entry:**
- Texas (employees must complete training annually)

**States Requiring Approval, Outreach, or Special Handling:**
- None identified

---

## California

**Next Steps:**
- Develop a communication plan to ensure all hotel and motel managers are aware of the training requirement and schedule it as soon as possible.

**States Allowing Immediate Market Entry:**
- California (employees must complete at least 20 minutes of training on or before January 1, 2020)

**States Requiring Approval, Outreach, or Special Handling:**
- None identified

---

## Illinois

**Next Steps:**
- Collaborate with the Department of Human Services to develop a communication strategy for lodging establishments to ensure they receive and comply with the human trafficking training requirement.

**States Allowing Immediate Market Entry:**
- Illinois (employees must complete training within six months of starting employment in a relevant role and every two years thereafter)

**States Requiring Approval, Outreach, or Special Handling:**
- None identified

---

## Minnesota

**Next Steps:**
- Engage with hotel and motel managers to schedule the annual human trafficking recognition training for their employees.

**States Allowing Immediate Market Entry:**
- Minnesota (employees must complete training within 90 days of hire and annually thereafter)

**States Requiring Approval, Outreach, or Special Handling:**
- None identified

---

## Connecticut

- Connecticut (employees must complete specific trainings, including Introduction to Child Trafficking in Connecticut and Introduction to Human Trafficking in Connecticut for Law Enforcement)

- None identified
