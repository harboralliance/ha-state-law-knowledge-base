# Harbor Alliance Action Items

## Texas
**Next Steps:** Ensure compliance with House Bill 390 by providing annual human trafficking training for all employees in commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, and to employees at all lodging establishments regardless of size. Use approved training videos from the Office of the Attorney General.
**States allowing immediate market entry:** None
**States requiring approval, outreach, or special handling:** Texas requires compliance with HB 390.

## California
**Next Steps:** Conduct internal review of human trafficking policies and ensure all employees are aware of the requirement for annual training. Provide necessary resources to complete the mandated training.
**States allowing immediate market entry:** None
**States requiring approval, outreach, or special handling:** California requires compliance with Senate Bill 970.

## Illinois
**Next Steps:** Develop a plan to provide annual human trafficking recognition training within six months of employment for all relevant staff and every two years thereafter. Use the model curriculum provided by the Department of Human Services.
**States allowing immediate market entry:** None
**States requiring approval, outreach, or special handling:** Illinois requires compliance with Act 2026.

## Minnesota
**Next Steps:** Ensure all hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff complete the required human trafficking prevention training within 90 days of hire and annually thereafter. Display the required poster in a visible location.
**States allowing immediate market entry:** None
**States requiring approval, outreach, or special handling:** Minnesota requires compliance with state law.

## Connecticut
**Next Steps:** Conduct internal review of current policies to identify areas for improvement and ensure all employees are aware of the requirement for annual human trafficking training. Provide necessary resources to complete the mandated training.
**States allowing immediate market entry:** None
**States requiring approval, outreach, or special handling:** Connecticut requires compliance with various state laws.
