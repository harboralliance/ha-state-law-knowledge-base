# Harbor Alliance Action Items

## Texas
**Next Steps:**
- Ensure all commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms complete the annual human trafficking training requirement.
- Promote and make available online/offline anti-trafficking training videos approved by the Office of the Attorney General.

**Immediate Market Entry:**
- No specific state-level requirements that require immediate market entry for Harbor Alliance in Texas.

## California
**Next Steps:**
- Provide mandatory human trafficking awareness training to hotel and motel employees as per Senate Bill 970.
- Encourage outreach to local hotels and motels regarding training availability through resources like the California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST).

**Immediate Market Entry:**
- No specific state-level requirements that require immediate market entry for Harbor Alliance in California.

## Illinois
**Next Steps:**
- Provide annual human trafficking recognition training to employees in lodging establishments, restaurants, and truck stops.
- Ensure compliance with the model curriculum developed by the Department of Human Services.

**Immediate Market Entry:**
- No specific state-level requirements that require immediate market entry for Harbor Alliance in Illinois.

## Minnesota
**Next Steps:**
- Provide mandatory human trafficking prevention training to hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff as per Minnesota Law.
- Ensure all required staff complete the training every year, including those who were initially trained before June 1, 2020.

**Immediate Market Entry:**
- No specific state-level requirements that require immediate market entry for Harbor Alliance in Minnesota.

## Connecticut
**Next Steps:**
- Provide annual human trafficking awareness training to all hotel and motel employees as mandated by Public Act 17-32.
- Promote the use of online/offline anti-trafficking training resources offered by the Department of Children and Families (DCF) through local hotels and motels.

**Immediate Market Entry:**
- No specific state-level requirements that require immediate market entry for Harbor Alliance in Connecticut.
