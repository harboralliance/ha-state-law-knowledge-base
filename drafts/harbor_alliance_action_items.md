# Harbor Alliance Action Items

## Texas
**Immediate Market Entry:** YES  
**Next Steps:**
- Ensure all commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms comply with the annual human trafficking training requirement by providing access to approved training videos and ensuring completion of the training.
- Develop a plan for outreach to smaller hotels and motels regarding the importance of human trafficking training.
- Monitor compliance through periodic audits.

## California
**Immediate Market Entry:** YES  
**Next Steps:**
- Notify hotel and motel managers about the upcoming human trafficking training requirement by January 1, 2020.
- Provide resources such as those offered by the California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST) and the National Human Trafficking Resource Center.
- Schedule training sessions for key employees who interact with guests.

## Illinois
**Immediate Market Entry:** YES  
**Next Steps:**
- Develop a communication plan to ensure all lodging establishment, restaurant, and truck stop owners receive the new human trafficking recognition training requirement by June 1, 2020.
- Provide access to the model curriculum developed by the Department of Human Services and facilitate its implementation.
- Conduct follow-up audits to ensure compliance.

## Minnesota
**Immediate Market Entry:** YES  
**Next Steps:**
- Distribute the mandatory human trafficking prevention and response training package to hotel, motel, and short-term rental owners within 90 days of their hire or renewal of license.
- Schedule annual refresher training for all required staff as per the new law.
- Monitor compliance through periodic audits.

## Connecticut
**Immediate Market Entry:** YES  
**Next Steps:**
- Provide all hotel and motel employees with the necessary human trafficking recognition training by the effective date.
- Develop an ongoing awareness campaign to reinforce the importance of these measures.
- Establish a system for mandatory data reporting of human trafficking cases to ensure compliance.
