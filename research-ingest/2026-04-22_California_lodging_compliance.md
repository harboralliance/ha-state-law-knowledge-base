### Analysis and Summary

#### 1. **Harmonization and Clarification:**
   - The three sources provide comprehensive information on human trafficking awareness training for employees in motels and hotels, specifically mandated by California's SB 970 (passed in 2018).
   - SOURCE 1: This summary is derived from the text provided by SOURCE 3.

#### 2. **SB 970 Requirements:**
   - **Effective Date:** January 1, 2020.
   - **Training Content:** At least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training and education regarding human trafficking awareness.
   - **Target Employees:** Employees who are likely to interact or come into contact with victims of human trafficking.

#### 3. **Exceptions:**
   - **Existing Training:** Employers who provided this training on or before January 1, 2019, do not need to provide additional training unless they have new hires falling under the same category as those initially trained.

#### 4. **Additional Information:**
   - **National Hotline:** Employees and potential victims can call 1-888-373-7888 or text "Be Free" (233733) for assistance.
   - **Local Resources:** For additional support, one may contact the California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking at 1-888-KEY-2FRE(EDOM) or 1-888-539-2373.

#### 5. **Compliance:**
   - Businesses, including motels and hotels, must post a model notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking about available resources, including hotline numbers.
   - The mandated notice is available for download in multiple languages (English, Spanish, additional languages as needed).

### Conclusion:
The California state mandate, enforced by SB 970, requires hotel and motel employees who interact with potential victims of human trafficking to receive at least 20 minutes of training on the subject. This training is mandatory from January 1, 2020, and applies to new hires as well. Employers are also required to post a model notice informing about available resources for assistance in multiple languages.
