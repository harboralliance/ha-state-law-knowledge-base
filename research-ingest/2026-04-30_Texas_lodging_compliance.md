The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for the hospitality industry. The purpose of this law is to provide additional opportunities for human trafficking to be reported to law enforcement by requiring human trafficking training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments.

The approved training videos include:

- "Human Trafficking Hotel Training" (Office of the Attorney General)
- "Human Trafficking - Texas" (Etactics)
- "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry" (Unbound Now)
- "Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training" (Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking)
- "Human Trafficking Training" (Extended Stay America)
- "Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry" (Marriott International)
- "Your Role in Preventing Human Trafficking: Recognize the Signs" (ECPAT-USA)
- "Survivor Services/HT101" (Refugee Services of Texas)
- "Human Trafficking Essentials: Texas Hospitality Awareness HB 390" (PBJ Learning)
- "Combating Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry" (Compliance Learning Solutions)

These training videos are available in English and Spanish, with some courses costing money while others are free. The delivery method for most courses is online, with a few options for in-person learning.

To request more information on specific courses or to contact organizations directly for course-specific questions, you can visit the Texas Attorney General's website or reach out to the email address provided in the article.
