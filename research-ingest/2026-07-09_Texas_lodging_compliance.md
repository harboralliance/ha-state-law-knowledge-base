The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has approved several human trafficking training videos for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. These videos are intended to provide additional opportunities for human trafficking to be reported to law enforcement and encourage all lodging establishments in the State of Texas regardless of size to complete the training.
The training requirement only applies to commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, but it is encouraged for smaller establishments to also complete the training. The approved training videos cover a range of topics, including identifying human trafficking signs, reporting suspected acts, and responding to victims.
Some of the approved training videos are available online, while others can be completed in-person. Some organizations offering the training include the Office of the Attorney General, Harbor Alliance Inc., Unbound Now, Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking, Extended Stay America, Marriott International, PACT (previously ECPAT-USA), Refugee Services of Texas, and Compliance Learning Solutions.
All employees must complete human trafficking training annually. The approved training provider must provide a certificate of completion to every employee who completes the training, and this certificate must include the date the course was taken and the title of the course. Operators of commercial lodging establishments are required to maintain sufficient records to show their compliance with Section 114.0051 of the Texas Business and Commerce Code and make these records available for review and inspection at the request of the OAG, as well as local, state, and federal law enforcement agencies.
