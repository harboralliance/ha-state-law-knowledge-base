### Overview of Human Trafficking Training Requirements and Approvals for Commercial Lodging in Texas

The Texas Legislature has mandated that commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms provide annual human trafficking training to their employees. This requirement aims to increase awareness about human trafficking and empower staff to recognize, respond to, and report potential incidents effectively.

### Key Points:
1. **Training Requirement**: Commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms must ensure that all new employees receive this training within 90 days of hiring and that all employees complete the training annually.
2. **Purpose**: The training is designed to help staff identify human trafficking, understand its prevalence in the hospitality industry, and know how to report suspected incidents.
3. **Approval Process**: Training materials must be approved by the Texas Attorney General's Office (OAG) before they can be used.

### Approved Training Materials:
The OAG provides a list of approved training videos that meet the requirements. These include:

1. **Human Trafficking Hotel Training** - Office of the Attorney General (Free, Online)
2. **Human Trafficking – Texas** - Etactics (Paid, Monthly Subscription, Online)
3. **Texas Commercial Lodging Human Trafficking Awareness and Response** - National Course Portal ($4.99 per individual learner, $0.99 per employee for bulk seats)

### Other Training Options:
There are several other organizations offering human trafficking training that can be used in compliance with the law:

- **Harbor Alliance Inc.**: Harbor Alliance Human Trafficking Prevention Training (Free, Online)
- **Unbound Now**: Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry (Free, Online and In-person)
