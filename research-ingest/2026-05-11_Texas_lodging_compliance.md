### Summary of Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging in Texas

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390 (87th Regular Session), which mandates annual human trafficking training for employees at commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms. This requirement is intended to enhance reporting and response mechanisms to human trafficking incidents within the hospitality industry.

#### Key Points of Training:
- **Training Content:**
  - Understanding how and why human trafficking occurs in the hospitality industry.
  - Identifying risks associated with human trafficking, such as physical injuries, inappropriate clothing, or multiple phone/social media accounts.
  - Reporting procedures for suspected human trafficking incidents, including calling emergency services (911), contacting local law enforcement through iWatchTX, and reaching out to the National Human Trafficking Hotline.

- **Training Requirements:**
  - New employees must complete training within 90 days of hire.
  - All employees must receive annual training sessions.
  - Training providers must issue a certificate of completion for each employee who completes the course. This certificate should include the date of the course and the title of the course.
  - Operators of commercial lodging establishments must retain copies of all certificates for review by the Office of the Attorney General, local, state, and federal law enforcement.

#### Approved Training Resources:
The Office of the Attorney General has approved several training resources that meet the requirements set forth in House Bill 390. These include:

- **Online Platforms:**
  - "Human Trafficking Hotel Training" by the Office of the Attorney General (22 minutes, free)
  - "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry" by Etactics and Unbound Now (25 minutes each, free)
  
- **Subscriptions:**
  - "Human Trafficking – Texas" by Etactics ($1/user/month)
  
- **In-Person Training:**
  - “Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training” by Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking ($25.95)
  - Various in-person training sessions offered by organizations like Marriott International, ECPAT-USA, Refugee Services of Texas, PBJ Learning, and Compliance Learning Solutions.

#### Reporting Mechanisms:
To facilitate reporting human trafficking incidents effectively, the training includes contact information for:
- National Human Trafficking Hotline (888-373-7888 or text 233733)
- iWatchTX (iWatchTX.org or 844-643-2251)

#### Non-Discrimination Policy:
Employees are protected from retaliation if they report suspected human trafficking incidents in good faith.

### Implementation and Compliance:
Commercial lodging establishments must ensure that all mandatory training requirements are met to comply with Texas law. This includes maintaining records of training completion and making these records available for inspection by relevant authorities upon request.

### Conclusion
The implementation of the Human Trafficking Training Requirement for commercial lodging establishments is a crucial step in combating human trafficking within the hospitality industry in Texas. Providing comprehensive and accessible training resources, along with established reporting mechanisms, helps ensure that employees are equipped to recognize and respond effectively to potential human trafficking incidents.
