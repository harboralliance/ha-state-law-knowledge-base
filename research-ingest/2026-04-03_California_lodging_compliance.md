### Overview and Analysis

The provided resources provide comprehensive information about human trafficking, its legal requirements, and the steps businesses must take to comply with them. Let's break down the key points from each source:

1. **Source 1: SB 970 (California Legislature)**
   - **Requirement**: Motel and hotel employers are required to provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness.
   - **Timeline**: The training must be completed by January 1, 2020, for employees who were employed as of July 1, 2019. New employees must also receive this training within six months of their employment.
   - **Exemption**: Employers who have already provided the required training on or before January 1, 2019, do not need to provide it again.

2. **Source 2: California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST)**
   - **Support Information**: CAST provides additional resources for those in immediate danger, including hotlines for assistance.
   - **Contact Information**: National Human Trafficking Resource Center (1-888-373-7888 or TEXT: Be Free or 233733), California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (1-888-KEY-2FRE(EDOM) or 1-888-539-2373).

3. **Source 3: Human Trafficking Model Notice**
   - **Purpose**: The model notice informs the public and victims of human trafficking about available telephone hotline numbers for help and reporting.
   - **Businesses Required to Post**: Hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns are required to post this notice.
   - **Languages**: Each business must post the notice in English and Spanish. For businesses located in specific counties, a third language posting may be required.

### Compliance Steps for Motel and Hotel Employers

1. **Training Requirement**:
   - Ensure all employees likely to interact with victims of human trafficking receive 20 minutes of training by January 1, 2020.
   - Provide the same training to new employees within six months of their employment.
   - Employees who received training on or before January 1, 2019, do not need additional training.

2. **Posting Requirement**:
   - Post a model notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking about available hotline numbers.
   - Ensure the notice is posted in English and Spanish.
   - For businesses located in specific counties with language assistance provisions under federal law, post the notice in an additional third language as required.

### Legal Framework

- **State Law**: California's civil rights laws mandate these requirements to protect victims of human trafficking and ensure awareness among employees who may interact with such individuals.
- **Penalties**: Failure to comply with posting and training requirements can result in fines and other penalties, as outlined by the California Attorney General's Office.

Motel and hotel employers are legally required to provide training on human trafficking awareness and post a model notice about available hotline numbers. By following the guidelines provided by SB 970, CAST, and other resources, businesses can comply with these legal requirements effectively.
