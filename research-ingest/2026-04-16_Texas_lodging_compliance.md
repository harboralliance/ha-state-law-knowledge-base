### Overview of Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging Establishments in Texas

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390 (87th Regular Session) to mandate human trafficking training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. This requirement applies specifically to establishments with 10 or more rooms but is encouraged for all lodging establishments across the state. The goal of these trainings is to provide employees with the knowledge and skills necessary to recognize signs of human trafficking, report incidents, and assist victims.

### Key Requirements

1. **Annual Training Requirement**: Employees must complete annual training.
2. **Training Content**: Training must cover identifying individuals at risk for human trafficking, reporting procedures, and relevant legislation.
3. **Non-Discrimination Clause**: Employees are protected from retaliation or discrimination for good faith reports of suspected human trafficking.
4. **Signage**: Commercial lodging establishments must display required human trafficking signage.

The Texas law requires annual human trafficking training for employees in commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, aiming to enhance their ability to recognize and respond to human trafficking incidents.
