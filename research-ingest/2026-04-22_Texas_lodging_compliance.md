This information is about training resources for human trafficking prevention in commercial lodging establishments in Texas. Specifically, it includes:

1. Legislation: 
House Bill 390 (87th Regular Session) passed by the Texas Legislature requires annual human trafficking training for employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms. However, it encourages all lodging establishments to provide this training regardless of size.

2. Training Requirements:
- Must be approved by the Office of the Attorney General (OAG)
- New employees must complete within 90 days of hire
- All employees must receive annual training
- Training provider must issue a certificate of completion for each employee

3. Training Content: 
Includes information on identifying human trafficking, reporting it, and required signage.

4. Training Delivery:
- Online courses available from various organizations
- Some are free, others have monthly subscription fees or one-time payments

5. Approved Training Providers:
A list of approved training videos is provided, including titles, lengths, languages, costs, and delivery methods. Examples include:
- Office of the Attorney General's "Human Trafficking Hotel Training"
- Etactics' "Human Trafficking - Texas"
- Unbound Now's "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry"

The goal is to provide businesses with resources to prevent human trafficking in their operations, thereby helping protect vulnerable individuals and comply with state law.
