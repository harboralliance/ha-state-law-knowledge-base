This Illinois law requires employers in lodging establishments, restaurants, and truck stops to provide human trafficking recognition training to their employees. The training must include information on identifying individuals at risk for human trafficking and reporting instances of human trafficking to the appropriate authorities. Employers may use their own training program or that developed by the Department of Human Services, which will be made available no later than July 1, 2020. Employers are required to provide this training within six months of beginning employment in such a role and every two years thereafter if still employed. The law also establishes penalties for violations of the Act, including a civil penalty of up to $1,500 for each offense.
