## Summary

The model notice for human trafficking is a document that businesses and establishments must post to inform the public and victims of human trafficking about the available telephone hotline numbers to seek help or report unlawful activity. This requirement applies to specific businesses, including hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns, as of April 1, 2013.

### Requirements
- **Posting:** The notice must be posted by April 1, 2013.
- **Languages:** The notice must be available in English and Spanish. Additional languages are required for businesses located in specific counties covered by the federal Voting Rights Act language assistance provisions.
- **Content:** The notice should include information on where to find help or report unlawful activity.

### Legal Implications
Failure to post the required notice can result in penalties.
