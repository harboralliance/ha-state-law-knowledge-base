## Summary
Connecticut has enacted several laws aimed at combating human trafficking, including sex trafficking. The state provides various training programs for different sectors such as DCF staff, foster parents, service providers, schools, law enforcement, and medical professionals. These trainings are designed to help identify victims, understand the laws, and respond appropriately.

## Extracted Requirements
- **Applicable industry:** DCF staff, foster parents, service providers, schools, law enforcement, hospital and medical staff, hospitality industry, other professionals, youth, and community.
- **Training required:** Yes, mandatory for Child Protective Services staff. Recommended for other groups.
  - Introduction to Child Trafficking in Connecticut
  - Introduction to Child Trafficking in Connecticut for School Settings
  - Introduction to Human Trafficking in Connecticut for Law Enforcement - Police Officer's Standards and Training (POST) Certified
  - Introduction to Human Trafficking in Connecticut for Emergency Medical Services (EMS)
  - Child Trafficking Training for Medical Providers/Emergency Medical Services
  - MyLifeMyChoice curriculum for youth.
- **Signage/Posters:** No specific requirement mentioned in the provided sources.
- **Posting of Laws and Regulations:** Hotels, motels, inns, and similar lodging must provide trafficking training and awareness campaigns to all employees annually.
- **Data Reporting:** Mandatory data reporting required.

## Enforcement
- Hotels, motels, inns, and similar lodging must keep guest transactions and receipts for at least six months.
- State attorneys and local law enforcement must document training, investigations of missing children, trafficking referrals, trafficking cases referred for prosecution, etc.

## Legislation
Connecticut has enacted several laws to address human trafficking:
1. **2017 Public Act 17-32:** An Act Concerning Human Trafficking.
2. **2016 Public Act 16-71:** An Act Concerning Human Trafficking.
3. **2015 Public Act 15-195:** An Act Strengthening Protections for Victims of Human Trafficking.
4. **2014 Public Act 14-186:** An Act Concerning the Department of Children and Families and the Protection of Children.
5. **2013 Public Act 13-166:** An Act Concerning Sexual Exploitation and Trafficking in Persons.
6. **2012 Public Act 12-141:** An Act Concerning Commercial Sexual Exploitation of a Minor.
7. **2011 Public Act 11-180:** An Act Concerning Notification by the Department of Children and Families When a Youth is Arrested for Prostitution.
8. **2010 Public Act 10-115:** An Act Providing a Safe Harbor for Exploited Children.

These laws cover various aspects of human trafficking, including expanding the crime conditions, providing services to victims, and strengthening protections for minors.
