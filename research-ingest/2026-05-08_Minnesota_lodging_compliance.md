The training materials for preventing sex trafficking in lodging establishments are required by law in Minnesota. The training covers identifying signs of sex trafficking, responding to potential situations, and reporting any incidents. 

The training requirements include:

1. **Completion by All Staff**: Owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the training.
2. **New Employees**: New hires must take the training within 90 days of their start date.
3. **Annual Retraining**: All staff must retake the training annually.
4. **Displaying a Poster**: Managers must display the required back-of-house poster in a location where all staff can see it.

The training materials are provided by the Minnesota Department of Health and cover various aspects including:
- A virtual presentation with guided exercises
- A Training Companion Guide 
- A Manager & Owner Training Toolkit
- A set of posters for different areas within the establishment

Training is available in multiple languages including English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.

Exemptions include:
- Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms.
- Employees working exclusively in restaurants providing catering services with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

The training package is required for all hotels, motels, and short-term rentals licensed by the Minnesota Department of Health. Exceptions can be made based on specific circumstances documented within the Manager & Owner Training Toolkit.

For more details and to access the training, individuals should visit the Minnesota Department of Health's Learning Center or contact health.safeharbor@state.mn.us for assistance.
