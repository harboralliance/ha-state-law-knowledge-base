### Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training

#### Overview
Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are significant locations for sex trafficking. This training equips hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff with the knowledge to identify signs of trafficking, respond effectively, and prevent such incidents within their establishments.

### Who Needs the Training?
- **Owners, Managers, and On-Site Employees:** All must complete the required training.
- **New Employees:** Must take the training within 90 days of hire.
- **Existing Staff:** Must retake the training annually.

#### Required Materials
The training package includes:
- A virtual presentation with guided exercises.
- The Training Companion Guide (PDF) as a resource for viewing the virtual presentation.
- Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF) to assist in compliance with the law.
- Poster set with required back of house, housekeeping, restaurant/bar, front desk/lobby, and security posters.

### Accessing the Training
#### Managers and Owners
1. **Pre-training Survey:** Complete a brief survey before accessing the training package.
2. **Training Package:** Access through the MDH Learning Center.

#### Employees
- **English:** Employee Training
- **Spanish/Español:** Employee Training
- **Somali:** Employee Training
- **Hmong:** Employee Training

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
1. **Have I Completed Training?**
   - Yes, you still need to complete the Minnesota-specific training.

2. **Do Resorts and Bed & Breakfasts Need Training?**
   - Only if they hold a hotel license.

3. **Does the Law Apply to Tribal Nations?**
   - No; however, MDH encourages participation.

4. **Are Any Employees Exempt from the Law?**
   - Yes, exempt employees include those under 16 (cleaning rooms), working exclusively in restaurants (catering services), or not having direct contact with guests.

5. **How Can I Verify My License?**
   - Contact MDH at `health.foodlodging@state.mn.us` or call 651-201-4500.

6. **Does MDH Charge for Training?**
   - No, the training package is free.

7. **Is Assistance Provided with Compliance Costs?**
   - MDH does not provide financial assistance except for training materials.

8. **Training in Other Languages:**
   - Additional languages will be available starting late fall 2023/early 2024. Contact MDH at `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us` for other language needs.

9. **Is Annual Retraining Required?**
   - Yes, it is mandatory annually tied to licensure.

10. **Can We Replace the MDH Training with Our Own?**
    - No, only specific parts may be replaced after MDH approval.

11. **Do Owners/Operators/Managers Need Training if They Don't Contact Guests?**
    - Yes, they are still required under the law.

12. **Is There an Exception for Seasonal Employees?**
    - The law does not explicitly exempt them; they must complete training within 90 days of starting work.

13. **Do Temporary Employees Need Training?**
    - Technically no, but MDH encourages it.

14. **Are Contracted Workers Subject to Training?**
    - Yes, all employees must receive the training.

15. **Are Whole House Vacation Rentals Covered?**
    - Yes, they are licensed as hotels/motels and must comply with the statute.

16. **What Happens After Training Completion?**
    - Fill out a roster of completed trainees and keep it on file for inspections.
