# State Research Capture

## State
Connecticut

## Summary
Connecticut has comprehensive legislation and training programs to combat human trafficking, particularly for sex and labor. Training is required for various professionals including DCF staff, foster parents, service providers, schools, law enforcement, hospital and medical staff, hospitality industry personnel, and others. Legislation includes acts that protect victims, require training for hotels and motels, and establish mandatory data reporting.

## Extracted Requirements
- Applicable industry: DCF staff, foster parents, service providers, schools, law enforcement, hospital and medical staff, hospitality industry personnel, youth, and the community.
- Training required: Introduction to Child Trafficking in Connecticut, Introduction to Child Trafficking in Connecticut for School Settings, Introduction to Human Trafficking in Connecticut for Law Enforcement - Police Officer's Standards and Training (POST) Certified, Introduction to Human Trafficking in Connecticut for Emergency Medical Services (EMS), Child Trafficking Training for Medical Providers/Emergency Medicine, Introduction to Human Trafficking in Connecticut for Hotels, Motels, and Similar Lodging.
- Signage or notice requirements: Not explicitly mentioned.
- Reporting requirements: Mandatory data reporting by various entities.
- Data retention requirements: Guest transactions and receipts must be kept for at least 6 months.
- Affirmative defense for minors: Specific to commercial sexual exploitation of a minor.

## Enforcement and Penalties
- Laws related to human trafficking are enforced through the state's criminal justice system, with public acts defining specific penalties for crimes such as prostitution and commercial sexual exploitation.
- Victim services and protections are provided under certain circumstances, including expanded protections for victims and wiretapping authorizations.

## Monitoring and Reporting
- Ongoing monitoring of efforts to combat trafficking is mandated.
- Mandatory data reporting ensures tracking and analysis of trafficking cases.

## Conclusion
Connecticut's approach to combating human trafficking is multifaceted, involving extensive legislation and comprehensive training programs across various sectors. This ensures a coordinated response from government agencies and professionals to prevent, identify, and address human trafficking incidents.
