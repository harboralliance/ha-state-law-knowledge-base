### Summary

**Training Requirement for Hotel and Motel Employees:**
Yes, according to California law (SB 970), hotel and motel employers must provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training and education regarding human trafficking awareness to each employee who is likely to interact or come into contact with victims of human trafficking. This training must be provided by January 1, 2020, for employees hired on or after July 1, 2019.

**Training Content:**
The training content should cover the basics of human trafficking, including identifying potential signs and reporting suspected cases to the appropriate authorities (such as the National Human Trafficking Resource Center at 1-888-373-7888).

### Compliance Steps

1. **Review Existing Training Programs:** Assess if your current employee training programs include content on human trafficking.
2. **Develop or Revise Training Materials:** If not, create new materials that cover the essential aspects of human trafficking awareness and reporting procedures.
3. **Schedule Training Sessions:** Ensure all employees who are likely to interact with guests (such as front desk staff, housekeeping, maintenance personnel) receive the required training by January 1, 2020.
4. **Document Training Completion:** Keep records of completed training sessions for each employee to ensure compliance and future reference.

### Key Resources

- **SB 970 (Effective January 1, 2020):** This law specifies the requirements for human trafficking awareness training in hotels and motels.
- **National Human Trafficking Resource Center:** Offers resources, including a hotline at 1-888-373-7888.
- **California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST):** Provides additional support and information on human trafficking issues.

### Reporting Requirements

If an employee suspects human trafficking activities, they are required to report these incidents immediately. Employees should be aware of the designated reporting procedures and have access to a hotline for confidential assistance.

### Conclusion

Compliance with SB 970 is crucial for hotel and motel employers in California. Providing comprehensive training on human trafficking awareness not only protects employees but also upholds legal requirements and helps prevent potential exploitation of guests.
