### Overview of Approved Human Trafficking Training Videos

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for commercial lodging establishments. This bill aims to increase awareness and reporting of human trafficking within the hospitality industry. The OAG maintains a list of approved training videos that meet the requirements set by the law.

#### Key Points:
- **Purpose**: To provide employees of commercial lodging establishments with necessary training on identifying, responding to, and preventing human trafficking.
- **Requirements**: Training must be completed by new employees within 90 days after hire and annually thereafter. Employees may not be disciplined for making good faith reports of suspected human trafficking acts.
