**Overview of Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals in Minnesota**

### Statutory Requirement
The Minnesota law requires all hotel, motel, and short-term rental owners, managers, and on-site employees to complete a specific training program aimed at preventing and responding to sex trafficking. This training is mandated by Minnesota Statutes section 173F.56.

### Training Requirements
- **New Employees:** Must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
- **All Required Staff:** Must retake the training every year.
- **Display of Poster:** All hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are required to display a back-of-house poster.
- **Awareness Campaign:** Managers and owners must conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for their employees.

### Training Components
The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:
1. **Virtual Presentation**: A video with guided exercises.
2. **Training Companion Guide (PDF)**: A resource for viewing the virtual presentation.
3. **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)**: Instructions to comply with the law.
4. **Poster Set**:
   - Back of House (required to display)
   - Housekeeping
   - Restaurant/Bar
   - Front desk/lobby
   - Security

### Access and Support
- **Training Access**: Available through the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) Learning Center. Managers and owners will be asked to complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package.
- **Language Availability**: The training materials are available in English, Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

### Frequently Asked Questions
1. **Exemptions**: Exempt employees include those under 16 (unless cleaning rooms), those working exclusively in restaurants providing catering services, or those with no direct contact with guests.
2. **Seasonal Employees**: While the law does not explicitly exempt seasonal employees, they must still receive training within 90 days of starting work.
3. **Training for All Employees**: MDH encourages training for all employees, regardless of their exemptions.

### Compliance and Costs
- **Costs**: Licensees are required to pay the costs of compliance with the law. MDH does not provide assistance such as printing posters.
- **Training Materials**: The training materials will be offered in additional languages (Spanish, Hmong, Somali) starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

### Next Steps After Training
After completing the training, owners, managers, and on-site employees should fill out a roster of names for all employees who completed the training along with the date of completion. This roster should be kept on file for compliance purposes.
