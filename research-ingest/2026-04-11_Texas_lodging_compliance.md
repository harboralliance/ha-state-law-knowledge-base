### Overview of Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging in Texas

The Texas Legislature has enacted House Bill 390, which mandates annual human trafficking training for employees at commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms. This requirement is aimed at enhancing awareness and providing guidance on identifying, reporting, and responding to human trafficking incidents within the hospitality industry.

### Training Requirements

1. **Frequency**: Annual training is required.
2. **Target Audience**: Employees directly employed by commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms.
3. **Content**: The training must cover:
   - Identifying red flags of human trafficking
   - Reporting procedures for suspected human trafficking incidents
   - Understanding the importance of confidentiality and non-disciplinary actions against employees who report trafficking

### Training Providers

The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) has approved several training providers that meet the state's requirements. These providers offer various formats, including online courses, in-person sessions, and webinars.

#### Online Courses
- **"Human Trafficking Hotel Training" by OAG**: Free 22-minute course available online.
- **"Human Trafficking – Texas" by Etactics**: $1/user/month online course.
- **"Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry" by Marriott International**: Available to Marriott associates, contact for more information.

#### In-Person Training
- **"Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry"**:
  - English version: Free online and in-person; contact for more details.
  - Spanish version: Free online and in-person; contact for more details.

### Additional Resources

1. **National Human Trafficking Hotline**: Available at (888) 373-7888 or text 233733.
2. **iWatchTX**: For reporting suspicious activity, accessible at iWatchTX.org or by calling 844-643-2251.

### Compliance and Reporting

1. **Certificate Requirement**: Approved training providers must provide a certificate of completion to each employee who completes the training.
2. **Record Keeping**: Operators of commercial lodging establishments must retain copies of completed certificates for at least three years and make them available for review by OAG, local, state, and federal law enforcement.

### Conclusion

Human trafficking is a significant issue in the hospitality industry, and the Texas Legislature's requirement for annual training aims to address this problem effectively. By providing comprehensive guidance and support, these training programs help employees recognize signs of trafficking, report incidents responsibly, and contribute to the safety and well-being of potential victims.
