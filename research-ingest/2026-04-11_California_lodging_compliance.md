### Analysis and Recommendations

#### 1. **Understanding the Requirement**
   - The requirement is for hotel and motel employers to provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness.
   - This training must be provided to employees who are likely to interact with victims of human trafficking, either as of July 1, 2019, or within six months of their employment in that role.

#### 2. **Compliance and Implementation**
   - Hotels and motels need to ensure they have a program in place for providing this training.
   - The training should be designed to educate employees about human trafficking, including its signs, how to identify potential victims, and what actions to take if a victim is encountered.

#### 3. **Reporting Requirements**
   - Hotels and motels are required to keep records of the training provided, including details such as the date of the training, number of participants, and any assessments taken.
   - These records should be maintained for at least five years in accordance with California's business recordkeeping laws.

#### 4. **Penalties for Non-Compliance**
   - Failure to provide the required training or to maintain accurate records can result in fines of up to $10,000 per violation.
   - Violations may also result in other penalties, including license suspension or revocation.

#### 5. **Supporting Information and Resources**
   - Hotels and motels should seek additional resources, such as model notices, training materials, and guidance from the Department of Justice to ensure compliance.
   - The Office of the Attorney General provides a Human Trafficking Model Notice that can be downloaded for posting in multiple languages.

#### 6. **Training Materials**
   - To facilitate compliance, hotels and motels should look for certified training programs offered by reputable organizations or through online courses.
   - It is advisable to verify the credentials and effectiveness of any training provider before enrolling employees.

#### 7. **Monitoring and Compliance Audits**
   - Hotels and motels should conduct periodic audits to ensure that all required training has been provided and maintained accurately.
   - Regularly reviewing employee records and ensuring compliance with the law can help prevent potential fines and legal issues.

### Conclusion

Compliance with the human trafficking awareness training requirement for hotel and motel employees is crucial for maintaining a safe and compliant business environment. By providing effective training, hotels and motels can help protect their employees and the public from the dangers of human trafficking. Additionally, keeping accurate records and regularly auditing compliance efforts will help ensure long-term adherence to the law.

### Recommendations

1. **Implement a Comprehensive Training Program**: Enroll all relevant employees in certified training programs.
2. **Maintain Detailed Records**: Keep comprehensive records of all training sessions and employee assessments for at least five years.
3. **Regular Audits**: Conduct periodic audits to ensure compliance with the training requirements.
4. **Seek Additional Resources**: Utilize model notices, training materials, and guidance from the Department of Justice and other reputable sources.
5. **Stay Informed**: Stay updated on any changes in law or regulations related to human trafficking awareness training.

By following these recommendations, hotels and motels can ensure they are fully compliant with the human trafficking awareness training requirement and contribute to a safer environment for all.
