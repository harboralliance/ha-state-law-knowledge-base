## Summary
Connecticut offers various trainings for providers, foster parents, and communities to combat human trafficking. These include specialized courses on child trafficking for different settings such as schools, law enforcement, emergency medical services, hotels, and lodging businesses. The state also has enacted several public acts related to human trafficking, including laws that strengthen protections for victims and expand the crime of human trafficking under certain circumstances.
