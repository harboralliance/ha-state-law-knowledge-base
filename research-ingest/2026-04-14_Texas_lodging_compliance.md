### Overview of Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging in Texas

#### **1. Legal Requirement:**
- **House Bill 390 (87th Regular Session):** The Texas Legislature passed this bill, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. This training is mandatory for facilities with 10 or more rooms but is encouraged for all lodging establishments.
- **Scope:** The requirement applies only to large lodging establishments, but it encourages smaller ones to offer similar training.

#### **2. Training Content:**
- **Core Topics Covered:**
  - Understanding human trafficking and its impact on the hospitality industry.
  - Identifying potential signs of human trafficking among employees.
  - Reporting procedures for suspected cases.
  - Legal protections afforded to individuals making good faith reports of trafficking.
  - Roles and responsibilities in reporting and responding to human trafficking incidents.

#### **3. Training Providers:**
- **Certification by OAG:** All training must be approved by the OAG to meet the legal requirements.
- **Types of Training Providers:** Various organizations offer approved training programs, including government agencies, private companies, and non-profits specializing in human rights education.
- **Training Formats:** The training can be delivered online or in-person.

#### **4. Availability of Approved Trainings:**
- **Online Options:** Many trainings are available for free or at a low cost through platforms like Etactics, Unbound Now, and Compliance Learning Solutions. These platforms provide flexibility in scheduling and location.
- **In-Person Training:** Some providers offer in-person training options, either as stand-alone events or integrated into existing employee development programs.

#### **5. Costs:**
- **Free Trainings:** Many approved trainings are offered at no cost to ensure broad access for businesses and employees.
- **Paid Options:** Some providers charge a fee per user, such as Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training from Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking, which costs $25.95.

#### **6. Reporting Mechanisms:**
- **Contact Information:** Training materials must include contact details for reporting human trafficking incidents:
  - National Human Trafficking Hotline (toll-free)
  - Appropriate local law enforcement agencies
  - Attorney general’s designated hotline

#### **7. Compliance Requirements:**
- **Training Frequency:** Employees in commercial lodging establishments are required to complete annual training.
- **Certificate of Completion:** A certificate must be provided to every employee who completes the training, including the date taken and course title.
- **Record Keeping:** Operators must retain records of completed trainings for each employee, available for review by OAG and other law enforcement agencies.

#### **8. Accessible Training:**
- **Language Availability:** Approved trainings are offered in multiple languages, including English and Spanish, to cater to diverse populations.

### Conclusion
The human trafficking training requirement for commercial lodging establishments in Texas is designed to enhance awareness and action against trafficking within the hospitality industry. By ensuring mandatory annual training and providing free or affordable options, the state aims to empower employees and protect vulnerable individuals from exploitation. Training providers are encouraged to adhere to strict guidelines set by the OAG to ensure that all approved courses meet these standards.
