Based on the information provided in the sources, here are key points about human trafficking training requirements and resources for commercial lodging establishments in Texas:

Key Requirements:
- The Texas Legislature passed HB 390 requiring annual human trafficking training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms. Training is encouraged for all lodging establishments regardless of size.
- New employees must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
- All employees must receive annual training.

Important Guidelines:
- Employees "may not be disciplined, retaliated against, or otherwise discriminated against for making a good faith report of a suspected act of human trafficking."
- Training must be approved by the Office of the Attorney General (OAG).
- Operators must retain copies of employee certificates and maintain records showing compliance.
- Training providers must provide a certificate of completion to each employee.

Approved Training Resources:
- The OAG has approved several training videos on their website, including:
  - "Human Trafficking Hotel Training" (22 min, free online)
  - "Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry" (60 or 80 min, free available to Marriott associates)

These resources aim to provide guidance for staff on identifying and responding to human trafficking incidents in commercial lodging establishments. They emphasize the importance of reporting suspected trafficking and maintaining a safe environment for employees and guests.

The training requirements are part of Texas's efforts to address human trafficking within its hospitality industry and provide workers with the knowledge and support they need to recognize and respond appropriately to potential trafficking situations.
