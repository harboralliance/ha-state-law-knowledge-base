## Summary
Connecticut has comprehensive legislation and training programs in place to address human trafficking, specifically focusing on child sex trafficking. Legislation includes public acts that expand protections for victims of human trafficking, mandate mandatory data reporting, and establish a statewide oversight body. Training programs are available for various groups including DCF staff, foster parents, service providers, schools, law enforcement, healthcare professionals, and the hospitality industry.

## Extracted Requirements
- Applicable industry: Hotels, motels, inns, and similar lodging; healthcare; hospitality industry; law enforcement; schools.
- Training required: Annual training for hotel employees; mandatory training for DCF staff, foster parents, service providers, schools, law enforcement, healthcare professionals.
- Signage required: No specific mention of signage requirements.
- Reporting required: Mandatory data reporting on trafficking cases.
- Enforcement agency: Department of Children and Families (DCF); state attorneys and local law enforcement.
- Penalties: Specific penalties outlined in various public acts, including class C felonies for commercial sexual exploitation of a minor and human trafficking.
- Effective date: Various legislation was enacted between 2010 and 2017.

## Relevant Legislation
- **Public Act 17-32**: An Act Concerning Human Trafficking (2017)
- **Public Act 16-71**: An Act Concerning Human Trafficking (2016)
- **Public Act 15-195**: An Act Strengthening Protections for Victims of Human Trafficking (2015)
- **Public Act 14-186**: An Act Concerning the Department of Children and Families and the Protection of Children (2014)
- **Public Act 13-166**: An Act Concerning Sexual Exploitation and Trafficking in Persons (2013)
- **Public Act 12-141**: An Act Concerning Commercial Sexual Exploitation of a Minor (2012)
- **Public Act 11-180**: An Act Concerning Notification by the Deparment of Children and Families When a Youth is Arrested for Prostitution and Out-of-State Placements of Children and Youth (2011)
- **Public Act 10-115**: An Act Providing a Safe Harbor for Exploited Children (2010)

## Training Programs
- **Hotel Employees**: Annual training on trafficking awareness and prevention.
- **DCF Staff**: Training on human trafficking of minors, multidisciplinary team formation.
- **Foster Parents**: Training on identifying and supporting children in trafficking situations.
- **Service Providers**: Training to better serve human trafficking victims.
- **Schools**: Awareness programs and possibly training for teachers and staff.

## Enforcement and Support
- **Department of Children and Families (DCF)**: Leads response efforts, provides services to victims.
- **State Attorneys and Local Law Enforcement**: Responsible for documenting training, investigations, and prosecution referrals.
- **Office of Victim Services**: May waive crime victim compensation limitations under certain circumstances.

This comprehensive approach underscores Connecticut's commitment to combating human trafficking.
