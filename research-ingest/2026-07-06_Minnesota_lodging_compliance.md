The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package is required for all hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in the state under Minnesota law. This training aims to help staff identify signs of sex trafficking and respond appropriately.

### Key Points:
1. **Training Requirements:**
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the training.
   - New employees must take it within 90 days of hire.
   - Required staff must retake it every year.
   - Display the required back-of-house poster in a location visible to all staff.

2. **Training Components:**
   - Virtual presentation (video with guided exercises).
   - Training Companion Guide.
   - Manager & Owner Training Toolkit.
   - Poster set (Back of House, Housekeeping, Restaurant/Bar, Front desk/lobby, Security).

3. **Access and Compliance:**
   - Managers/owners access the training through MDH Learning Center.
   - Employees can receive the training in English or other languages provided by request.

4. **Exceptions:**
   - Exemptions include employees under 16 who clean rooms, those working exclusively in restaurants, and no direct contact with guests.
   - Only facilities holding a hotel license are required to provide this training.

5. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):**
   - The training is mandatory for all lodging industry staff.
   - Resorts and bed and breakfasts may be exempt if they don't hold a hotel license.
   - MDH provides the training at no cost, and there is no assistance with printing posters.
   - Training materials will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

6. **Next Steps After Training:**
   - Employees should complete a roster of names with the date of completion and keep it on file for inspections.

### Contact Information:
For questions or assistance accessing the training, contact `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.

This training is crucial in preventing sex trafficking within the lodging industry and protecting vulnerable individuals.
