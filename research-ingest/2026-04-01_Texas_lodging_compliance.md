## Overview of Human Trafficking Training Requirements for Commercial Lodging in Texas

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390 (87th Regular Session) to mandate human trafficking training for employees directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. This law aims to enhance the ability of hospitality industry workers to recognize and report signs of human trafficking, thereby increasing the likelihood of such incidents being detected and addressed promptly.

### Key Points:
1. **Applicability**: The requirement applies only to commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms but is encouraged for all lodging establishments regardless of size.
2. **Frequency**: Employees must receive annual human trafficking training.
3. **Reporting**: Training must include guidance on how employees should report suspected human trafficking, including the need to contact local law enforcement and the National Human Trafficking Hotline.

### Steps for Compliance:
1. **Training Content**: The training must cover identifying potential victims of human trafficking, recognizing red flags, reporting procedures, and the role of hospitality industry workers in preventing and responding to such incidents.
2. **Certification**: Employees must complete a certified training program approved by the Office of the Attorney General (OAG).
3. **Record Keeping**: Employers are required to maintain records of completed training certifications for each employee.

### Approved Training Resources:
The OAG maintains a list of approved human trafficking training videos and programs. Some key points about these resources:

1. **Online Accessibility**: Many of the approved training courses are available online, making it convenient for employers and employees.
2. **Languages**: Many training materials are offered in multiple languages (English and Spanish) to accommodate diverse employee populations.
3. **Costs**: While some training programs may incur a cost, many are free or have affordable pricing structures.

### Example Training Programs:
- **Etactics "Human Trafficking - Texas"**: A 26-minute video that covers the basics of human trafficking in Texas.
- **Unbound Now "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry"**: Free online courses available in both English and Spanish.
- **Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training**: A paid program with a 45-minute duration.

### Reporting Procedures:
Training materials must include specific instructions on reporting human trafficking incidents, which typically involve contacting local law enforcement (dialing 911 in emergencies), the National Human Trafficking Hotline at 888-373-7888, or using iWatchTX if available. Employees should also be aware of the legal protections they have for making good faith reports.

### Record Keeping:
Employers must keep a copy of each employee's certification of completion, which may be in paper or electronic form. These records must be accessible to OAG and other relevant law enforcement agencies upon request.

By complying with these requirements, commercial lodging establishments can significantly contribute to preventing and addressing human trafficking within their facilities.
