### Analysis and Summary

**Introduction:**
The requirement for human trafficking awareness training in motels and hotels has been mandated by California state law, specifically through Senate Bill 970. This bill requires such training for hotel and motel employees who are likely to interact with victims of human trafficking.

#### **1. Training Requirement:**
- **Timing:** The training must be completed by January 1, 2020.
- **Duration:** At least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training.
- **Content:** Must cover human trafficking awareness and the resources available for victims.
- **Exemptions:** Employees who received training before July 1, 2019, are exempt from additional training.

#### **2. Signage Requirement:**
- **Posting:** Businesses (including hotels and motels) are required to post a notice informing the public of telephone hotline numbers for help or reporting unlawful activity related to human trafficking.
- **Languages:** The notice must be posted in English and Spanish. In specific counties, it may also need to be posted in a third language.

#### **3. Enforcement:**
- **Penalties:** Failure to comply with these requirements can result in fines.

### Conclusion
The requirement for human trafficking awareness training in hotels and motels is a critical step towards protecting victims and raising awareness about this form of exploitation. It ensures that employees are better equipped to identify and respond to potential cases, ultimately helping to reduce the incidence of human trafficking in the hospitality sector.
