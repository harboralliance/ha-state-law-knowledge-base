### Overview

The Minnesota law mandates that hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff undergo a specific training program to prevent and respond to sex trafficking. This requirement aims to equip hospitality workers with the necessary knowledge to identify potential incidents and take appropriate actions.

### Training Requirements

1. **Who Must Take the Training:**
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
   - Exceptions include employees under 16 unless they clean rooms, employees working exclusively in a restaurant or providing catering services, and those with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

2. **Timing for New Employees:**
   - New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
   
3. **Annual Retraining:**
   - All required staff must retake the training every year.

4. **Displaying the Training Poster:**
   - A specific back-of-house poster is required to be displayed in a location all staff will see it.

5. **Ongoing Awareness Campaign:**
   - Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals should conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for their employees.

### What is Included in the Training?

The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:

- A virtual presentation (video with guided exercises)
- A Training Companion Guide (PDF) as a resource for viewing the virtual presentation
- A Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF) to assist with compliance with the law
- A poster set:
  - Back of House (required to display)

### Accessing and Completing the Training

1. **Managers and Owners:**
   - They will be asked to complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package.
   - Access the training through the MDH Learning Center.

2. **Employees:**
   - Employee training is available in multiple languages, including English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. **Re-taking Training:**
   - Yes, you still need to complete the Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training even if you have taken similar training before.
   
2. **Applicability of Regulations to Tribes:**
   - The law does not apply to tribal nations, but MDH encourages them to provide the training and use the MDH materials.

3. **Exemptions for Employees:**
   - Exemptions include employees under 16 unless they clean rooms, employees working exclusively in a restaurant or providing catering services, and those with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms.
   
4. **Costs of Compliance:**
   - MDH does not provide financial assistance for printing posters; licensees are responsible for the costs.

5. **Training Materials in Other Languages:**
   - Training materials will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

6. **Annual Requirement:**
   - Yes, managers and owners must complete the training each year as it is tied to licensure.

7. **Replacement of MDH Training Materials:**
   - Replacement is not allowed; however, exceptions may be granted with prior review and approval from MDH.
