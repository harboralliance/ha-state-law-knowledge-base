### Summary

This response outlines the requirements for human trafficking awareness training for employees in motels and hotels as per various legal and administrative sources.

### Human Trafficking Awareness Training Requirement

1. **Purpose**: To ensure that employees who may interact with victims of human trafficking are informed and equipped to recognize signs of trafficking and respond appropriately.

2. **Legal Basis**: 
   - **Senate Bill 970 (SB 970)**: This law requires hotel and motel employers to provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness to employees who are likely to interact with victims of trafficking.
   - **Section 52.6 of the Civil Code**: This section mandates that specified businesses post a notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking about available telephone hotline numbers for help and reporting.

3. **Training Requirements**:
   - **Timing**: 
     - Employers must provide training to employees who are employed as of July 1, 2019, by January 1, 2020.
     - New employees likely to interact with victims of trafficking must receive training within six months of their employment in that role.

4. **Exceptions**: 
   - Employers who have provided this training and education to an employee on or before January 1, 2019, are not required to provide additional training.

### Posting Requirements

1. **Posting Notice**:
   - Businesses specified by law (including hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns) must post a notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking about available telephone hotline numbers.
   
2. **Languages**:
   - The notice must be posted in English and Spanish.
   - For businesses located in specific counties subject to federal Voting Rights Act language assistance provisions, a third language (such as Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, Cambodian, Chinese, etc.) must also be posted.

3. **Penalties for Non-Compliance**: Failure to post the notice or comply with language requirements can result in penalties.

### Model Notice

The California Attorney General's Office provides a model notice template that businesses are required to use and post according to specific guidelines. The notice includes information on contacting hotlines, seeking help, and reporting unlawful activities.

### Conclusion

Hotel and motel employers in California are required to provide at least 20 minutes of training on human trafficking awareness to employees who interact with victims. Additionally, they must post a specific notice informing the public and victims about available hotlines. Compliance with these requirements is crucial for ensuring employee knowledge and support in cases of human trafficking.
