**Human Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals**

The Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) requires hotels, motels, and short-term rentals to provide training on human trafficking prevention to ensure the safety and well-being of guests. This article outlines the key requirements, available resources, and FAQs related to this mandatory training.

### Key Requirements
1. **Training for All Employees**: All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
2. **New Employees**: New employees must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
3. **Annual Refresher Training**: All required staff must retake this training every year.
4. **Displaying a Poster**: The back-of-house poster must be displayed in a location where all staff will see it.
5. **Awareness Campaign**: Conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees.

### Training Package
The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:
- A virtual presentation with guided exercises
- Training Companion Guide (PDF)
- Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)
- Poster set (Back of House, Housekeeping, Restaurant/Bar, Front desk/lobby, Security)

### Access to Training
Managers and owners can access the training through the MDH Learning Center. Employees should complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. **Do I Still Need This Training If I Already Took One?**
   - Yes, you need to complete the Minnesota-specific lodging sex trafficking prevention and response training.

2. **Are Resorts, Bed and Breakfasts, Vacation Shares, Short-Term Rentals Required?**
   - Only if they hold a hotel license.

3. **Does the Law Apply to Tribal Nations?**
   - No, but MDH encourages tribal nations to provide the training and use MDH materials.

4. **Are Any Employees Exempt from the Training?**
   - Yes, employees under 16 unless they clean rooms, employees who work exclusively in a restaurant providing catering services, or those with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms are exempt. However, MDH encourages training for all employees.

5. **Can We Replace MDH Training with Our Own Materials?**
   - No. The entire package cannot be replaced; only specific parts can be substituted with prior approval from MDH.

6. **Does the Law Exempt Seasonal Employees?**
   - No, seasonal employees must receive training within 90 days of starting work.

7. **Do Contractors, Such as Cleaning Staff, Need Training?**
   - Yes, contractors are required to take the training.

8. **Whole House Vacation Rental Properties: Do They Comply with the Statute?**
   - Yes, MDH licenses whole home vacation rentals as hotels/motels in most cases and subject them to following the statute.

9. **What Happens After Training is Completed?**
   - Fill out a roster of names for all employees who completed the training along with the date of completion. Keep this on file for your next inspection.
