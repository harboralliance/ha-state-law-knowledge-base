The Office of the Attorney General in Texas has approved several training videos on human trafficking for commercial lodging establishments. These training resources are available for free or a fee, depending on the provider. The required training must be completed by new employees not later than 90 days after hire and annually thereafter. The training must also include language that protects employees from retaliation for making reports of suspected human trafficking. 

The training covers topics such as recognizing signs of human trafficking, reporting suspicions to law enforcement, and implementing policies to prevent trafficking within the hospitality industry. It is recommended that commercial lodging establishments provide this training to all employees, regardless of their size. The Office of the Attorney General has a list of approved training providers and materials on its website for further information.

It's important for hotel staff to be aware of human trafficking and take appropriate action if they suspect or observe any signs of it occurring within the establishment. This includes reporting suspicious activity to law enforcement and providing assistance to any victims who may seek help. The Office of the Attorney General provides contact information for individuals who wish to report suspected human trafficking, including the National Human Trafficking Hotline and local law enforcement agencies.
