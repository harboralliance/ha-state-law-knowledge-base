### Key Findings and Recommendations:

#### Training Requirements for Employees:
- **Source 2** states that hotels and motels in California must provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or interactive training to employees who interact with potential trafficking victims.
- The training is mandatory as of January 1, 2020, for employees employed as of July 1, 2019.

#### Posting Requirements:
- **Source 3** outlines that hotels and motels must post a model notice regarding human trafficking hotlines. This notice should be posted in English and Spanish.
- For businesses located in specific counties, a third language (such as Arabic, Cambodian, Chinese, etc.) is also required, adhering to federal Voting Rights Act provisions.

#### Penalties:
While specific penalties are not detailed in the provided sources, non-compliance with posting requirements could result in fines and legal action. It's recommended that businesses review their compliance status regularly.

3. **Post Required Notices**: Place the model notice on premises in English and Spanish. If applicable, post in additional languages as required.
4. **Verify Compliance**: Regularly review the hotel's compliance status with the posting requirements to avoid any potential fines or legal issues.

The requirement for human trafficking awareness training and posting notices are legally mandated for hotels and motels in California by Senate Bill 970. Businesses must ensure they meet these requirements promptly and adhere to ongoing compliance obligations to maintain legal and ethical standards.
