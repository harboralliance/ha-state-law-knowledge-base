Based on the information provided, here are key points about human trafficking training requirements for commercial lodging establishments in Texas:

1. The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390 requiring annual human trafficking training for employees at certain commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms.

2. This requirement only applies to establishments meeting the size threshold - smaller lodgings are encouraged but not required to provide the training.

3. The goal is to increase opportunities for victims of human trafficking to report their situation to law enforcement through trained staff.

4. Training must be completed by new employees within 90 days of hire and all employees annually thereafter. 

5. Approved training providers must issue certificates to employees upon completion.

6. Operators of commercial lodging establishments must retain copies of annual training certificates for all employees.

7. The training must include specific guidance on:
- Identifying signs of human trafficking
- Reporting suspected trafficking activities 
- The role of employees in responding to trafficking
- Required human trafficking signage

8. Approved training videos and materials are available from various organizations, with some provided free of charge.

9. Operators may choose between online or in-person training options.

10. Employees cannot be disciplined for good faith reports of suspected trafficking.

The requirement aims to increase awareness and responsiveness to human trafficking among hospitality workers, potentially saving lives by providing quicker assistance to victims.
