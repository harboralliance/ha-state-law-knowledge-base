### Overview of Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotel, Motel, and Short-Term Rental Staff

#### Legal Requirement:
The training is mandated by Minnesota law (Minnesota Statutes § 298E.01). All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training within the first 90 days of employment. This requirement applies annually.

#### Key Points to Remember:
1. **Training Requirements:**
   - Complete the training in English or one of the provided languages (Spanish/Español, Somali, Hmong).
   - Training completion is mandatory for new hires within 90 days and must be retaken every year.
   - Display a required back-of-house poster.

2. **Responsibilities of Managers and Owners:**
   - Conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees.
   - Access training via the MDH Learning Center, where they will complete a pre-training survey before accessing the package.
   
3. **Training Content:**
   - The training includes a virtual presentation with guided exercises, a Training Companion Guide (PDF), and a Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF).
   - Topics covered include recognizing signs of sex trafficking, understanding the legal requirements, and knowing how to respond.

4. **Exceptions to Training:**
   - Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms.
   - Employees working exclusively in a restaurant or providing catering services.
   - Employees with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms.
   However, MDH encourages training for all employees despite these exceptions.

5. **Accessibility and Language Support:**
   - The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.
   - Employees with specific language needs should contact health.safeharbor@state.mn.us for additional resources.

6. **Fees and Assistance:**
   - The training package is free of charge from MDH.
   - MDH does not provide assistance in printing posters; licensees must cover these costs.

7. **Continuous Compliance:**
   - Training materials must be reviewed and approved by MDH before any replacement or alteration can occur.

#### Conclusion:
Adhering to the sex trafficking prevention training requirements is crucial for hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff in Minnesota. It not only helps prevent sex trafficking but also ensures compliance with state laws. Proper training and ongoing education are vital steps in creating a safe and secure environment for all guests.
