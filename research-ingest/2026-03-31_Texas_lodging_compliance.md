Based on the provided sources, here are key points about human trafficking training for commercial lodging establishments in Texas:

- The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390 (87th Regular Session) requiring annual human trafficking training for employees directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms. However, it's encouraged for all lodging establishments regardless of size.

- The training requirement only applies to commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms. 

- Training must be completed by new employees not later than 90 days after hiring and annually thereafter for all employees.

- Approved training providers must provide a certificate of completion to each employee who completes the training, including the date taken and course title.

- Operators must maintain records of annual completion certificates for review by OAG and law enforcement agencies upon request.

- Training content should cover identifying risk factors, reporting procedures (e.g. 911, National Human Trafficking Hotline), human trafficking signage requirements, and roles in reporting.

- Approved training videos include resources from organizations like the Office of the Attorney General, Etactics, Unbound Now, Marriott International, ECPAT-USA, etc.

The goal is to provide staff with knowledge and procedures to identify and respond appropriately to human trafficking incidents in lodging establishments.
