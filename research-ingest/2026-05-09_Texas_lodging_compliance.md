### Summary of Human Trafficking Training Requirements for Commercial Lodging in Texas

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. This requirement applies only to establishments with 10 or more rooms but is encouraged for all lodging establishments regardless of size.

#### Key Requirements:
1. **Annual Training**: Employees must receive annual human trafficking training.
2. **Reporting**: Staff should know how to identify individuals at risk and report suspected human trafficking. Contact numbers include the National Human Trafficking Hotline, local law enforcement agencies, and an attorney general-designated number.
3. **Training Content**: Training must cover:
   - How human trafficking occurs in the hospitality industry.
   - The role of commercial lodging in addressing sex trafficking.
   - Vulnerabilities among hotel contract workers.
   - Reporting procedures and available resources.
4. **Certification**: Providers must issue a certificate to each employee completing the training, including completion date and course title.

#### Training Approvals:
The OAG has approved several training videos that meet these requirements. Here are some of the approved courses:

- **"Human Trafficking Hotel Training"** by Etactics (22 minutes, English, Free, Online)
- **"Human Trafficking – Texas"** by Unbound Now (26 minutes, English, $1/user/month, Online)
- **"Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry"** by Unbound Now (25 minutes each, English/Spanish, Free, Online/In-person)
