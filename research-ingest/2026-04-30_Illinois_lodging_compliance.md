Based on the information provided in the source text, here are some key points about human trafficking recognition training requirements for lodging establishments, restaurants, and truck stops in Illinois:

1. Effective date: June 1, 2020.

2. Training requirement:
- Employers must provide employees with training in recognizing human trafficking and reporting it to appropriate authorities.
- Training must be completed within 6 months of starting the job role, and every 2 years thereafter if still employed by the employer.

3. Minimum content requirements for training: 
- Definition of human trafficking and commercial exploitation of children
- How to identify individuals at risk for human trafficking
- Difference between labor and sex trafficking in lodging establishments, restaurants, and truck stops
- Role of employees in reporting and responding to human trafficking instances

4. Curriculum development:
- Employers can use their own program or a third-party one.
- The Department of Human Services will develop an approved curriculum if the employer doesn't provide their own.

5. Penalties:
- Violators face a civil penalty of up to $1,500 per offense if not corrected within 30 days after notice.

6. Effective date: This act takes effect January 1, 2026.

The goal is to ensure employees are trained to recognize potential signs of human trafficking and report it promptly to appropriate authorities to protect victims and prevent exploitation.
