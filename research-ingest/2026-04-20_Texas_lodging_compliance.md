### Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for the hospitality industry. This annual training requirement only applies to commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms but is encouraged for all lodging establishments in the State of Texas regardless of size.

### Key Points:
- **Training Requirement**: Annual training for commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms.
- **Free Training Options**: Multiple free options available, including those from the OAG and Unbound Now.
- **Costly Training Options**: Several courses are available for a fee, ranging from $25.95 to over $40 per user/month.

These resources aim to help commercial lodging establishments comply with the law and protect their staff and guests from human trafficking.
