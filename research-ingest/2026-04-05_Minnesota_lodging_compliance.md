The Minnesota Sex Trafficking Prevention Training, also known as the "Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training," is a required course for hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff in the state of Minnesota. The training aims to educate employees about recognizing signs of sex trafficking and providing appropriate responses.

### Key Points:

1. **Requirement**: All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete this training. New hires must take it within 90 days of their start date. Existing staff must retake the training annually.

2. **Content**:
   - The training covers identifying signs of sex trafficking.
   - It includes information on how to respond in various situations.
   - Employees are encouraged to learn about the specific laws and resources available in Minnesota.

3. **Implementation**:
   - Staff must display a required poster at their workplace.
   - An ongoing awareness campaign for employees is recommended.

4. **Language Availability**: The training is offered in multiple languages, including English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong, to accommodate different communities.

5. **Exceptions**: There are certain exemptions from the requirement, such as:
   - Employees under 16 years old unless they clean rooms.
   - Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant or bar providing catering services.
   - Employees with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

6. **Assistance and Costs**:
   - The Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) provides the training package for free.
   - MDH does not assist with printing posters or other compliance costs.

7. **Compliance**: Licensees are responsible for covering the costs of compliance, which may include purchasing materials like poster sets.

8. **Resources**: For those outside the lodging industry interested in this training, they can contact health.safeharbor@state.mn.us for further information.

### Training Package:
The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:
- A virtual presentation
- A Training Companion Guide (PDF)
- The Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)
- Various poster sets to be displayed in different parts of the property

This training is crucial for protecting against sex trafficking, as hotels, motels, and short-term rentals can often serve as locations where this crime occurs. By completing the required training and demonstrating a commitment to prevention, lodging staff can help ensure their establishments are safe spaces for guests.
