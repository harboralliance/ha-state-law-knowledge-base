**House Bill 390: Human Trafficking Training Requirements for Commercial Lodging Establishments**

**Introduction:**
The Texas Legislature enacted House Bill (HB) 390, which requires commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms to provide human trafficking training annually. This initiative aims to enhance awareness and reporting mechanisms among hospitality workers to prevent and respond to human trafficking.

### Key Requirements:

1. **Annual Training Requirement:**
   - Commercial lodging establishments must provide annual human trafficking training to their employees.
   - New employees must complete the training within 90 days of hire.

2. **Training Content:**
   - The training must cover:
     - How human trafficking occurs in the hospitality industry.
     - Red flags indicating potential victims of human trafficking.
     - Procedures for identifying and reporting suspected human trafficking incidents.
     - Role of employees in preventing human trafficking.
     - Requirements for maintaining human trafficking signage.

3. **Certification:**
   - Each employee must receive a certificate of completion from an approved training provider.
   - The certification must include the date of the course and the title of the course.

4. **Employee Rights:**
   - Employees "may not be disciplined, retaliated against, or otherwise discriminated against for making a good faith report of a suspected act of human trafficking."

5. **Signage Requirement:**
   - Commercial lodging establishments must display human trafficking signage as required by the Office of the Attorney General.

### Implementation and Compliance:

- Commercial lodging establishments must retain records of employee training completions.
- Records must be available for review and inspection by the Office of the Attorney General, local, state, and federal law enforcement agencies.
