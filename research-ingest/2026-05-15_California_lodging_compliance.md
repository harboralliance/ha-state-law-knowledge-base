### Summary of Information from Sources

#### Summary Points:
1. **Human Trafficking Awareness Training Requirement for Hotel and Motel Employees**:
   - **Source**: California Civil Code Section 52.6 (Senate Bill 970, 2018) and subsequent regulations.
   - **Requirement**: Effective January 1, 2020, hotel and motel employers must provide at least 20 minutes of training on human trafficking awareness to employees likely to interact with victims.
   - **Exemptions**: Employers who provided the required training before or on January 1, 2020, do not need to repeat it.

2. **Posting Requirement**:
   - **Source**: California Civil Code Section 52.6.
   - **Requirement**: Specified businesses must post a model notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking of hotline numbers for assistance or reporting unlawful activity.
   - **Businesses Covered**: Includes hotels, motels, bed and breakfasts.
   - **Posting Languages**: English and Spanish, with an additional language required in specific counties covered by federal language assistance provisions.

3. **Model Notice**:
   - **Source**: California Attorney General's Office (DOJ).
   - **Content**: The notice must include telephone hotline numbers for help or reporting human trafficking activity.
   - **Language Options**: Available in multiple languages including English, Spanish, and additional Asian, African, and Middle Eastern languages.

4. **Public Awareness and Support**:
   - **Hotlines**: National Human Trafficking Resource Center at (888) 373-7888 or Text: Be Free or 233733.
   - **Support Services**: Victims can access various forms of support through the DOJ Victims' Services Unit and local resources.

5. **Civil Remedies for Survivors**:
   - **Source**: California Civil Code Section 52.6.
   - **Availability**: Survivors of human trafficking may be entitled to civil remedies, including but not limited to monetary damages and injunctive relief.

#### Additional Resources:
- **Website**: [California Attorney General's Office](https://oag.ca.gov/human-trafficking/model-notice)
- **Document Type**: Human Trafficking Model Notice
- **Contact Information**: Victims' Services Unit at (877) 433-9069 or via email: victimservices@doj.ca.gov

### Conclusion:
The California legislation requires hotel and motel employees to receive training on human trafficking awareness by January 1, 2020. This training covers interactions with potential victims of trafficking. Additionally, specified businesses must post a model notice in English and Spanish (and potentially other languages) informing the public about human trafficking resources. Victims can access help through various hotlines and local resources. For more detailed information, including downloadable model notices in multiple languages, visit the California Attorney General's website.
