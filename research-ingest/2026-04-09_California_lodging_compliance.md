## Summary
The California Department of Justice's Office of the Attorney General provides resources and guidance on human trafficking, including how to report complaints and the availability of civil remedies for survivors. The state requires businesses and other establishments to post a notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking about telephone hotline numbers.

## Extracted Requirements
- **Applicable industry:** Hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns.
- **Training required:** Yes, employers must provide at least 20 minutes of training on human trafficking awareness to employees who are likely to interact with victims of human trafficking.
- **Signage required:** Yes, businesses are required to post a model notice informing the public about hotline numbers.
- **Reporting required:** Victims can report human trafficking by calling a hotline or texting a specified number.
- **Enforcement agency:** California Department of Justice's Office of the Attorney General.
- **Penalties:** Not explicitly detailed in the provided sources, but failure to comply with posting requirements could result in penalties.
- **Effective date:** Training requirements effective as of January 1, 2020.
