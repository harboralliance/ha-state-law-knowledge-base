The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package provides essential training for hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff to recognize and respond to potential signs of sex trafficking in their establishments. This training is required by law and must be completed annually by all required employees. The package includes a virtual presentation, a training companion guide, a manager/owner toolkit, and a set of posters that staff must display.

Key points about the training:

1. **Required Training**: All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the training within 90 days of hire or annually thereafter.
2. **Exceptions**: Some employees are exempt from the requirement, such as those under 16 years old who clean rooms but do not have direct contact with guests, or those in restaurants providing catering services without guest interaction.
3. **Access and Compliance**: Training can be accessed through the MDH Learning Center. Licensees must display a required back-of-house poster and conduct ongoing awareness campaigns for their employees.
4. **Language Availability**: The training is available in multiple languages including English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong to accommodate different communities.
5. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**: The FAQs address common concerns such as exemptions, compliance requirements, and the availability of materials in other languages.

The Minnesota Department of Health collaborates with Hospitality Minnesota to develop and provide this training to ensure that the lodging industry is better equipped to prevent and respond to sex trafficking. This collaborative effort underscores the commitment to protecting vulnerable individuals within these establishments.
