The provided documents outline training requirements for hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota to prevent and respond to sex trafficking. The key points from both the legislation and the training materials are summarized below:

### Legislation:
1. **Who Must Take the Training:**
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
   - Exceptions include:
     - Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms.
     - Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant, providing catering services, or both (no direct contact with guests).
   
2. **Timeline for Training:**
   - New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake the training every year.

3. **Required Materials:**
   - Virtual presentation
   - Training Companion Guide
   - Manager & Owner Training Toolkit
   - Back of House poster set (required to display)

4. **Compliance Costs:**
   - Licensees are required to pay the costs of compliance, including printing posters.
   
5. **Exceptions and Exemptions:**
   - Some facilities may have limited exemptions based on their operations.

### Training Materials:
1. **Purpose:**
   - To educate staff on identifying signs of sex trafficking and how to respond effectively.
   
2. **Format:**
   - Virtual presentation with guided exercises
   - Training Companion Guide (PDF)
   - Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)

3. **Language Availability:**
   - English, Spanish/Español, Somali, Hmong

4. **Access and Support:**
   - Managers and owners can access training through the MDH Learning Center.
   - Employees can access training in multiple languages.

5. **FAQs:**
   - Answers cover various scenarios like seasonal employees, temporary workers, and exempt categories.
   
### Steps After Training:
1. **Documentation:**
   - Managers/owners should create a roster of names for all employees who completed the training, along with the date of completion.
   - This roster should be kept on file for inspection.

2. **Communication:**
   - Contact MDH at `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us` if any questions arise.

### Resources:
- **Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) Safe Harbor Program:**
  - Email: `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`
  - Website: [MDH Safe Harbor](https://www.health.state.mn.us/safeharbor/)

This comprehensive approach ensures that hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff in Minnesota are equipped with the knowledge and resources to prevent and respond effectively to sex trafficking.
