The California Attorney General (AG) has enacted legislation that requires certain businesses and establishments, including hotels and motels, to post a notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking about telephone hotline numbers for seeking help or reporting unlawful activity. This law took effect on April 1, 2013, with additional requirements added by subsequent legislation.

The model notice must be posted in English and Spanish, and in certain counties where language assistance is required under federal Voting Rights Act provisions, a third language (such as Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, Cambodian, Chinese, Eastern Armenian, Farsi, Haitian Creole, Hindi, Hmong, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Laotian, Pashto, Polish, Punjabi, Romanian, Russian, Tagalog, Thai, or Vietnamese) may also be required.

Failure to post the notice as mandated can result in penalties. Each mandated business is required to provide a copy of the model notice upon request.

Resources for victims of human trafficking include:
- The California Attorney General's Victims' Services Unit at (877) 433-9069 or victimservices@doj.ca.gov
- The National Human Trafficking Hotline at (888) 373-7888, which can also be texted by dialing 233733 (Be Free)

For more information on human trafficking legislation and resources, visit the California Attorney General's website.
