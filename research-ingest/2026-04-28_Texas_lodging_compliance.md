The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for the hospitality industry. The goal is to provide additional opportunities for human trafficking to be reported to law enforcement by requiring human trafficking training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments.

### Key Points:

1. **Training Requirement**:
   - Applies to commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms.
   - Encouraged but not mandatory for smaller establishments.

2. **Content of Training**:
   - Training must include identifying individuals at risk of human trafficking.
   - Information on the role of employees in reporting and responding to human trafficking.
   - Details on contact information for reporting human trafficking, including the National Human Trafficking Hotline and local law enforcement agencies.
   - Requirements for human trafficking signage.
   - Employee training completion requirements.

3. **Training Providers**:
   - Various organizations offer approved human trafficking training videos.
   - These include the Office of the Attorney General, Etactics, Unbound Now, Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking, Extended Stay America, Marriott International, ECPAT-USA, Refugee Services of Texas, PBJ Learning, and Compliance Learning Solutions.

### Steps to Obtain Training:

1. **Contact Approved Organizations**: For course-specific questions, contact the training organization directly.
2. **Visit Official Website**: Many approved videos can be accessed online through official links provided on the OAG's website.
3. **Check Delivery Method**: Some courses are available online only, while others may also offer in-person sessions.

### Example of an Approved Training Video:
- **Title**: "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry"
  - **Organization**: Unbound Now
  - **Length**: 25 minutes (English) or 25 minutes (Spanish)
  - **Cost**: Free
  - **Delivery Method**: Online or in-person. Contact Unbound Now for more information.

### Compliance Requirements:
- New employees must complete the training within 90 days of hiring.
- All employees must be trained annually.
- Certificates of completion must be retained by the operator of a commercial lodging establishment.
- Records must be made available for review and inspection upon request from the OAG, local, state, and federal law enforcement agencies.
