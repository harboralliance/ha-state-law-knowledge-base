This content provides information about approved human trafficking training videos for commercial lodging establishments in Texas. The required training must be completed by new employees within 90 days of hire and annually thereafter. Key points include:

1. Training covers recognizing and responding to human trafficking in the hospitality industry.
2. It is mandatory for establishments with 10 or more rooms but encouraged for all.
3. The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) must approve training providers.
4. Employees may not be disciplined for reporting suspected human trafficking.
5. Approved training providers include various organizations offering online and in-person courses.

The content also mentions specific approved training videos, their durations, languages, costs, delivery methods, and contact information for more details on each course. This training aims to improve awareness and response capabilities among hospitality industry workers regarding human trafficking issues.
