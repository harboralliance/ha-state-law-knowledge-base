The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has implemented a requirement for human trafficking training for workers directly employed by commercial lodging establishments. The legislation, House Bill 390 (87th Regular Session), mandates that these establishments provide annual human trafficking training to their employees.

### Key Requirements:

1. **Annual Training Requirement**: 
   - Commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms must provide annual human trafficking training.
   - The training is encouraged for all lodging establishments regardless of size.

2. **Content of Training**:
   - The training should cover how and why human trafficking takes place in the hospitality industry.
   - It should include guidance on identifying individuals at risk of human trafficking.
   - Employees must be trained to recognize signs of human trafficking, such as physical injuries or submissive behavior.
   - Staff must understand their roles in reporting and responding to human trafficking incidents.

3. **Training Completion**:
   - New employees must complete the training not later than 90 days after being hired.
   - All employees must receive annual training.
   - The approved training provider must provide a certificate of completion, which must include the date the course was taken and the title of the course.

4. **Signage Requirement**:
   - Commercial lodging establishments are required to display human trafficking signage.

5. **Disciplinary Protection**:
   - Employees "may not be disciplined, retaliated against, or otherwise discriminated against for making a good faith report of a suspected act of human trafficking."

### Approved Training Videos:

The OAG has approved several training videos that comply with the requirements set forth in House Bill 390. These include:

- **Human Trafficking Hotel Training** (Office of the Attorney General) - Free, Online
- **Human Trafficking – Texas** (Etactics) - $1/user/month, Online
- **Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry** (Unbound Now) - Free, Online & In-person
- **Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training** (Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking) - $25.95, Online & In-person
- **Human Trafficking Training** (Extended Stay America) - Free, Online via internal website only accessible to ESA associates.
- **Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry** (Marriott International) - Free, Available to Marriott associates.
- **Your Role in Preventing Human Trafficking: Recognize the Signs** (ECPAT-USA) - Free, Online
- **Survivor Services/HT101** (Refugee Services of Texas) - Free, In-person
- **Human Trafficking Essentials: Texas Hospitality Awareness HB 390** (PBJ Learning) - $24.99, Online
- **Combating Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry** (Compliance Learning Solutions) - $25.95, Online
