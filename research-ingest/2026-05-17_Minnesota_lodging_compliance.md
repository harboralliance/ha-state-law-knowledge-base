### Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals in Minnesota

#### Overview
Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are significant locations where sex trafficking can occur. The Minnesota Legislature requires these establishments to provide specific training to prevent and respond to sex trafficking.

#### Requirements
1. **Completion of Training:**
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the training.
   - New hires must take the training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake the training annually.

2. **Posting the Back of House Poster:**
   - Display the back-of-house poster in a location where all staff can see it.

3. **Ongoing Awareness Campaign:**
   - Conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees to reinforce their understanding and response to sex trafficking.

#### Training Package
The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:
- A virtual presentation with guided exercises
- A training companion guide (PDF)
- Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF) to comply with the law
- A set of posters, including one for the back of house

#### About This Training
- The training is developed by the Minnesota Department of Health in partnership with Hospitality Minnesota.
- It covers recognizing signs of sex trafficking and understanding how to respond effectively.

#### Accessing the Training
- **Managers and Owners:**
  - Access the training through the MDH Learning Center.
  - Complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package.
  
- **Employees:**
  - Employee training is available in multiple languages (English, Spanish, Somali, Hmong).

#### Frequently Asked Questions
1. **Do I still need to take this training if I already took another one on sex trafficking?**
   - Yes, you must complete the Minnesota-specific training.

2. **Are resorts and vacation shares required to provide the training?**
   - Only establishments holding a hotel license are required.

3. **Does the law apply to tribal nations?**
   - No, but MDH encourages participation using MDH materials.

4. **What if I'm unsure about my licensing status?**
   - Contact health.foodlodging@state.mn.us or 651-201-4500 for assistance.

5. **Do managers/owners and employees have to take the training every year?**
   - Yes, it is an annual requirement tied to licensure.

6. **Can we replace the MDH training with our own materials?**
   - No, but exceptions can be made under prior approval from MDH.

7. **If a hotel or short-term rental owner/operator/manager does not have direct contact with guests, are they required to take the training?**
   - Yes, they must still take the training.

8. **Are seasonal employees exempted by law?**
   - No, but they should be trained within 90 days of starting work.

9. **Do contracted workers need to complete the training?**
   - Yes, all staff must complete the training.

10. **Are whole house vacation rental properties subject to compliance with the statute?**
    - Yes, MDH licenses them as hotels/motels in many cases.

#### Contact Information
For questions or assistance accessing the training, contact health.safeharbor@state.mn.us.

### Conclusion
The Minnesota law mandates specific training for hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff to prevent and respond to sex trafficking. This comprehensive guide outlines the requirements and provides detailed information on obtaining and complying with the mandated training.
