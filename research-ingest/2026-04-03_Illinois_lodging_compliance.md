The bill (SB1422) introduced in Illinois requires lodging establishments, restaurants, and truck stops to provide their employees with training on recognizing human trafficking and protocols for reporting it. The training must be completed within six months of employment and every two years thereafter. The Department of Human Services is tasked with developing a curriculum for the required training program. Any employer or law enforcement agency that discovers a violation of this act may issue a reasonable notice to the employer, informing them that they will face a civil penalty if they fail to cure the violation within 30 days after receiving the notice. The bill takes effect January 1, 2026.
