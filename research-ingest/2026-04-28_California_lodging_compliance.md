### Summary

#### Overview:
The document provides comprehensive information on human trafficking, including legal requirements, resources for survivors, and model notices. The primary focus is on training requirements for employees in motels and hotels.

#### Key Points:

1. **Training Requirement:**
   - **SB 970 (2018)** requires hotel and motel employers to provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness.
   - Training must be provided to employees likely to interact with victims of human trafficking, both existing employees as of July 1, 2019, and new hires within six months.

2. **Training Content:**
   - The training should cover basic information about human trafficking, including how to recognize signs of trafficking, the importance of reporting suspicions, and what to do in case of an incident.
   
3. **Model Notice Requirement:**
   - Businesses required by law must post a notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking of hotline numbers for assistance.
   - The notice must be available in multiple languages, including English and Spanish.

4. **Penalties:**
   - Failure to comply with posting requirements may result in fines or other penalties.

5. **Resources:**
   - Various resources are provided for survivors, including the National Human Trafficking Hotline, the California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST), and guidance on contacting local law enforcement agencies.

### Detailed Breakdown:

#### Training Requirement:
- **SB 970 (2018)** mandates that hotel and motel employers provide at least 20 minutes of training.
  - This includes existing employees as of July 1, 2019.
  - New hires within six months must also receive the training.

#### Model Notice Requirement:
- **SB 1193 (2013)** requires specified businesses to post a notice informing the public and victims about hotline numbers for help.
  - Additional requirements were added with SB 225 (2017) and AB 260 (2017).
  - The notice must be available in English, Spanish, and additional languages as required by federal law.

#### Penalties:
- Failure to post the required notices can result in fines or other penalties.
