The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for the hospitality industry. The bill seeks to provide additional opportunities for human trafficking to be reported to law enforcement by requiring human trafficking training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. This annual training requirement only applies to commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, but it is encouraged for all lodging establishments in the State of Texas regardless of size.

The OAG has approved several training videos on human trafficking that are available for use. These include:

1. "Human Trafficking Hotel Training" by Etactics (22 minutes, English, Free, Online)
2. "Human Trafficking - Texas" by Unbound Now (26 minutes, English, $1/user/month, Online)
3. "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry" by Unbound Now (25 minutes, English, Free, Online & in-person; Contact for more information)
4. "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry" by Unbound Now (25 minutes, Spanish, Free, Online & in-person; Contact for more information)
5. "Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training" by Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking (45-60 minutes, English, $25.95, Online & In-person; Contact for more information)
6. "Human Trafficking Training" by Extended Stay America (30 minutes, English, Free, Online via internal website only accessible to ESA associates)
7. "Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry" by Marriott International (60-80 minutes, English, Free, Available to Marriott associates; Contact for more information)
8. "Your Role in Preventing Human Trafficking: Recognize the Signs" by ECPAT-USA (30 minutes, English and Spanish*; Contact for more information)
9. "Survivor Services/HT101" by Refugee Services of Texas (60 minutes, English, Free, In-person; Contact for more information)
10. "Human Trafficking Essentials: Texas Hospitality Awareness HB 390" by PBJ Learning (26 minutes, English, $24.99, Online; Contact for more information)
11. "Combating Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry" by Compliance Learning Solutions (45 minutes, English, $25.95, Online; Contact for more information)

To use these training videos, organizations must contact the respective training organization directly to obtain more information about the course and how to enroll employees.

Overall, the OAG has made it easier for commercial lodging establishments to provide human trafficking training to their employees, which can help prevent human trafficking in the hospitality industry.
