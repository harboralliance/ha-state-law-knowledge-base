### Summary of Required Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals in Minnesota

#### Legal Requirements:
The training requirement stems from **Minnesota law** (specifically, Minn. Stat. § 173A.04), which mandates that all hotel, motel, and short-term rental property owners, managers, and on-site employees complete the required sex trafficking prevention and response training.

### Key Requirements:

1. **All Employees Must Complete Training:**
   - Owners, managers, and all on-site employees must complete the required training.
   - There are some exceptions based on specific criteria (e.g., employees under 16 who clean rooms).

2. **New Employees:**
   - New employees must take this training within **90 days** of hire.

3. **Annual Retraining:**
   - All required staff must retake the training every year.

4. **Displaying a Poster:**
   - Managers and owners are required to display a back-of-house poster that outlines the requirements for sex trafficking prevention and response.

5. **Ongoing Awareness Campaign:**
   - Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are encouraged to conduct an ongoing awareness campaign to educate their staff about sex trafficking signs and responses.

### Training Content:

The training includes:
- A **virtual presentation** with guided exercises.
- A **Training Companion Guide** (PDF) for viewing the virtual presentation.
- A **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit** (PDF) with detailed instructions on complying with the law.
- A set of posters, including one that must be displayed in a visible location.

### Accessing the Training:

1. **Managers and Owners:**
   - They can access the training through the Minnesota Department of Health’s Learning Center.
   - Before accessing the training package, they are asked to complete a brief pre-training survey.

2. **Employees:**
   - Employees can access the employee training in multiple languages (English, Spanish, Somali, Hmong).

### Compliance and Costs:

- The law does not require any fees for the mandatory training package.
- Licensees (owners, managers) are required to pay for the costs of compliance, such as printing posters.

### Additional Information:

1. **Training Availability:**
   - Training materials will be available in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.
   
2. **Exemptions and Exceptions:**
   - Managers, owners, and employees who do not have direct contact with guests must still complete the training.

### Contact Information:

For assistance or questions about accessing the training:
- Email: health.safeharbor@state.mn.us

### Conclusion:

The Minnesota law requires hotels, motels, and short-term rental properties to provide comprehensive sex trafficking prevention and response training to all employees annually. This includes displaying a required poster and conducting ongoing awareness campaigns. Failure to comply can lead to penalties or other enforcement actions.

This training is crucial for protecting both staff and guests from the risk of sex trafficking and can help save lives by providing early intervention.
