Based on the information provided in the two sources, here are key points about human trafficking and requirements for businesses in California:

Key points about human trafficking:

- Human trafficking is a serious criminal and civil violation of law.
- CRD (California's Office of Civil Rights) enforces the state's human trafficking civil rights laws and investigates complaints of trafficking.
- Civil remedies may be available to survivors.

Requirements for businesses:

- By Jan 1, 2020, hotels/motels must provide at least 20 mins of training on human trafficking awareness to employees who interact with victims. This applies to existing employees as of July 1, 2019 and new employees within 6 months.
- Employers must post a model notice informing the public/victims about human trafficking hotline numbers.
- The mandated notice must be posted in English and Spanish. For businesses in certain counties, posting in a third language may also be required.
