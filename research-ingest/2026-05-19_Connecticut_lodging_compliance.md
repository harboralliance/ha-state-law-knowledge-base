## Summary
Connecticut has robust training and legislative frameworks in place to address human trafficking, including sex trafficking. The state requires various professionals, such as DCF staff, foster parents, service providers, schools, law enforcement, hospital and medical staff, hospitality industry personnel, and other professionals, to receive annual basic training on human trafficking. Additionally, the state has passed several public acts that strengthen protections for victims of human trafficking, expand conditions under which certain crimes are committed, and establish mandatory data reporting.

Two-day comprehensive training program for DCF Academy for Workforce Development.
- **Signage requirement:** No specific signage requirements mentioned.
- **Reporting requirements:** Mandatory data reporting by state agencies and professionals involved in human trafficking cases.

## Enforcement and Penalties
Connecticut laws mandate that public acts related to human trafficking must be documented by state attorneys and local law enforcement. Hotels, motels, inns, and similar lodging must provide trafficking training and awareness campaigns annually and keep guest transactions and receipts for at least six months.

## Legal Framework
The state has enacted several public acts aimed at combating human trafficking:
- **2017 Public Act 17-32:** Expands the definition of human trafficking and imposes penalties.
- **2016 Public Act 16-71:** Requires annual trafficking training for lodging establishments.
- **2015 Public Act 15-195:** Expands protections for victims, including expanding the crime of human trafficking.
- **2014 Public Act 14-186:** Provides services to human trafficking victims and trains law enforcement on human trafficking cases.
- **2013 Public Act 13-166:** Aligns with federal trafficking laws and expands affirmative defenses for minors.
- **2012 Public Act 12-141:** Defines commercial sexual exploitation of a minor as a class C felony.
- **2011 Public Act 11-180:** Requires reporting of suspected abuse or neglect to the Department of Children and Families.
- **2010 Public Act 10-115:** Provides a safe harbor for exploited children.

## Victims' Services
Connecticut offers services for human trafficking victims, including:
- Training for DCF staff on handling human trafficking cases.
- Creation of multidisciplinary teams to work with human trafficking cases.
- Affirmative defense for minors in trafficking cases.
- Expanding the crime of human trafficking by broadening conditions under which it is committed.

## Public Awareness and Prevention
Connecticut has established a statewide oversight and monitoring body and mandates ongoing monitoring efforts to combat trafficking. Additionally, public awareness campaigns are encouraged through various training programs and resources provided by state agencies.
