### Analysis

#### Training Requirement for Employees in Motels and Hotels:

1. **Source 1: SB 970**
   - **Summary:** This law requires hotel and motel employers to provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness to employees who are likely to interact with victims of trafficking.
   - **Date of Effectiveness:** January 1, 2020.

2. **Source 2: California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST)**
   - **Summary:** CAST provides additional information on the requirement and supports employees who need it for compliance with SB 970.
   - **Date of Effectiveness:** January 1, 2020.

3. **Source 3: Model Notice from State Department of Justice**
   - **Summary:** The model notice includes language that establishments must post to inform the public and victims of human trafficking about the hotline numbers available for help or reporting unlawful activity.
   - **Date of Effectiveness:** Applies to businesses as of April 1, 2013.

#### Summary:
The requirement for training employees in motels and hotels regarding human trafficking awareness is mandated by **SB 970**, which became effective on January 1, 2020. This legislation aims to enhance awareness among employees who interact with potential victims of human trafficking, thereby improving their ability to respond appropriately.

### Key Points:
- **Training Duration:** Minimum 20 minutes of interactive training.
- **Effective Date:** January 1, 2020.
- **Employees Affected:** Those likely to interact with victims of human trafficking.

### Compliance and Enforcement:
Compliance with this requirement is crucial for businesses to avoid penalties. The California Department of Justice provides resources and support to help businesses understand and fulfill their obligations under SB 970.

By adhering to these requirements, businesses can ensure they are prepared to address potential situations involving human trafficking, thereby contributing to the safety and well-being of individuals in their care.
