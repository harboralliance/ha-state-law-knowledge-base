### Laws and Regulations

**Minnesota Law Requirements:**
1. **Training Requirement:**
   - All hotel, motel, and short-term rental owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
   - New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
   - Training must be retaken every year.

2. **Exceptions to Training Requirements:**
   - Employees under 16 years old unless they clean rooms
   - Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant, providing catering services, or both (if no direct contact with guests)
   - Employees with no direct contact with guests

**Statute Location:**
- The training requirement is mandated by Minnesota law. More details can be found in the [Minnesota Statutes](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/).

### Training Package and Content

**Training Components:**
1. **Virtual Presentation:** A video with guided exercises.
2. **Training Companion Guide (PDF):** A resource for viewing the virtual presentation.
3. **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF):** Instructions on how to comply with the law.
4. **Poster Set:** 
   - Back of House (required to display)
   - Housekeeping
   - Restaurant/Bar
   - Front desk/lobby
   - Security

**Training Duration:**
- The training is approximately 45 to 50 minutes long, but viewing translated versions may take longer.

### Implementation and Compliance

1. **Posting the Back of House Poster:** It must be displayed in a location visible to all staff.
2. **Awareness Campaigns:** Conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees.
3. **Access to Training:** 
   - Managers and owners can access the training through MDH’s Learning Center, completing a pre-training survey first.

### Conclusion

The Minnesota law mandates annual sex trafficking prevention and response training for hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff to help prevent and respond to human trafficking. The training package includes a video presentation, companion guide, toolkit, and required posters. Managers and owners are responsible for ensuring compliance by posting the necessary signs and conducting ongoing awareness campaigns for their employees.
