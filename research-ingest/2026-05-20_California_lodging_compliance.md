### Summary

The key points extracted from the provided sources are:

1. **Training Requirement for Hotel and Motel Employees**:
   - Effective January 1, 2020, hotels and motels must provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training on human trafficking awareness to employees who interact with or come into contact with victims.
   - The training must be given to current employees by July 1, 2019, and to new employees within six months of their start date.

2. **Posting Requirement for Public Awareness**:
   - Hotels, motels, and bed and breakfasts are required to post a notice informing the public about human trafficking resources, including hotline numbers.
   - The notice must be posted in English and Spanish, and in additional languages in certain counties subject to federal Voting Rights Act requirements.

3. **Model Notice for Human Trafficking**:
   - A model notice template is available on the California Department of Justice website for downloading.
   - Different versions of the notice are provided for various languages including Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, Cambodian, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Eastern Armenian, Farsi, Haitian Creole, Hindi, Hmong, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Laotian, Pashto, Polish, Punjabi, Romanian, Russian, Tagalog, Thai, and Vietnamese.
