The text discusses the requirements for sex trafficking prevention training in hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota, which are governed by state law.

### Requirements:
1. **Training Content**: The required training includes:
   - A virtual presentation with guided exercises
   - A Training Companion Guide
   - A Manager & Owner Training Toolkit
   - Back of House posters (required to display)
   - Various sections for housekeeping, restaurant/bar, front desk/lobby, and security staff

2. **Implementation**: All hotel, motel, and short-term rental owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the training:
   - New hires must take it within 90 days of hiring
   - All required staff must retake the training annually

3. **Displaying the Poster**: Licensees are required to display a back-of-house poster, which includes the MNHTPRN logo and contact information.

4. **Awareness Campaign**: Managers and owners are encouraged to conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for all employees regarding sex trafficking.

5. **Training Package Availability**:
   - The package is available in English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.
   - Employees can access it through the MDH Learning Center.
   - A brief pre-training survey must be completed by managers/owners before accessing the training package.

### Exemptions:
- Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms
- Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant or provide catering services with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms

### Exceptions and Clarifications:
- Owners, operators, and managers are still required to take the training even if they do not have direct contact with guests.
- The law does not explicitly exempt seasonal employees, but MDH recommends training them within 90 days of starting work.

### Compliance and Costs:
- The MN Department of Health (MDH) provides the free training package.
- Licensees are responsible for the costs of compliance, including printing posters.
- Contracted workers, such as cleaners, must also take the required training.

### Resources:
- For further questions or assistance accessing the training, contact MDH at `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.
- The MNHTPRN (Minnesota Human Trafficking Prevention and Response Network) can be reached at `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.

This comprehensive approach ensures that staff in the lodging industry are equipped with the knowledge to recognize and respond to sex trafficking, thereby contributing to the prevention of such exploitation.
