### Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training

#### About This Training
The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:
- A virtual presentation – a video with guided exercises
- Training Companion Guide (PDF)
- Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF) to comply with the law
- Poster set (Back of House, Housekeeping, Restaurant/Bar, Front desk/lobby, Security)

#### Requirements for Compliance
To comply with Minnesota law:
1. All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
2. New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
3. All required staff must retake this training every year.
4. Display the required back of house poster.

#### Training Access
- **Managers and Owners**: Access through the MDH Learning Center. Complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package.
- **Employees**: Access in English or other languages upon request.

#### Frequently Asked Questions
1. **Already Completed Training on Sex Trafficking**:
   - Yes, you still need to complete this specific Minnesota lodging training.

2. **Resorts, Bed and Breakfasts, Vacation Shares, Short-Term Rentals**:
   - Only those holding a hotel license are required.

3. **Tribal Nations**:
   - No specific requirement but encouraged to provide the training.

4. **Exempt Employees**:
   - Employees under 16 (unless they clean rooms), those exclusively working in restaurants, providing catering services, or both.

5. **Employee Licenses**:
   - Contact the Food, Pool, and Lodging section at MDH for verification.

6. **Training Costs**:
   - Free package provided by MDH; additional costs for printing posters are not covered.

7. **Language Availability**:
   - Training materials will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

8. **Annual Requirement**:
   - Yes, training must be completed annually tied to licensure.

9. **Replacing MDH Materials**:
   - Replacement is not allowed without prior review and approval from MDH.

10. **Seasonal Employees**:
    - If covered by a license, they must receive the training within 90 days of starting work.

11. **Temporary Employees**:
    - Even if employed for less than 90 days, it is encouraged to complete training.

12. **Contracted Workers**:
    - Contracted workers, including cleaners, are required to take the training.

13. **Whole House Vacation Rentals**:
    - Subject to compliance with the statute as hotels/motels in most cases.

#### Next Steps After Training
- Complete a roster of employees who completed the training and date.
- Keep this roster on file for inspections.

For more information or assistance, contact MDH at [health.safeharbor@state.mn.us](mailto:health.safeharbor@state.mn.us).
