**Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotel and Motel Staff in Minnesota**

The Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) requires hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff to complete sex trafficking prevention training as per state law. This requirement aims to educate employees about identifying the signs of sex trafficking and how to respond effectively.

### Key Requirements:

1. **All Employees Must Complete Training:**
   - Owners, managers, and on-site employees must participate in the required training.
   
2. **Timing for New Employees:**
   - New employees need to complete the training within 90 days of hire.
   
3. **Annual Retraining:**
   - All required staff members must retake the training every year.

### Training Components:

The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:
- A virtual presentation (video with guided exercises)
- A Training Companion Guide (PDF) as a resource for viewing the virtual presentation
- A Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF) to help comply with the law
- Posters: Back of House (required to display), Housekeeping, Restaurant/Bar, Front desk/lobby, Security

### Displaying the Poster:

Managers and owners must ensure that the required back-of-house poster is displayed in a location where all staff can see it.

### Conducting an Ongoing Awareness Campaign:

In addition to completing the training, managers and owners are encouraged to conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees to reinforce the importance of recognizing and responding to sex trafficking.

### Exceptions:

The law includes some exceptions:
- Employees under 16 years old (unless they clean rooms)
- Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant providing catering services
- Employees with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms

However, these exemptions do not apply to owners, operators, and managers.

### Additional Information:

- **Access to Training:** Managers and owners can access the training through the MDH Learning Center. They must complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the package.
- **Employee Training:** Employee training is available in multiple languages, including English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.
- **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):** The FAQs provide detailed information on various aspects of the training requirements.

### Compliance:

Licensees are responsible for paying the costs of compliance. MDH does not assist with printing posters or any other additional costs associated with the training.

By completing this required training, hotel and motel staff can play a crucial role in preventing sex trafficking within their establishments and helping to protect vulnerable individuals.
