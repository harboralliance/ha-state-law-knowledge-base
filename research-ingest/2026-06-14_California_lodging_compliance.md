## Summary of Requirements

The requirements outlined in the provided sources pertain to human trafficking awareness training for employees of motels and hotels in California. Below is a detailed summary:

### Training Requirement
**SB 970 (External Link)** mandates that, by January 1, 2020:
- Hotel and motel employers provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness.
- The training must be provided to each employee who is likely to interact or come into contact with victims of human trafficking. This includes employees employed as of July 1, 2019, and new employees within six months of their employment in the role.

### Training Content
The training should cover:
- Awareness of human trafficking laws.
- Recognition of signs and indicators of potential human trafficking situations.
- The responsibilities and obligations of employees regarding human trafficking.
- Information on how to report suspected cases of human trafficking.
- Strategies for supporting victims of human trafficking within the workplace.

### Notice Posting Requirement
**Senate Bill 1193 (External Link)** mandates that specified businesses post a notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking of telephone hotline numbers to seek help or report unlawful activity. This requirement includes hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns as of April 1, 2013.

### Notice Content
The mandated notice must include:
- Telephone hotline numbers for seeking help.
- Information on how to text for services and support (as required by SB 225).
- Contact information for the National Human Trafficking Hotline: 888-373-7888 or Text: 233-733 (Be Free).
- Contact information for local victims' service units.

### Languages Required
Each mandated business must post the notice in English and Spanish. For businesses located in specific counties that are subject to the language assistance provisions of the federal Voting Rights Act, a third language posting is also required. Specific languages include:

### Penalties for Non-Compliance
Failure to post the notice in compliance with the requirements can result in penalties. The exact penalties are not detailed in the provided sources but typically involve fines or other legal repercussions.

### Key Points
- **Training**: Required by January 1, 2020.
- **Notice Posting**: Required for hotels, motels, and bed and breakfasts as of April 1, 2013.
- **Languages**: Must be posted in English, Spanish, and a third language if applicable.
- **Penalties**: Fines or legal repercussions for non-compliance.

These requirements are aimed at enhancing awareness and response capabilities within the hospitality industry regarding human trafficking, ensuring that employees are better equipped to identify, report, and support victims.
