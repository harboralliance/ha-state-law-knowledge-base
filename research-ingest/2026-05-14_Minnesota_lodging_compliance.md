### Overview

The Minnesota law requires hotels, motels, and short-term rentals to provide sex trafficking prevention and response training to all on-site employees, including managers and owners. The training is mandatory for compliance with state laws.

### Key Requirements:

1. **New Employees**: Must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
2. **Current Staff**: Must retake the training every year.
3. **Displaying the Poster**: All required staff must display the back-of-house poster in a location where all employees will see it.
4. **Ongoing Awareness Campaign**: Managers and owners are required to conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for their employees.

### Training Content:

The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:
- A virtual presentation (video with guided exercises).
- A Training Companion Guide in PDF format.
- The Manager & Owner Training Toolkit in PDF format.
- A poster set, including the back-of-house poster that is required to be displayed.

### Exceptions:

The training package generally applies to all hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota. However, there are some exceptions:
1. **Employees Under 16**: Unless they clean rooms.
2. **Restaurant Staff**: If their sole employment is in a restaurant, providing catering services, or both.
3. **No Direct Contact with Guests or Guest Rooms**: Employees who do not interact directly with guests.

### Training Accessibility:

- The training package is available in English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.
- Managers and owners can access the training through the MDH Learning Center.
- Employees must complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package.

### Additional Information:

1. **Frequently Asked Questions**: There are several FAQs to help clarify the requirements and address common concerns.
2. **Contact Information**: For any questions or assistance with accessing the training, contact `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.
3. **Costs of Compliance**: MDH does not charge a fee for the mandatory training package but does not assist with printing posters.

### Training Schedule:

- The Minnesota-specific aspects of the MDH training must be delivered to employees.
- Updates to the package will be provided by November 1 each year.
- Seasonal employees should be trained within 90 days of starting work.

### Conclusion:

The Minnesota law for sex trafficking prevention and response training is designed to ensure that hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff are well-equipped to identify and respond to instances of sex trafficking. Compliance with this law is essential for licensees and can make a significant difference in preventing and responding to trafficking incidents.
