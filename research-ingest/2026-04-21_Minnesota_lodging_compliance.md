Based on the information provided in both the legislation (Minnesota Statutes Section 169A.705) and the accompanying training materials, here are the key points regarding sex trafficking prevention and response training for hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota:

### Legislation Requirements:
1. **Who Must Take Training**: All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
2. **New Employees**: New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
3. **Annual Requirement**: All required staff must retake this training every year.
4. **Displaying Posters**: Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals must display a specific back-of-house poster that is part of the training package.

### Training Package:
The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:
- A virtual presentation (video with guided exercises)
- Training Companion Guide
- Manager & Owner Training Toolkit
- Posters for various locations within the establishment

### Key Training Components:
1. **Virtual Presentation**: Educates staff on recognizing signs of sex trafficking and how to respond.
2. **Training Materials**: Companions provide additional resources and reference materials.
3. **Poster Set**: Specific posters must be displayed in key areas, including back-of-house, housekeeping, restaurant/bar, front desk/lobby, and security.

### Training Delivery:
- **Managers and Owners**: Access training through the MDH Learning Center with a pre-training survey required before accessing the package.
- **Employees**: Training is available in multiple languages (English, Spanish, Somali, Hmong).

### Exceptions:
The law provides exemptions for certain categories of employees, such as those under 16 who clean rooms, those working exclusively in restaurants providing catering services, and those with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

### Implementation:
- Training completion should be documented and maintained on file.
- Owners, operators, and managers are still required to complete the training even if they do not have direct contact with guests.

### Legal Compliance:
Licensees (those holding hotel licenses) are responsible for paying the costs of compliance. The MDH does not provide assistance with printing posters.

### Additional Resources:
- **MDH Learning Center**: For managers and owners.
- **Safe Harbor Program Contact Email**: health.safeharbor@state.mn.us

This training is crucial for ensuring that all staff within hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are equipped to recognize and respond effectively to potential instances of sex trafficking.
