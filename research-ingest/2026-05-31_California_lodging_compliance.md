### Summary:

**Purpose:** The document provides a model notice that businesses, including hotels and motels, are required to post regarding human trafficking. This notice includes contact information for reporting suspicious activity or seeking help.

**Key Requirements:**
1. **Posting:** Businesses must post the notice in English and Spanish.
2. **Language Variants:** For businesses located in certain counties subject to federal language assistance provisions of the Voting Rights Act, a third language (such as Arabic, Chinese, Hindi, etc.) is also required.
3. **Content:** The notice should include:
   - Telephone hotline numbers for seeking help or reporting unlawful activity.
   - A specified number that victims can text for services and support.
4. **Penalties:** Failure to post the notice as required can result in penalties.
