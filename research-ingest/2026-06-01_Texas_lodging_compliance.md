The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas is implementing new requirements for human trafficking training in commercial lodging establishments. Specifically, House Bill 390 requires certain commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms to provide annual human trafficking training to their employees. Smaller establishments are also encouraged to provide this training.

The OAG has approved a number of human trafficking training videos that meet the requirements set forth by House Bill 390. These videos cover various aspects of identifying and responding to human trafficking, including in the hospitality industry. Some examples of approved training videos include:

1. "Human Trafficking Hotel Training" by the Office of the Attorney General
2. "Human Trafficking – Texas" by Etactics
3. "Texas Commercial Lodging Human Trafficking Awareness and Response" by National Course Portal

These training videos vary in length and cost, and can be delivered online or in-person. Some are free, while others require a one-time fee for each user.

In addition to providing approved training videos, the OAG also provides guidelines for human trafficking training requirements. These guidelines state that the training must be completed by new employees not later than 90 days after they are hired, and all employees must receive annual training. The approved training provider must provide a certificate of completion to every employee who completes the training.

Overall, these new training requirements and approved training videos are designed to help prevent human trafficking in commercial lodging establishments and ensure that employees have the knowledge and skills needed to identify and respond to potential cases of human trafficking.
