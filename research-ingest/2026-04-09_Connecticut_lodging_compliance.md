# State Research Capture

## State
Connecticut

## Summary
Connecticut provides various trainings for providers, foster parents, schools, law enforcement, healthcare workers, hospitality industry professionals, and the community to combat human trafficking. The state has also enacted several public acts addressing human trafficking laws, including sex trafficking, with effective dates ranging from 2010 to 2025.

## Extracted Requirements
- **Applicable Industry:** Hotels, motels, inns, similar lodging businesses; schools; law enforcement; healthcare providers; hospitality industry professionals.
- **Training Required:** Introduction to Child Trafficking in Connecticut, Introduction to Human Trafficking in Connecticut for Law Enforcement, Introduction to Human Trafficking in Connecticut for Emergency Medical Services, Child Trafficking Training for Medical Providers/Emergency Medicine, Introduction to Labor Trafficking in Connecticut.
- **Signage Required:** Not explicitly mentioned.
- **Reporting Required:** State attorneys and local law enforcement must document training, investigations of missing children, trafficking referrals, trafficking cases referred for prosecution, etc.
- **Enforcement Measures:** Hotels, motels, inns, similar lodging businesses must provide trafficking training and awareness campaigns annually and keep guest transactions and receipts for at least six months.
- **Effective Dates:** Vary from 2010 to 2025.

## Effective Dates of Legislation
- **An Act Concerning Human Trafficking (2017):** February 8, 2025
- **An Act Strengthening Protections for Victims of Human Trafficking (2015):** June 30, 2025
- **An Act Concerning the Department of Children and Families and the Protection of Children (2014):** July 1, 2025
- **An Act Concerning Sexual Exploitation and Trafficking in Persons (2013):** January 1, 2026
- **Commercial Sexual Exploitation of a Minor (2012):** July 1, 2026
- **Notification by the Deparment of Children and Families When a Youth is Arrested for Prostitution (2011):** January 1, 2027
- **An Act Providing a Safe Harbor for Exploited Children (2010):** July 1, 2028

## Enforcement Measures
- Hotels, motels, inns, similar lodging businesses must provide trafficking training and awareness campaigns annually.
- Keeping guest transactions and receipts for at least six months.

These measures aim to prevent and combat human trafficking effectively across various sectors within Connecticut.
