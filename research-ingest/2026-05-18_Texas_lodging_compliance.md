### Overview

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which mandates human trafficking training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. This requirement aims to provide additional opportunities for human trafficking to be reported to law enforcement and improve awareness within the hospitality industry.

### Requirements and Responsibilities

1. **Training Requirement**: Commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms must ensure that their employees receive annual human trafficking training.
2. **Role of Employees**: Employees should be informed that they may not be disciplined, retaliated against, or otherwise discriminated against for making a good faith report of a suspected act of human trafficking.
3. **Contact Information**: Training materials must include contact information for appropriate entities to report human trafficking.
