### Overview of Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals

#### Purpose
The Minnesota sex trafficking prevention training is mandatory for hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff to help prevent and respond to sex trafficking incidents within their establishments. This guide outlines the requirements, content, and how to access the necessary training.

### Who Must Take the Training

- **All owners, managers, and on-site employees** must complete the required training.
- **New employees** must take this training within 90 days of hire.
- **All required staff** must retake this training every year.

### Displaying the Back of House Poster

Hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff are required to display a specific back-of-house poster in a location all staff will see. This poster is essential for maintaining compliance with the law.

### Conducting an Ongoing Awareness Campaign

In addition to completing the training, managers and owners are expected to conduct an ongoing awareness campaign to educate all employees about the signs of sex trafficking and how to respond effectively.

### Training Requirements for Managers and Owners

Managers and owners must complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package. This ensures they understand their responsibilities and can prepare appropriately.

### Employee Training Access

Employees can access the employee training in multiple languages:
- English
- Spanish
- Somali
- Hmong

#### Training Package Components

The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:
- A virtual presentation (video with guided exercises)
- Training Companion Guide (PDF)
- Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)
- Poster set (Back of House required to display)

### Frequently Asked Questions

1. **Do I still need to take the training if I already took another one?**
   - Yes, you must complete the Minnesota-specific training to meet the law’s requirements.

2. **Are resorts, bed and breakfasts, vacation shares, and short-term rentals required to provide the training?**
   - Only those holding a hotel license are required.

3. **Does the law apply to tribal nations?**
   - No, but MDH encourages their participation in providing the training.

4. **Are any employees exempt from the law?**
   - Exemptions include:
     - Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms
     - Employees working exclusively in restaurants or providing catering services without guest contact

5. **What if I'm unsure about my license type?**
   - Contact the Food, Pool, and Lodging section at MDH for clarification.

6. **Does MDH provide financial assistance with training costs?**
   - No, MDH only provides the free training package; printing posters is the responsibility of the licensee.

7. **Are training materials available in other languages?**
   - Training will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

8. **Do managers/owners and employees have to take the training every year?**
   - Yes, it is an annual requirement tied to licensure, with updates provided by November 1 of each year.

9. **Can we replace MDH training with our own materials?**
   - No, but limited parts can be replaced with prior approval from MDH.

10. **If a property owner/operator/manager does not interact with guests, are they required to take the training?**
    - Yes, all managers and owners are required to complete the training.

11. **Does the law exempt seasonal employees?**
    - The law does not explicitly exempt them; they must receive the training within 90 days of starting work.

12. **If an employee is temporary (employed for two months), do they still need to complete the training?**
    - Technically no, but MDH encourages all employees.

13. **Are contracted workers subject to the training?**
    - Yes, all employees must complete the training.

14. **Are whole-house vacation rental properties subject to compliance with the statute?**
    - Yes, as these are typically licensed as hotels/motels by MDH.
