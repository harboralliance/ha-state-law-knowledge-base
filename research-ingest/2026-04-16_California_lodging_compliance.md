### **1. Training Requirement for Hotel and Motel Employees**
According to California Senate Bill 970, effective January 1, 2020, hotel and motel employers are required to provide at least 20 minutes of interactive training on human trafficking awareness to employees who interact with or may come into contact with trafficking victims. This includes new hires within six months of employment.

- **Effective Date:** January 1, 2020
- **Required Training Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Type of Training:** Interactive and classroom-based
- **Target Employees:** Those who interact with or may come into contact with trafficking victims

### **2. Posting Requirement for Human Trafficking Hotline Numbers**
As per Senate Bill 1193, businesses required to post a notice informing the public about human trafficking must also include telephone hotline numbers for assistance and reporting unlawful activity. The law applies to hotels, motels, bed and breakfast inns as of April 1, 2013.

- **Effective Date:** April 1, 2013
- **Businesses Affected:** Hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns
- **Required Information:** Telephone hotline numbers for help or reporting unlawful activity

### **3. Model Notice for Posting**
The California Attorney General provides a model notice that businesses are required to post. This notice must be available in English and Spanish, and in additional languages as needed based on local regulations.

- **Languages Required:**

- **Contact Information:**
  - National Human Trafficking Hotline: (888) 373-7888 or TEXT: 233-733
  - California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking: (1-888-KEY-2FRE(EDOM)) or (1-888-539-2373)
