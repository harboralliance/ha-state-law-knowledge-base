Here's a summary of the key information provided about human trafficking training for commercial lodging establishments in Texas:

1. Requirement: The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires annual human trafficking training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms.

2. Training Content: Required topics include:
- Identifying individuals at risk for human trafficking
- Reporting and responding to suspected human trafficking
- Human trafficking signage requirements

3. Training Providers: Approved providers of training courses include the Office of the Attorney General, Etactics, Unbound Now, and others.

4. Course Delivery Methods: Online and in-person options are available from approved providers.

5. Compliance: Employees must complete annual training by their 90th day of employment and retain proof of completion for review by the OAG and law enforcement agencies.

6. Signage Requirements: Approved commercial lodging establishments must display specific human trafficking awareness signage.

7. Cost: The cost varies depending on the provider and course, with some courses being free and others requiring payment.

8. Availability: Training is encouraged for all lodging establishments in Texas, regardless of their room count.

This requirement aims to increase awareness of human trafficking among hospitality workers and provide them with the knowledge and resources needed to identify and report suspicious activity related to human trafficking.
