The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has established requirements for commercial lodging establishments to provide annual human trafficking training to their employees. The training aims to increase awareness and help identify potential victims of human trafficking, particularly within the hospitality industry.

### Key Requirements:

1. **Annual Training Requirement:**
   - Commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms must provide annual human trafficking training.
   - All lodging establishments in Texas are encouraged to offer this training annually, regardless of size.

2. **Training Content:**
   - The training must cover:
     - How and why human trafficking occurs within the hospitality industry.
     - Roles and responsibilities of employees in reporting and responding to human trafficking.
     - Identification of signs and indicators of human trafficking among individuals working in commercial lodging.
     - Contact information for appropriate entities to report human trafficking.

3. **Completion Requirements:**
   - New employees must complete the training not later than 90 days after their hire date.
   - All employees must receive annual training.
   - The approved training provider must provide a certificate of completion to each employee, which must include the date taken and the title of the course.

4. **Record Keeping:**
   - Operators of commercial lodging establishments are required to maintain records showing compliance with Section 114.0051 of the Texas Business and Commerce Code.
   - These records must be available for review and inspection by the OAG, as well as local, state, and federal law enforcement.

### Approved Training:

The OAG maintains a list of approved human trafficking training videos, which are intended to meet the requirements set forth. Some of the approved courses include:

1. **“Human Trafficking Hotel Training”** - Office of the Attorney General
   - Duration: 22 minutes
   - Language: English
   - Cost: Free
   - Delivery Method: Online

2. **“Human Trafficking – Texas”** - Etactics
   - Duration: 26 minutes
   - Language: English
   - Cost: $1/user/month
   - Delivery Method: Online

3. **“Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry”** - Unbound Now (English)
   - Duration: 25 minutes
   - Language: English
   - Cost: Free
   - Delivery Method: Online and in-person (Contact for more information)

4. **“Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry”** - Marriott International
   - Duration: 60 or 80 minutes
   - Language: English
   - Cost: Free (Available to Marriott associates)
   - Delivery Method: Online

5. **"Your Role in Preventing Human Trafficking: Recognize the Signs"** - ECPAT-USA
   - Duration: 30 minutes
   - Language: English and Spanish*
   - Cost: Free
   - Delivery Method: Online (Contact for more information)
