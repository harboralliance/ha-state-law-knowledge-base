### Answering the Question

**Question:** Does human trafficking awareness training requirement apply to employees of motels and hotels?

**Answer:** Yes, based on Source 2, Senate Bill 970 requires hotel and motel employers to provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training and education regarding human trafficking awareness to each employee who is likely to interact or come into contact with victims of human trafficking. This requirement applies to employees employed as of July 1, 2019, and new employees within six months of their employment in that role.

### Additional Information

**Effective Date:** The training requirement under SB 970 is effective by January 1, 2020.

**Exceptions:** Employers who have provided this training on or before January 1, 2019, are not required to provide additional training.

**Model Notice Requirement:** Hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns must post a model notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking about telephone hotline numbers to seek help or report unlawful activity. This requirement also applies to certain counties requiring third language postings under federal Voting Rights Act provisions.

### Conclusion

The training requirement for employees of motels and hotels is a legal mandate established by SB 970, ensuring that these employees are informed about human trafficking and can better protect victims.
