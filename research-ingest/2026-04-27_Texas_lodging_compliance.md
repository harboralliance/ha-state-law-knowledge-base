**Overview of Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging in Texas**

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for the hospitality industry. This bill aims to provide additional opportunities for human trafficking to be reported to law enforcement by requiring human trafficking training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. The annual training requirement applies only to commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, but it is encouraged for all lodging establishments in the state regardless of size.

### Key Requirements for Training
1. **Content**: The training must cover identifying and responding to human trafficking within the hospitality industry.
2. **Frequency**: All employees must be trained annually.
3. **Documentation**: Operators must retain a copy of each annual certificate of completion for every employee who completes the training.
4. **Employee Rights**: Employees "may not be disciplined, retaliated against, or otherwise discriminated against for making a good faith report of a suspected act of human trafficking."

### Approving Training Providers
Only approved training providers can offer human trafficking training videos to commercial lodging establishments in Texas. The OAG must approve each provider before they can be listed on the Office's website.
