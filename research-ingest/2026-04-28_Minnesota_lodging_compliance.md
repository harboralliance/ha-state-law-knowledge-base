Based on the information provided in both the legal text (Minnesota Statute) and the governmental training materials, here are the key points regarding sex trafficking prevention training for hotel/motel/short-term rental staff:

### Legal Requirements:
1. **Who Must Take Training:**
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees of hotels, motels, and short-term rentals must complete the required training.
   - There are limited exemptions, including employees under 16 who clean rooms, those working exclusively in restaurants providing catering services (with no direct guest contact), and staff with no direct guest contact.

2. **Training Schedule:**
   - New employees must take the training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake the training every year.

3. **Display Requirements:**
   - A back-of-house poster is required to be displayed in a location all staff will see.

4. **Awareness Campaign:**
   - Conducting an ongoing awareness campaign for employees is mandatory.

### Training Package:
1. **Content:**
   - The training includes:
     - A virtual presentation (video with guided exercises)
     - A Training Companion Guide (PDF) as a resource
     - A Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF) to comply with the law
     - A poster set, including a back-of-house poster

2. **Duration:**
   - The training takes approximately 45 to 50 minutes to complete.

3. **Language Availability:**
   - The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package is available in English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

### Compliance Verification:
1. **Roster Requirement:**
   - After completing the training, hotels/motels should fill out a roster of names for all employees who completed the training along with the date of completion.
   - This roster should be kept on file for the next inspection and does not need to be sent to MDH.

### Additional Information:
- **Exceptions:** The law applies only to those holding a hotel license, so resorts, bed and breakfasts, vacation shares, and short-term rentals are exempt unless they hold a hotel license.
- **Tribal Nations:** While the law does not apply to tribal nations, MDH encourages them to provide training and use MDH materials.
- **Cost:** The training package is provided free of charge. Licensees are responsible for any additional costs such as printing posters.

This comprehensive approach ensures that staff in the lodging industry are equipped with the knowledge and resources necessary to recognize and respond to instances of sex trafficking, thereby preventing its occurrence within their establishments.
