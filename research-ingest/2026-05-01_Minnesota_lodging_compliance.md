### Overview

The Minnesota Sex Trafficking Prevention Training requirements are designed to help hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff recognize the signs of sex trafficking and know how to respond effectively. This training is essential for preventing sex trafficking within these establishments.

### Key Points

1. **Training Requirements**:
   - All hotel, motel, and short-term rental owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
   - New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake this training every year.

2. **Displaying the Poster**:
   - Hotels must display a specific back-of-house poster that is part of the training package. This poster should be placed in a location where all staff can see it.

3. **Ongoing Awareness Campaigns**:
   - Hotels are required to conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees regarding sex trafficking prevention and response.

4. **Training Package Components**:
   - The training includes a virtual presentation, a Training Companion Guide, a Manager & Owner Training Toolkit, and a poster set.
   - Employees are required to complete the 45-50 minute virtual presentation.

### Exceptions

The law provides certain exceptions for who must receive the training, such as:

- Employees under 16 years old (unless they clean rooms)
- Employees working exclusively in restaurants or providing catering services (with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms)

However, these exemptions do not apply to owners, operators, and managers of hotels.

### Implementation Steps

1. **Completion**:
   - All required employees must complete the training.
   - A roster of all completed trainings should be kept on file.

2. **Displaying the Poster**:
   - The back-of-house poster must be displayed in a visible location within the establishment.

3. **Conducting Awareness Campaigns**:
   - Regular campaigns for employee awareness are necessary to ensure ongoing vigilance and education.

### Resources

- **Minnesota Department of Health**: Offers detailed information on the training requirements, compliance, and additional resources.
- **Hospitality Minnesota**: A partnership between MDH and Hospitality Minnesota provides comprehensive support and materials.

By following these guidelines, hotels, motels, and short-term rentals can significantly reduce the risk of sex trafficking in their establishments and contribute to a safer environment for all.
