### Summary

**1. Training Requirement for Hotel and Motel Employees:**
   - **Source:** SOURCE 1 (SB 970) and SOURCE 2 (California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking)
   - **Content:** Hotel and motel employers in California must provide at least 20 minutes of training on human trafficking awareness to employees who are likely to interact with victims of trafficking. This requirement applies to employees hired as of July 1, 2019, and new employees within six months of their hire date.
   - **Timeline:** The training must be completed by January 1, 2020.

**2. Signage Requirement for Hotels, Motels, and Bed and Breakfast Inns:**
   - **Source:** SOURCE 3 (Human Trafficking Model Notice)
   - **Content:** These businesses are required to post a notice that includes hotline numbers for seeking help or reporting unlawful activity related to human trafficking.
   - **Languages:** The notices must be posted in English, Spanish, and any additional language mandated by federal law for specific counties.

**3. Enforcement:**
   - **Source:** SOURCE 3 (Human Trafficking Model Notice)
   - **Content:** Failure to post the required notice can result in fines. The mandated notices are available for download on the website provided in the source.

### Details

- **Training Content:**
  - **Purpose:** The training aims to educate employees about human trafficking and equip them with knowledge to identify and respond to potential victims.
  - **Content:** Topics may include recognizing signs of human trafficking, knowing how to report suspicions, understanding their legal obligations, and providing basic support.

- **Signage Requirements:**
  - **What is Required:** Posting of a notice that provides hotline numbers for reporting human trafficking incidents.
  - **Who Needs It:** Applies to hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns.
  - **Languages:** The notices must be posted in English, Spanish, and additional languages as required by federal law.

- **Enforcement:**
  - **Penalties:** Failure to post the mandated notice can lead to fines or other penalties.
  - **Compliance:** Each business is responsible for ensuring that the notice is posted in a visible location within their establishment.

  - California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST): 1-888-KEY-2FRE(EDOM) or 1-888-539-2373

These resources provide further assistance and support for businesses and individuals dealing with human trafficking issues.
