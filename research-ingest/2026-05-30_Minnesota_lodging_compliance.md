### Overview

The training package for hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota is designed to help staff recognize the signs of sex trafficking and understand how to respond effectively. This training is mandatory under state law and applies to all facilities holding a hotel license, regardless of whether they are resorts, bed and breakfasts, vacation shares, or other lodging types.

### Key Components

1. **Virtual Presentation**: A video with guided exercises.
2. **Training Companion Guide (PDF)**: Resource for viewing the virtual presentation.
3. **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)**: Includes instructions for managers and owners to comply with the law.
4. **Poster Set**:
   - Back of House (required to display)
   - Housekeeping
   - Restaurant/Bar
   - Front desk/lobby
   - Security

### Requirements

- **Completion**: All on-site employees, including managers, must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
- **Renewal**: Annual refresher training for all required staff.
- **Display**: The "Back of House" poster must be displayed in a location where all staff can see it.
- **Awareness Campaign**: Conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees.

### Training Compliance

1. **Managers and Owners**:
   - Access the training through the MDH Learning Center.
   - Complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package.

2. **Employee Training**:
   - Available in multiple languages (English, Spanish, Somali, Hmong).

### Exceptions to Training Requirements

- Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms.
- Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant providing catering services.
- No direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

### Additional Information

- **Exemptions for Tribes**: While the law does not apply to tribal nations, MDH encourages them to provide training and use MDH materials.
- **Contact Information**: For questions or assistance accessing the training, contact `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.
- **Fees**: There is no charge for the mandatory training package.
- **Language Availability**: Training materials will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

### Compliance Verification

Once training is completed, facilities should:

- Fill out a roster of names for all employees who completed the training, along with the date of completion.
- Keep this roster on file for their next inspection. There is no need to send the roster to MDH for compliance verification.

For any further questions or concerns, contact `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.
