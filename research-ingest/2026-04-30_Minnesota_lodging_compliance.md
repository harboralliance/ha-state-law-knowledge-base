### Overview of Minnesota Law on Sex Trafficking Prevention for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals

#### **Legislation:**
The legislation governing sex trafficking prevention training in hotels, motels, and short-term rentals is found at Minn. Stat. § 268C.405.

#### **Training Requirements:**
1. **Who Must Take the Training:**
   - All hotel, motel, and short-term rental owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the training.
   - Exceptions include:
     - Employees under 16 years old unless they are involved in room cleaning.
     - Employees working exclusively in a restaurant or providing catering services (if there is no direct contact with guests).
     - Employees without any direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

2. **Timing of Training:**
   - New employees must complete the training within 90 days of their hire date.
   - All required staff members must retake the training every year.

3. **Displaying Posters:**
   - Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals must display a specific poster in all areas where employees work (e.g., back-of-house, housekeeping, restaurant/bar, front desk/lobby, security).

4. **Ongoing Awareness Campaigns:**
   - Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals must conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for their employees.

5. **Training Package Components:**
   - A virtual presentation (video with guided exercises).
   - Training Companion Guide (PDF) as a resource for viewing the virtual presentation.
   - Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF) to assist in compliance with the law.
   - A poster set, including a back-of-house poster required to be displayed.

#### **Compliance and Enforcement:**
- Non-compliance with these requirements can lead to potential penalties, although specific details of penalties are not provided in Minn. Stat. § 268C.405.

### Training Details

#### **Training Content:**
The training focuses on recognizing the signs of sex trafficking and understanding how to respond effectively. It covers:
- The nature of sex trafficking.
- Risks associated with being employed in a hotel, motel, or short-term rental.
- How to identify potential victims of sex trafficking.
- What actions to take if an employee suspects or witnesses suspicious activity.

#### **Accessibility:**
The training materials are available in multiple languages, including English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong. However, the Minnesota-specific aspects of the MDH training must be delivered to employees.

### Accessing Training

#### **For Managers/Owners:**
- To access the training package, managers and owners will need to complete a brief pre-training survey through the MN Department of Health (MDH) Learning Center.

#### **For Employees:**
- Employee training is available in multiple languages. You can find links to the employee training materials for English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong on the MDH website.

### Frequently Asked Questions

- **Q: Do I still need to take this training if I already took a different sex trafficking prevention course?**
  - A: Yes, you must complete the Minnesota-specific lodging sex trafficking prevention and response training package.

- **Q: Are resorts, bed and breakfasts, vacation shares, short-term rentals required to provide the training?**
  - A: Only those who hold a hotel license are required to provide the training.
