The training requirements for preventing and responding to sex trafficking in hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota are detailed below:

1. **Who Must Take the Training:**
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
   - Exemptions include employees under 16 unless they clean rooms, those working exclusively in a restaurant providing catering services or both, and those with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

2. **Timing of Training:**
   - New employees must take the training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake this training every year.

3. **Training Content:**
   - The training covers recognizing signs of sex trafficking and how to respond appropriately.
   - It is delivered through a virtual presentation with guided exercises, a Training Companion Guide (PDF), and the Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF).

4. **Display Requirements:**
   - Owners must display the required back-of-house poster in a location all staff will see it.

5. **Ongoing Awareness Campaign:**
   - Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals should conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees to reinforce training content.

6. **Language Availability:**
   - The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package is available in English, Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

7. **Compliance Verification:**
   - Completing the training includes filling out a roster of names for all employees who completed the training along with the date of completion.
   - This roster should be kept on file for future inspections, as there is no need to send it to MDH to check compliance.

These requirements are in line with Minnesota law and are part of efforts to prevent sex trafficking within hotel, motel, and short-term rental facilities.
