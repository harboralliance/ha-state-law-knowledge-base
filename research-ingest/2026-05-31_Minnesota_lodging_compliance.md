Based on the information provided in both the Minnesota Statutes and the Minnesota Department of Health's guidelines, here are key points about sex trafficking prevention training for hotels, motels, and short-term rentals:

### Legal Requirements:
- **All Owners, Managers, and On-Site Employees Must Complete Training:** This includes hotel staff who do not typically interact with guests (e.g., security).
- **New Employees Must Take Training Within 90 Days of Hire.**
- **Training Must Be Retaken Every Year.**

### Training Content and Format:
- **Virtual Presentation:** A video with guided exercises.
- **Training Companion Guide:** Provides resources for viewing the virtual presentation.
- **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit:** Includes instructions on compliance requirements.
- **Poster Set:** Including a required back-of-house poster.

### Displaying the Poster:
- The back-of-house poster is mandatory and must be displayed in a location where all staff will see it.

### Exceptions to Training Requirements:
- **Employees Under 16:** Unless they clean rooms.
- **Employees Working Exclusively in Restaurants/Catering Services:** If there's no direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

### Compliance:
- Licensees are required to pay the costs of compliance. MDH does not provide assistance with printing posters or other materials.

### Other Languages:
- Training materials will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

### Annual Updates:
- The training package will be updated by November 1 of each year to reflect any changes.

### Additional Requirements for Owners/Operators/Managers:
- Even if they do not have direct contact with guests, owners, operators, and managers must still take the training.

### Contact Information:
For questions or assistance accessing the training, contact health.safeharbor@state.mn.us.

This comprehensive approach ensures that staff in these establishments are equipped to identify and respond effectively to instances of sex trafficking.
