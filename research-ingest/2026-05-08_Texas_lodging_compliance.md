## Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging Providers in Texas

### Overview:
Effective human trafficking training is crucial for commercial lodging providers to help identify, prevent, and respond to potential incidents of human trafficking. The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390 (87th Regular Session) to require such training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms. This requirement also encourages training for all lodging establishments in the state.

### Training Requirements:
- **Annual Requirement:** Employees of commercial lodging establishments must receive annual human trafficking training.
- **Content:** The training must cover identifying red flags, reporting procedures, and the importance of not retaliating against employees who report suspected human trafficking incidents.
- **Certificate:** Training providers must issue a certificate to each employee upon completion.

### Approval Process:
Training materials must be approved by the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) before they can be considered for approval. Approved training videos are available on the OAG's website.
