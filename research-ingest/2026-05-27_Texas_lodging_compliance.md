### Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging in Texas

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390 (87th Regular Session) to require annual human trafficking training for workers directly employed by commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms. This law aims to provide additional opportunities for human trafficking to be reported to law enforcement.

#### Key Requirements:
- Annual training requirement for lodging establishments with at least 10 rooms.
- Training is encouraged for all lodging establishments regardless of size.
- Training must include information on the role of commercial lodging in addressing sex trafficking, hotel contract workers vulnerable to labor trafficking, and staff roles in reporting and responding to human trafficking.

#### Approving Authority:
The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) is responsible for approving training programs that meet these requirements. The OAG approves videos and other training materials that are submitted by various organizations.
