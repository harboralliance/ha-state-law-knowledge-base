## Summary of Requirements for Sex Trafficking Prevention Training in Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals

### Legal Requirement
The training is mandated by **Minnesota law** (specifically, Minnesota Statutes, section 169A.32). This applies to all hotels, motels, and short-term rentals holding a hotel license.

### Key Requirements for Training Compliance:
1. **Who Must Complete the Training:**
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete it.
   - Exceptions include employees under 16 who clean rooms or work exclusively in restaurants providing catering services without direct guest contact.

2. **Timing:**
   - New employees must complete the training within **90 days of hire**.
   - All required staff must retake the training annually.

3. **Training Content:**
   - The training covers identifying the signs of sex trafficking and knowing how to respond to it.
   - It includes a virtual presentation, a training companion guide, a manager/owner toolkit, and a set of posters (back of house required).

4. **Displaying the Poster:**
   - A specific back-of-house poster must be displayed in all areas of the hotel/motel where staff can see it regularly.

5. **Conducting Awareness Campaigns:**
   - Ongoing awareness campaigns for employees are required to reinforce learning.

### Accessing and Completing Training
1. **Managers/Owners Training:**
   - They can access the training through the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) Learning Center.
   - A brief pre-training survey is required before accessing the training package.

2. **Employee Training:**
   - Employees can complete the training in their preferred language (English, Spanish, Somali, Hmong).
   - The training is available at no cost from MDH.

### Compliance and Reporting
1. **Completion Records:**
   - Managers/owners must keep a roster of employees who completed the training along with the dates of completion.
   - This roster should be kept on file for any inspections.

2. **No Obligation to Report Compliance to MDH:**
   - There is no requirement to send this roster to MDH for compliance verification.

### Additional Information
- The law does not explicitly exempt tribal nations, but MDH encourages them to provide the training.
- Training materials will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.
- The Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) does not provide financial assistance for compliance beyond offering the free training package.

### FAQs
1. **Who is Required to Complete the Training?**
   - All hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff must complete it, with specific exemptions for certain employee categories.

2. **What if I’m Not in the Lodging Industry?**
   - Non-lodging industry professionals can request additional training resources by contacting `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.

3. **Are Tribes Exempt?**
   - No; however, MDH encourages tribal nations to provide the training.

4. **What Languages are Available?**
   - The training is available in English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

5. **Is There a Fee for Training Materials?**
   - No, the training materials and package are provided at no cost by MDH.

6. **Do Managers/Owners Need to Take the Training Every Year?**
   - Yes, it is an annual requirement tied to licensure.

### Conclusion
The Minnesota law mandates comprehensive sex trafficking prevention training for hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff to ensure they are equipped to identify and respond to potential incidents of trafficking. Compliance includes mandatory training completion, ongoing awareness campaigns, and displaying specific posters. Training materials and support are available free from MDH, with a focus on inclusivity in multiple languages to reach diverse communities effectively.
