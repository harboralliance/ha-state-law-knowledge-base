### Summary

#### Key Points from Sources:

1. **Training Requirement**:
   - **SB 970**: Requires hotel and motel employers to provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness.
   - Training must be given by Jan 1, 2020, to employees employed as of July 1, 2019.
   - New employees likely to interact with victims must receive training within six months.

2. **Posting Requirement**:
   - **Civil Code Section 52.6**: Specifies businesses required to post a notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking of telephone hotline numbers.
   - Hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns are included in the list of specified businesses.
   - Notices must be posted in English, Spanish, and an additional language if applicable.

3. **Penalties**:
   - Failure to post or provide the required notice leads to penalties under the law.

4. **Hotline Numbers**:
   - National Human Trafficking Hotline: 1-888-373-7888 or TEXT: Be Free (233733)

### Detailed Analysis:

#### Training Requirement (SB 970):
The requirement for hotel and motel employees to receive training on human trafficking awareness is a significant step towards preventing exploitation. The law aims to educate staff who are likely to interact with potential victims, thereby enhancing their ability to recognize signs of trafficking and respond appropriately. Failure to comply could result in penalties, ensuring accountability.

#### Posting Requirement (Civil Code Section 52.6):
Posting hotline numbers for reporting human trafficking is another critical measure. It ensures that the public is aware of resources available if they suspect or are victims of trafficking. The requirement to post notices in multiple languages caters to the diverse population in California, making the information accessible to all.

#### Penalties:
Enforcing penalties for non-compliance with posting and training requirements underscores the seriousness of the issue. It serves as a deterrent for businesses that may otherwise neglect these crucial responsibilities.

#### Hotline Numbers:
Providing clear hotline numbers ensures victims and potential victims can seek immediate assistance. The National Human Trafficking Hotline is available for those in immediate danger or seeking support.

### Conclusion:

The combination of training requirements, posting notices with hotline numbers, and penalties serves as a comprehensive approach to combating human trafficking in California. These measures, along with public awareness campaigns, are crucial steps towards protecting vulnerable individuals and ensuring their safety.
