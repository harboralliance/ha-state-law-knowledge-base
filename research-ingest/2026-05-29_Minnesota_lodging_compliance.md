## Overview

The training on preventing and responding to sex trafficking in hotels, motels, and short-term rentals is a mandatory requirement under Minnesota law for all owners, managers, and on-site employees. The purpose of this training is to educate staff about the signs of sex trafficking and provide them with the necessary knowledge and skills to help prevent and respond to such incidents.

### Training Requirements

1. **All Employees Must Complete the Training**: This includes managers, owners, and any on-site employees.
2. **New Employees**: Must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
3. **Annual Retraining**: All required staff must retake this training every year.
4. **Display the Required Back of House Poster**: The poster set is mandatory for all hotels, motels, and short-term rentals.
5. **Conduct an Ongoing Awareness Campaign**: While part of the package, it's recommended but not explicitly mandatory.

### Training Package Components

The training includes:

1. **Virtual Presentation**: A video with guided exercises.
2. **Training Companion Guide (PDF)**: A resource for viewing the virtual presentation.
3. **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)**: Provides instructions on how to comply with the law.
4. **Poster Set**:
   - **Back of House (required to display)**: Must be placed in a location all staff will see it.

### Accessibility and Language

- The training is available in multiple languages, including English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.
- Additional languages may become available in the future.

### Questions and Clarifications

1. **Re-taking Training**: Even if you’ve already taken similar training before, you still need to complete the Minnesota-specific training as it meets the requirements of the law.
2. **Exemptions for Specific Staff**: Employees under 16 (unless they clean rooms), those who work exclusively in restaurants or bars, and those with no direct contact with guests are exempt but MDH encourages all employees to be trained.
3. **License Verification**: If unsure about holding the correct license, contact the Food, Pool, and Lodging section at MDH.

### Compliance Costs

The law states that licensees are responsible for the costs of compliance. While MDH provides the training package for free, they do not assist with additional costs such as printing posters.
