### Key Information Extracted from Sources:

1. **Training Requirement for Hotel and Motel Employees:**
   - **SB 970 (California State Bill 970)**
     - Effective January 1, 2020.
     - Requires hotel and motel employers to provide at least 20 minutes of training on human trafficking awareness to employees who are likely to interact with victims of trafficking.
     - Training must be provided within six months for new hires in the same role.
     - Employees already trained by January 1, 2019, are not required to undergo additional training.

2. **Human Trafficking Hotline:**
   - National Human Trafficking Resource Center: 
     - Phone: 1-888-373-7888
     - Text: "Be Free" or 233733
   - California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST):
     - Phone: 1-888-KEY-2FRE(EDOM) or 1-888-539-2373

3. **Civil Remedies for Survivors of Human Trafficking:**
   - Survivors may be eligible for civil remedies through California's human trafficking law.

4. **Model Notice Requirement:**
   - Specified businesses (including hotels and motels) must post a notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking of telephone hotline numbers to seek help or report unlawful activity.
   - The notice must be posted in English and Spanish, and may require posting in additional languages in certain counties.

5. **Enforcement:**
   - Failure to comply with posting requirements can result in penalties as specified by state law (Senate Bill 1193).

### Summary:
The California State Bill 970 mandates that hotel and motel employees receive training on human trafficking awareness within a year of their employment or re-employment. This training is aimed at preventing exploitation and assisting victims. Additionally, businesses must post notices with hotline numbers for reporting human trafficking, ensuring accessibility to resources in multiple languages where required by law.

This initiative underscores California's commitment to combating human trafficking and supporting survivors through comprehensive legislation and public awareness efforts.
