Here's a summary of key points from the approved human trafficking training resources for commercial lodging establishments in Texas:

1. Training requirements:
- Commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms must provide annual human trafficking training to all employees.
- Smaller establishments are encouraged but not required.
- New hires must complete training within 90 days of hire.

2. Training content:
- Explains how and why human trafficking occurs in the hospitality industry
- Discusses commercial lodging's role in addressing sex trafficking
- Identifies vulnerable hotel contract workers 
- Guides staff on reporting and responding to human trafficking

3. Certification:
- Approved training providers must provide a certificate of completion for each employee.
- Certificates must include completion date and course title.

4. Training delivery:
- Online, in-person or self-paced via websites like National Course Portal
- Providers must ensure employees can verify their certification online

5. Reporting:
- Trainees should know to call 911 in emergencies
- Contact iWatchTX for suspicious activity related to trafficking
- Report to National Human Trafficking Hotline (888-373-7888) or text 233733

6. Signage requirements:
- Commercial lodging establishments must display human trafficking signage

7. Compliance records:
- Operators must retain certificates and maintain compliance records for inspection by OAG and law enforcement

This training aims to educate hospitality workers about human trafficking risks and provide them with the tools to report suspected cases and protect vulnerable workers.
