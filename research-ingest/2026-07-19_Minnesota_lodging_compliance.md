The provided text outlines a mandatory training program for hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff in Minnesota to prevent and respond to human trafficking and labor trafficking within their establishments. Here's a structured summary:

### Training Requirements:
- **Who Must Take the Training:**
  - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete it.
  - New employees must complete within 90 days of hire.
  - All required staff must retake annually.

- **Posting Requirement:**
  - Display the back-of-house poster that is required to be displayed in locations where all staff will see it.

- **Awareness Campaign:**
  - Conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees.

### Training Package:
- **Components:**
  - Virtual Presentation (video with guided exercises)
  - Training Companion Guide
  - Manager & Owner Training Toolkit
  - Poster Set

### Training Overview:
- **Duration:** The training takes approximately 45 to 50 minutes.
- **Multilingual Availability:** Available in English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.

### About the Training Package:
- **Development:** Developed by the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) in partnership with Hospitality Minnesota.
- **Access:** Managers and owners can access the training through MDH’s Learning Center. Employees can access it in their preferred language.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):
1. **Repetition Required:** Yes, managers and owners must complete the training annually, as well as all employees who have not done so already.
2. **Exceptions:** Certain groups, such as employees under 16, those working exclusively in restaurants or bars without direct guest contact, and seasonal employees (with specific conditions), are exempt but highly encouraged to take the training.
3. **Costs:** The law does not require MDH to cover any costs of compliance, including printing posters.
4. **Language Availability:** Additional languages (Spanish, Hmong, Somali) will be available in late fall 2023/early 2024.

### Compliance Verification:
- **Documentation:** After completing the training, managers and owners must maintain a roster of employees who completed the training along with the date of completion.
- **No Reporting Requirement:** MDH does not require submitting this roster to verify compliance.

### Contact Information:
For any questions or assistance accessing the training, contact `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.

### Additional Resources:
- Minnesota Human Trafficking Prevention and Response Network (MNHTPRN)
- Reports related to human trafficking

This training is crucial for all hospitality industry professionals in Minnesota to help prevent human trafficking and ensure a safer environment for guests.
