## Summary
Connecticut has a comprehensive set of laws and training programs aimed at addressing human trafficking. The state requires various groups, including DCF staff, foster parents, service providers, schools, law enforcement, hospital and medical staff, hospitality industry professionals, and other professionals, to undergo annual basic human trafficking training. Additionally, specialized training is provided for youth, community leaders, and hotels/motels. Laws enacted since 2010 include protections for victims, penalties for offenders, and requirements for data reporting.
