**Texas Human Trafficking Training Requirements and Resources**

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for the hospitality industry. This training is aimed at providing workers in commercial lodging establishments with the knowledge to recognize, report, and respond to human trafficking.

### Key Points from House Bill 390:
- **Requirement**: Workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments must receive annual human trafficking training.
- **Applicability**: Applies only to lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, but is encouraged for all lodging establishments in the state.

### Training Approvals and Resources:
The OAG has approved several human trafficking training videos and resources that meet these requirements. These include both free and paid options available online or in-person.

#### Free Training Videos:
- **Human Trafficking Hotel Training** (Etactics)
  - Duration: 22 minutes
  - Language: English
  - Delivery Method: Online

- **Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry** (Unbound Now)
  - Duration: 25 minutes (English) / 25 minutes (Spanish)
  - Languages: English, Spanish
  - Delivery Method: Online and in-person (Contact for more information)

#### Paid Training Videos:
- **Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training** (Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking)
  - Duration: 45-60 minutes
  - Language: English
  - Cost: $25.95
  - Delivery Method: Online and in-person

- **Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry** (Marriott International)
  - Duration: 60 or 80 minutes
  - Language: English
  - Cost: Free (Available to Marriott associates)

- **Your Role in Preventing Human Trafficking: Recognize the Signs** (ECPAT-USA)
  - Duration: 30 minutes
  - Languages: English, Spanish*
  - Cost: Free

- **Survivor Services/HT101** (Refugee Services of Texas)
  - Duration: 60 minutes
  - Language: English
  - Delivery Method: In-person

- **Human Trafficking Essentials: Texas Hospitality Awareness HB 390** (PBJ Learning)
  - Duration: 26 minutes
  - Language: English
  - Cost: $24.99
  - Delivery Method: Online

- **Combating Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry** (Compliance Learning Solutions)
  - Duration: 45 minutes
  - Language: English
  - Cost: $25.95
