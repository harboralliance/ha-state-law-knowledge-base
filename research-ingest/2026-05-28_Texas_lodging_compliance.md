The provided information details the requirements and available resources for commercial lodging establishments in Texas to comply with House Bill 390, which mandates annual human trafficking training for employees at certain establishments. Here's a breakdown of key points:

### Requirements:
1. **Training Content**: The training must cover various aspects of human trafficking, including its definition, how it occurs within the hospitality industry, the role of hotel staff in reporting and responding to trafficking incidents, and the importance of not discriminating against employees who report suspected acts of trafficking.
2. **Frequency**: The training is mandatory for all employees annually.
3. **Timing**: New employees must complete the training within 90 days of their hire date.
4. **Certification**: Training providers must issue a certificate upon completion, which must include the employee's name, the course title, and the date of completion.

### Signage:
- Commercial lodging establishments are required to display specific human trafficking signage in public areas.

### Reporting:
- Employees must be provided with contact information for reporting suspected acts of human trafficking, including the National Human Trafficking Hotline, local law enforcement agencies, and a telephone number designated by the attorney general.
- In emergencies, employees should call 911.

### Training Approvals:
- The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) is responsible for approving training programs.
- Approved trainings are listed on the OAG's website and include videos from various organizations like Etactics, National Course Portal, Harbor Alliance, and others.

### Available Training Videos:
- The list includes both free and paid options. Paid courses often come with additional benefits such as employer dashboards and instant verifiable certificates.
- Some trainings are available in English only, while others provide content in Spanish to meet the requirements for bilingual training.

### Contact Information:
For specific course inquiries or general questions about compliance, one can contact the OAG at a designated email address. Detailed information on each training program is provided on the organization's website or via direct contact details given in the listings.

This comprehensive approach ensures that commercial lodging establishments are well-prepared to handle human trafficking incidents and contribute to the safety of their workforce and guests.
