### Overview of Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals

#### Legal Requirement
The sex trafficking prevention training requirement in Minnesota applies to all hotel, motel, and short-term rental owners, managers, and on-site employees. New employees must complete the training within 90 days of hire, while existing staff must retake it annually.

### Training Content and Format
The training is designed to equip staff with knowledge about recognizing signs of sex trafficking and understanding how to respond effectively. The training package includes:
1. **Virtual Presentation**: A video with guided exercises.
2. **Training Companion Guide (PDF)**: As a resource for viewing the virtual presentation.
3. **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)**: Guidance on complying with the law.
4. **Poster Set**: 
   - Back of House (required to display)
   - Housekeeping
   - Restaurant/Bar
   - Front desk/lobby
   - Security

The training is available in English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.

### Training Compliance
- All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
- New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
- All required staff must retake this training every year.
- Display the required back of house poster. Place the poster in a location all staff will see it.
- Conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees.

### Exceptions
- Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms.
- Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant, providing catering services, or both (no direct contact with guests or guest rooms).

### Additional Resources and Support
- **MDH Learning Center**: For managers and owners to complete the training.
- **MDH Safe Harbor Program**: Contact for questions or assistance accessing the training.
- **MDH Food, Pool, and Lodging Section**: For inquiries regarding licenses.

### FAQs
1. **Do I need to take this training if I already took sex trafficking training?**
   - Yes, you still need to complete the Minnesota-specific lodging training.

2. **Are resorts, bed and breakfasts, vacation shares, short-term rentals required to provide the training?**
   - Only if they hold a hotel license.

3. **Does the law apply to tribal nations?**
   - No; however, MDH encourages it.

4. **Can we replace the MDH training with our own training materials?**
   - No, it can't be replaced entirely. Exceptions may be made under prior review and approval by MDH.

5. **If a hotel, motel, or short-term rental owner does not have direct contact with guests, are they required to take the training?**
   - Yes, they still need to take the training.
