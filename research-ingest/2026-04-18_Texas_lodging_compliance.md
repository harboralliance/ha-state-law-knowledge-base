The Office of the Attorney General in Texas has implemented a requirement for certain commercial lodging establishments to provide human trafficking training to their employees. This is part of an effort to increase awareness and reporting of potential cases of human trafficking.

### Key Requirements:

1. **Training Content**: The training must cover various aspects of identifying and responding to human trafficking, including red flag indicators, the role of hotel staff in reporting trafficking, and information on the required human trafficking signage.

2. **Frequency of Training**: Employees must be trained annually, and new employees must complete the training within 90 days of being hired.

3. **Certification**: The approved training provider must issue a certificate to each employee who completes the training, which must include the date completed and the course title.

4. **Records Retention**: Operators of commercial lodging establishments must maintain records of all annual training certificates for review by the Office of the Attorney General, local, state, and federal law enforcement agencies.

### Approved Training:

The Office has approved several video training programs that meet these requirements. These include:

- **Etactics' "Human Trafficking – Texas"**: 26 minutes, English language, free, online.
- **Unbound Now's "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry" (English)**: 25 minutes, free, online and in-person.
- **Unbound Now's "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry" (Spanish)**: 25 minutes, free, online and in-person.
- **Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training**: 45 minutes - 1 hour, English language, $25.95, online and in-person.
- **Extended Stay America's "Human Trafficking Training"**: 30 minutes, English language, free, online via internal website only accessible to ESA associates.
- **Marriott International's "Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry"**: 60 or 80 minutes, English language, free, available to Marriott associates (contact for more information).
- **ECPAT-USA's "Your Role in Preventing Human Trafficking: Recognize the Signs"**: 30 minutes, English and Spanish, free, online.
- **Refugee Services of Texas' "Survivor Services/HT101"**: 60 minutes, English language, free, in-person (contact for more information).
- **PBJ Learning's "Human Trafficking Essentials: Texas Hospitality Awareness HB 390"**: 26 minutes, English language, $24.99, online.
- **Compliance Learning Solutions' "Combating Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry"**: 45 minutes, English language, $25.95, online.

### How to Obtain Training:

For more information on these training programs or to find other approved courses, individuals can contact the Office of the Attorney General directly. For course-specific questions, it's recommended to contact the training organization offering the course.

### Conclusion:

The Texas Attorney General's initiative to require human trafficking training for hospitality industry employees is a significant step in combating human trafficking within commercial lodging establishments. The availability of approved training videos and resources makes it easier for establishments to comply with these requirements and enhance their ability to identify and respond to potential cases of human trafficking.
