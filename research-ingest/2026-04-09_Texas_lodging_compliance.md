The Texas Attorney General's Office has established requirements for commercial lodging establishments to provide annual human trafficking training to their employees. This is in compliance with House Bill 390, which was passed in the 87th Regular Session of the Texas Legislature.

Key requirements include:

1. Training must be provided to all new employees not later than 90 days after they are hired.
2. All current and new employees must receive annual human trafficking training.
3. Approved training providers must provide a certificate of completion to each employee who completes the training.
4. Certificates must include the date the course was taken and the title of the course.
5. Operators of commercial lodging establishments must retain copies of all annual certificates for at least three years.
6. Employees "may not be disciplined, retaliated against, or otherwise discriminated against for making a good faith report of a suspected act of human trafficking."

Eligible employees are those directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms. However, the law encourages training for all lodging establishments in Texas, regardless of size.

Approved training providers include:

- Office of the Attorney General
- Etactics
- Unbound Now (English and Spanish versions)
- Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking
- Extended Stay America
- Marriott International
- ECPAT-USA
- Refugee Services of Texas
- PBJ Learning
- Compliance Learning Solutions

These training programs cover topics such as identifying human trafficking, responding to potential incidents, and reporting suspicious activities. Some courses are free, while others require payment.
