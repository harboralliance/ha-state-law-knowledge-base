**Overview of Minnesota's Sex Trafficking Prevention Training Requirement**

Minnesota has enacted laws requiring hotels, motels, and short-term rentals to provide training on sex trafficking prevention and response to their employees. This requirement aims to equip staff with the knowledge to recognize and address instances of sex trafficking, thereby helping prevent these crimes.

### Key Points:

1. **Legal Basis**: The requirement is based on Minnesota Statute § 626B.3455 et seq., which mandates specific training for hotel, motel, and short-term rental owners, managers, and on-site employees.

2. **Training Requirements**:
   - **Owners/Managers and On-Site Employees**: All must complete the required training.
   - **New Employees**: Must take the training within 90 days of hire.
   - **Annual Requirement**: All staff must retake this training every year.

3. **Content and Format**:
   - The training is designed to educate employees on recognizing signs of sex trafficking, understanding their legal obligations, and knowing how to respond appropriately.
   - The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes a virtual presentation, a training companion guide, a manager/owner toolkit, and a set of back-of-house posters.

4. **Display Requirements**:
   - Employers must display the required back-of-house poster in a location where all staff will see it.
   - An ongoing awareness campaign for employees is also required.

5. **Exceptions**:
   - Some employees are exempt from the training, including those under 16 who clean rooms, and those working exclusively in restaurants providing catering services with no direct contact with guests.

6. **Access to Training**:
   - Training can be accessed through MDH’s Learning Center.
   - Language versions (English, Spanish, Somali, Hmong) are available for employee training materials starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

### Compliance and Enforcement:

- **Record Keeping**: Employers must maintain a roster of names for all employees who complete the training, along with the date of completion. This roster should be kept on file.
- **No Fee**: The law does not require employers to pay for the training package; however, they are responsible for the costs associated with printing posters and other materials.
- **MDH Assistance**: MDH provides the free training package but does not assist with additional compliance costs such as printing.

### Conclusion:

The Minnesota sex trafficking prevention training requirement is a crucial step in protecting vulnerable individuals and preventing sex trafficking. By complying with this law, hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff can play a vital role in safeguarding their communities from these crimes.
