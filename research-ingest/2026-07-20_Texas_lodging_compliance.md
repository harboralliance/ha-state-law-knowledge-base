**Approved Human Trafficking Training Videos for Commercial Lodging Establishments**

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for the hospitality industry. This requirement aims to enhance reporting opportunities for human trafficking by mandating annual training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. The training is mandatory for commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms but encouraged for all lodging establishments in Texas.

### Required Training and Compliance
All employees must complete the approved training annually. The certificate of completion must include the date the course was taken and the title of the course.

**Records Retention:**
An operator of a commercial lodging establishment is required to retain a paper or electronic copy of each annual certificate of completion for every employee who completes the training.

**Certification:**
The approved training provider must provide a certificate of completion to every employee who completes the training. These records must be made available for review and inspection at the request of the OAG, local, state, and federal law enforcement agencies.
