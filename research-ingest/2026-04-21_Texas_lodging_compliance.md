## Overview of Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging Providers in Texas

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to develop and approve training programs for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. This training aims to provide additional opportunities for human trafficking to be reported to law enforcement.

### Key Requirements:

1. **Annual Training**: The requirement applies only to commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, but it is encouraged for all lodging establishments in the State of Texas regardless of size.
   
2. **Content**: Training must cover identifying and responding to human trafficking, including signs that an individual may be a victim, reporting procedures, and the role of employees in addressing human trafficking.

3. **Certification**: Employees must receive annual training and complete a certificate of completion, which must include the date taken and the course title.

4. **Employment Protections**: Employees who report suspected acts of human trafficking cannot be disciplined or retaliated against.

### Training Approvals:

The OAG approves specific training programs based on their content and compliance with the requirements outlined in House Bill 390. The following are some approved training videos:

#### Office of the Attorney General:
- **"Human Trafficking Hotel Training"**: Free, online.
- **"Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry"**: Available in English and Spanish.

#### Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking:
- **"Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training"**: $25.95, available online or in-person.

#### Marriott International:
- **"Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry"**: Free, available to Marriott associates upon request.

#### ECPAT-USA:

#### Refugee Services of Texas:

### Reporting Requirements:

Employees must report any suspected acts of human trafficking to appropriate entities, including the National Human Trafficking Hotline, local law enforcement agencies, and a telephone number designated by the attorney general for reporting suspected human trafficking. Employees are encouraged to call 911 in an emergency and iWatchTX if they have information regarding suspicious activity.

### Availability:

Many of these training programs are available online or on-demand. Some may require employees to complete the course within a specific timeframe (e.g., 90 days after hiring). It's important for lodging establishments to review the requirements carefully and ensure compliance with the law.

For more detailed information about any specific training program, interested parties should contact the organization directly using the provided contact details.

### Summary:

The requirement for annual human trafficking training in commercial lodging establishments is designed to enhance employee awareness and encourage reporting of suspected acts. Approved training programs cover key aspects of identifying and responding to human trafficking, providing valuable resources for compliance.
