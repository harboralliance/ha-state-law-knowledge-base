The bill, known as SB1422, aims to address human trafficking by requiring employers in lodging establishments, restaurants, and truck stops to provide training for their employees on recognizing human trafficking and reporting it to the appropriate authorities. The training must be completed within 6 months of starting a job in one of these roles and every 2 years thereafter. The bill also establishes penalties for employers who violate the act, including fines up to $1,500 per offense.
