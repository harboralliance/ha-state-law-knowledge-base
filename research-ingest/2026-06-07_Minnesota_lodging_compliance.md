### Summary of Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals in Minnesota

#### Legal Requirements
1. **Who Must Take the Training:**
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees of hotels, motels, and short-term rentals must complete the training.
   - Exceptions include:
     - Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms.
     - Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant, providing catering services, or both.
     - No direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

2. **Timing:**
   - New employees must take the training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake the training every year.

3. **Display Requirement:**
   - Display the "Back of House" poster in a location all staff will see.

4. **Ongoing Awareness:**
   - Conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees.

#### Training Package Components
The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:
- A virtual presentation (video with guided exercises)
- A Training Companion Guide (PDF)
- A Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)
- A poster set

#### Language Availability
- The training package will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

#### Accessing the Training
- **Managers and Owners:** Access through the MDH Learning Center. They must complete a pre-training survey before accessing the training.
- **Employee Training:** Available in English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
1. **Do I Still Need to Take This Training if I Already Took One?**
   - Yes, you still need to complete the Minnesota-specific training package as it meets the requirements of the Sex Trafficking Prevention Training legislation.

2. **Are Resorts, Bed and Breakfasts, Vacation Shares, Short-Term Rentals Required to Provide the Training?**
   - Only those holding a hotel license are required.

3. **Does the Law Apply to Tribal Nations?**
   - No, but MDH encourages them to provide the training and use MDH materials.

4. **Can We Replace the MDH Training with Our Own Training Materials?**
   - No, it cannot be replaced. In limited situations, a part may be replaced only with prior review and approval by MDH.

5. **What if I'm Not Sure If I Hold the Correct License?**
   - Contact the Food, Pool, and Lodging section at MDH.

6. **Does MDH Assist with Any Costs of Compliance with the Law?**
   - No, aside from providing the free training package, MDH does not assist with printing posters or other costs.

7. **If a Hotel, Motel, or Short-Term Rental Owner, Operator, or Manager Does Not Have Contact with Guests, Are They Required to Take the Training?**
   - Yes, they are still required to take the training. Exemptions do not apply to owners, operators, and managers.

8. **Is the Law Exempt for Seasonal Employees?**
   - The law does not explicitly exempt seasonal employees, so if the licensed facility is covered, employees must receive the training.

9. **If an Employee Is Temporary and Only Employed for Two Months, Do They Still Need to Complete the Training?**
   - Technically no, but MDH encourages the training of all employees.

10. **Are Contracted Workers Subject to the Training?**
    - Yes, they are still required to take the training.

11. **Whole House Vacation Rental Properties:**
    - MDH licenses whole home vacation rentals as hotels/motels in most cases, subjecting them to compliance with the statute.
