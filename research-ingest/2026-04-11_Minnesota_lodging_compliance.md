The information provided in these resources outlines mandatory training requirements for hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff in Minnesota to prevent and respond to sex trafficking. The key points include:

1. **Training Requirements**:
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
   - New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake this training every year.

2. **Content of the Training**:
   - The training covers identifying signs of sex trafficking.
   - It includes a virtual presentation, a training companion guide, and specific toolkits for managers and owners.
   - A back-of-house poster is required to be displayed in all areas where staff are present.

3. **Exemptions**:
   - Employees under 16 who clean rooms.
   - Employees working exclusively in restaurants providing catering services without direct contact with guests or guest rooms.
   - However, these exemptions do not apply to owners, operators, and managers.

4. **Language Availability**:
   - The training materials are available in English, Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

5. **Compliance Costs**:
   - Licensees are required to pay for the costs of compliance, including printing posters.
   - MDH does not provide financial assistance with these costs.

6. **Training Delivery**:
   - Access is through the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) Learning Center.
   - Managers and owners must complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package.

7. **Ongoing Awareness Campaign**:
   - Training staff to conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees is mandatory.

8. **License Verification**:
   - If unsure about licensing, contact the Food, Pool, and Lodging section at MDH.

9. **Annual Requirement**:
   - The training must be completed annually by all employees covered under the law.

10. **Exemptions for Seasonal Employees**:
    - Seasonal employees must receive the training within 90 days of starting work.

11. **Training Materials and Compliance**:
    - After completing training, managers/owners need to fill out a roster of names and dates of completion, which should be kept on file for inspections.

These requirements are designed to ensure that hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff have the necessary knowledge to recognize and respond appropriately to potential incidents of sex trafficking within their establishments.
