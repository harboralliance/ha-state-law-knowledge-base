The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas requires that certain commercial lodging establishments provide annual human trafficking training for their employees. This is done to help workers identify and report instances of human trafficking, which can occur in the hospitality industry.

To comply with this requirement, establishments must either complete an approved course or create their own training program based on OAG-approved materials. The courses must be completed by new employees within 90 days of hire and all employees must receive annual training.

The OAG provides a list of approved human trafficking training videos that establishments can use to fulfill their training requirements. These videos cover topics such as identifying red flags for human trafficking, reporting suspected incidents, and the role of hospitality workers in addressing this issue.

In addition to completing the required training, establishments are also required to display human trafficking signage in their facilities. This signage should include information on how to report suspected instances of human trafficking.

Overall, complying with Texas' human trafficking training requirements is an important step that lodging establishments can take to help prevent and respond to instances of this serious crime within their businesses.
