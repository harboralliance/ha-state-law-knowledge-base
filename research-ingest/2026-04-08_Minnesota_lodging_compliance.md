### Overview of Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals in Minnesota

#### Legal Requirement
The requirement to provide sex trafficking prevention training to hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff is mandated by **Minnesota law**. This legislation aims to protect vulnerable individuals from the dangers of human trafficking.

#### Target Audience
- All owners, managers, and on-site employees who hold a hotel license.
- New employees must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
- All required staff must retake this training annually.

#### Training Components
The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:
- **Virtual Presentation**: A video with guided exercises.
- **Training Companion Guide (PDF)**: Resource for viewing the virtual presentation.
- **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)**: Detailed instructions on complying with the law.
- **Poster Set**:
  - **Back of House (required to display)**
  - Housekeeping
  - Restaurant/Bar
  - Front desk/lobby
  - Security

#### Compliance Requirements
1. **Completion**: All staff must complete the training within the required timeframe.
2. **Displaying the Poster**: The back-of-house poster is mandatory and must be displayed in a location where all staff can see it.
3. **Ongoing Awareness Campaign**: Conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees.

#### Exceptions
- Employees under 16, unless they clean rooms.
- Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant, providing catering services, or both.
- No direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

#### Resources and Support
- **Access to Training**: Available through the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) Learning Center.
- **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**: Address common questions regarding compliance and exceptions.
- **Contact Information**: Reach out to MDH at `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us` for assistance.

#### Benefits
- Helps prevent sex trafficking within the lodging industry.
- Saves lives by educating staff on how to identify and respond to potential victims.
- Compliance with state law, ensuring business operations are legal and ethical.

By adhering to these requirements and participating in comprehensive training, hotels, motels, and short-term rentals can play a crucial role in preventing human trafficking and protecting their communities.
