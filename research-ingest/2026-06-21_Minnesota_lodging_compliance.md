### Overview of Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals

#### Purpose:
To educate hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff about the signs and risks of sex trafficking and provide them with actionable steps to prevent it.

#### Legal Requirement:
Minnesota law mandates that all hotel owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete this training. New hires must take it within 90 days of their start date, and all required staff must retake the training annually.

#### Training Components:

1. **Virtual Presentation:**
   - A video with guided exercises designed to educate participants about sex trafficking.
   
2. **Training Companion Guide (PDF):**
   - Provides resources for viewing the virtual presentation and additional information.
   
3. **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF):**
   - Offers detailed guidance on how to comply with the law, including specific steps managers and owners need to follow.
   
4. **Posters:**
   - A required back of house poster that must be displayed in locations where all staff can see it.

#### Steps to Complete the Training:

1. **Access the Training:**
   - Managers and owners should access the training through the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) Learning Center.
   
2. **Completion Requirement:**
   - Both managers/owners and employees must complete the 45-50 minute virtual presentation.
   
3. **Language Availability:**
   - The training is available in English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.

#### Key Points:

- **Exemptions:** 
  - Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms.
  - Employees working exclusively in restaurants or providing catering services without direct contact with guests.
  
- **Annual Requirement:** 
  - Both managers/owners and employees must complete the training annually, tied to their licensure.

#### Compliance:

- **Display of Poster:**
  - Managers/owners must display the back of house poster in all required locations.

- **Awareness Campaigns:**
  - Conduct ongoing awareness campaigns for all staff involved.

- **Costs:** 
  - The law specifies that licensees are responsible for the costs of compliance, including printing posters. MDH does not provide financial assistance with these costs.

#### Additional Resources:

- For more detailed information and additional resources, visit the [Minnesota Human Trafficking Prevention and Response Network (MNHTPRN)](https://www.health.state.mn.us/mnhtprn/).

This comprehensive training aims to equip hotel staff with the knowledge and tools necessary to prevent sex trafficking within their establishments, ultimately helping to save lives.
