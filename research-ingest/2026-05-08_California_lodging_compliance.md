### Training Requirement and Compliance

**1. Who Needs to be Trained:**
   - Hotel and motel employers must provide training.

**2. What is Required:**
   - At least 20 minutes of classroom or effective interactive training on human trafficking awareness.
   - The training must cover employees who are likely to interact with victims of human trafficking.

**3. Timing:**
   - By January 1, 2020, for all existing employees (those employed as of July 1, 2019).
   - Within six months of employment for new employees.

**4. Exceptions:**
   - Employers who have provided this training to an employee on or before January 1, 2019, do not need to provide additional training.

### Training Content and Methodology

**1. Training Content:**
   - The training must be comprehensive and cover the following aspects:
     - Identification of signs and indicators of human trafficking.
     - Understanding legal protections available for victims of human trafficking.
     - Procedures for reporting suspected human trafficking activities.
     - How to provide assistance, such as directing individuals to appropriate resources.

**2. Training Methodology:**
   - The training can be delivered in various formats:
     - Classroom sessions
     - Online modules
     - Interactive workshops
   - The key is that it must be interactive and engaging to ensure effective learning.

### Reporting and Compliance

**1. Record Keeping:**
   - Employers are required to maintain records of the training provided, including documentation of the date, type of training, and number of employees trained.

**2. Verification:**
   - The Department of Justice (DOJ) can verify compliance through audits and inspections.

**3. Penalties:**
   - Non-compliance with the training requirement is subject to penalties, which may include fines or other sanctions.

### Resources

- **Statewide Language Assistance:** For businesses in specific counties, a third language notice is required.
  - List of covered jurisdictions: [https://www.justice.gov/crt/about-language-minority-voting-rights#coveredjuris](https://www.justice.gov/crt/about-language-minority-voting-rights#coveredjuris)

- **Model Notices:** Available in multiple languages on the DOJ website.
  - English Model Notice, Spanish Model Notice, and dual language versions are provided.

### Conclusion

Hotel and motel employers must comply with the human trafficking awareness training requirement by providing at least 20 minutes of interactive training to employees who interact with potential victims. Non-compliance can result in penalties. The Department of Justice provides resources for compliance and offers model notices in various languages to accommodate businesses operating in multi-lingual environments.
