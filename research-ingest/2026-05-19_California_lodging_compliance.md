## Summary of Findings

### Training Requirement for Hotel and Motel Employees
**Training Requirement:** Yes  
**Enforcement Date:** January 1, 2020  
**Employee Involvement:** Employees likely to interact or come into contact with victims of human trafficking.  
**Specifics:** Employers must provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness. This requirement applies to employees employed as of July 1, 2019, and new hires within six months of employment.

### Posting Requirement for Hotel and Motel Employees
**Posting Requirement:** Yes  
**Enforcement Date:** April 1, 2013  
**Languages Required:** English and Spanish. For businesses in specific counties, a third language is required based on federal Voting Rights Act provisions.  
**Specifics:** Hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns are mandated to post notices informing the public and victims of human trafficking about telephone hotline numbers for help or reporting unlawful activity.

### Penalty for Non-Compliance
**Penalty:** Penalties can include fines or other legal actions as per California law.

### Model Notice Downloadable
A downloadable model notice is available on the Department of Justice website, with options for English, Spanish, and additional languages. The notice must be posted in all required languages.

### Relevant Legislation
- **SB 970:** Effective January 1, 2020. Requires hotel and motel employers to provide training.
- **SB 225 (2017):** Adds text hotline number requirement.
- **AB 260 (2017):** Expands posting requirements to hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns.
