### Summary

#### Legal Requirement for Training
1. **Minnesota Law**: Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are required to provide training on sex trafficking prevention and response.
2. **Who Must Take Training**:
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the training.
   - New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake this training every year.

#### Key Components of Training
1. **Virtual Presentation**: A video with guided exercises.
2. **Training Companion Guide (PDF)**: A resource for viewing the virtual presentation.
3. **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)**: To comply with the law.
4. **Poster Set**:
   - **Back of House (required to display)**: For all staff visibility.
   - **Housekeeping**: Required.
   - **Restaurant/Bar**: Required.
   - **Front desk/lobby**: Required.
   - **Security**: Required.

#### Important Points
- Training must be completed within 90 days for new hires and annually thereafter.
- The training package is available in multiple languages, including Spanish, Hmong, and Somali.
- Employees under 16 (unless they clean rooms), those who work exclusively in restaurants providing catering services, or those with no direct contact with guests are exempt. However, MDH encourages all employees to be trained.

#### Compliance and Cost
1. **Compliance**: Licensees must pay for the costs of compliance.
2. **Costs**: MDH does not provide assistance such as printing posters.

#### Additional Information
- Contact: health.safeharbor@state.mn.us for questions or assistance accessing the training.

### Conclusion

The Minnesota law mandates that hotels, motels, and short-term rentals provide comprehensive sex trafficking prevention and response training to all employees. This requirement ensures staff are equipped with the knowledge necessary to identify and respond appropriately to potential incidents of sex trafficking within their establishments. The training package includes a virtual presentation, guidebooks, and posters, and must be completed annually by all required staff.
