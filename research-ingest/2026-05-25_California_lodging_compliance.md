### Summary

This document is a comprehensive guide on human trafficking in California, providing details on required training for hotel and motel employees, posting requirements for notices, and resources for victims. It also includes model notices that businesses must post to inform the public of available resources.

### Training Requirements
- **Effective Date**: January 1, 2020.
- **Duration**: At least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training.
- **Target Employees**: Employees likely to interact or come into contact with victims of human trafficking.

### Posting Requirements
- **Businesses Subject to Requirement**: Hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns.
- **Languages Required**: English and Spanish.
- **Additional Languages**: Depending on the county, additional languages may be required (e.g., Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, Cambodian, Chinese, etc.).

### Resources for Victims
- **National Human Trafficking Resource Center**: 1-888-373-7888 or TEXT: Be Free or 233733.
- **California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST)**: 1-888-KEY-2FRE(EDOM) or 1-888-539-2373.

### Model Notice
A model notice is provided for download on the California Attorney General's website, which businesses must post in multiple languages to comply with state law.

### Contact Information
- **Office of the Attorney General**: Bilingual Services Program at EERROffice@doj.ca.gov.
- **National Human Trafficking Hotline**: 888-373-7888 or TEXT: Be Free or 233733.

### Legal Compliance
Failure to comply with posting requirements can result in penalties.

### Conclusion

This document provides a detailed overview of the human trafficking laws and regulations in California, emphasizing the importance of training for employees and proper notice posting. Businesses must adhere to these requirements to ensure compliance and support victims effectively.
