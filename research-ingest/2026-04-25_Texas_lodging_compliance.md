The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for the hospitality industry. This annual training requirement only applies to commercial lodging establishments of 10 or more rooms but is encouraged for all lodging establishments in the State of Texas regardless of size.
