### Overview of Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals in Minnesota

#### Legal Requirements
The sex trafficking prevention training for hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff is required by law in Minnesota. This includes all owners, managers, and on-site employees. The law mandates that new employees must complete the training within 90 days of hire, and all required staff must retake it every year.

#### Training Components
The training package includes:
- A virtual presentation: a video with guided exercises.
- A Training Companion Guide (PDF) for viewing the virtual presentation.
- A Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF) to assist in complying with the law.
- A poster set, specifically the back-of-house poster, which is required to be displayed.

#### Key Points of Training
1. **Signs of Sex Trafficking**: Participants will learn how to identify signs that staff might encounter which could indicate a potential victim of sex trafficking.
2. **Response Protocol**: The training will cover what actions employees should take if they suspect or witness any signs of trafficking.
3. **Compliance with the Law**: It ensures that all required aspects of the law are met.

#### Accessing Training
- **Managers and Owners**: They need to complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package via the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) Learning Center.
- **Employees**: Training materials are available in multiple languages including English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong. Employees can access these materials online.

#### FAQs
1. **Do I Need to Retake Training Each Year?**
   - Yes, the training is an annual requirement tied to licensure.

2. **Can We Replace MDH Training with Our Own Materials?**
   - No, it cannot be replaced. Only specific parts may be replaced after obtaining prior review and approval from MDH.

3. **Are Seasonal Employees Required to Take the Training?**
   - Yes, seasonal employees must receive the training within 90 days of starting their employment.

4. **Do All Employees Need to Be Trained?**
   - While exemptions exist for certain employees (e.g., those under 16 not cleaning rooms, restaurant staff without guest contact), MDH encourages training for all employees.

#### Responsibilities
- **Display the Back-of-House Poster**: This poster must be placed in a location where all staff can see it.
- **Conduct an Ongoing Awareness Campaign**: Regularly remind and train staff about the importance of recognizing signs of trafficking and their roles in responding to such situations.

By completing the required training, hotels, motels, and short-term rentals can play a vital role in preventing sex trafficking and protecting potential victims.
