## Summary

### Key Requirements:
1. **Training Requirement**: Hotel and motel employers must provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness to employees who are likely to interact with victims.
2. **Timing**:
   - Initial training: By January 1, 2020, for employees hired as of July 1, 2019.
   - Future training: For new employees within six months of employment in the role.

### Training Content:
The training should cover awareness and knowledge about human trafficking to protect both the business and its employees.

### Exceptions:
- Employers who have already provided this training on or before January 1, 2020 are exempt from providing additional training.

### Legal Compliance:
Failure to comply with these requirements can result in potential legal actions by the California Attorney General's Office.

### Additional Resources:
- **Model Notice**: Available for download at [OAG Website](https://oag.ca.gov/human-trafficking/model-notice).
- **Hotline Numbers**: National Human Trafficking Resource Center: 1-888-373-7888 or TEXT: Be Free or 233733.

### Contact Information:
For any questions, contact the Bilingual Services Program at EERROffice@doj.ca.gov.
