### Summary of Sex Trafficking Prevention Training Requirements for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals in Minnesota

#### Legal Requirement:
Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are required to provide training on preventing and responding to sex trafficking under Minnesota law. This requirement includes:

- **New Employees**: Must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
- **All Staff**: Must retake the training every year.

#### Training Components:
The training package includes:
- **Virtual Presentation**: A video with guided exercises
- **Training Companion Guide (PDF)**: A resource for viewing the virtual presentation
- **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)**: Instructions on complying with the law
- **Poster Set**:
  - Back of House (required to display)
  - Housekeeping
  - Restaurant/Bar
  - Front desk/lobby
  - Security

#### Display Requirements:
Managers and owners must ensure that all staff see the required "Back of House" poster.

#### Awareness Campaign:
Managers and owners need to conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees to keep them informed about sex trafficking prevention.

### Training Content Overview:

The training aims to educate hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff on recognizing signs of sex trafficking and knowing how to respond appropriately. It covers:
- **Understanding Sex Trafficking**: Definition, types, and impacts.
- **Signs of Sex Trafficking**: Common indicators that an individual may be a victim.
- **Responsibilities of Staff**: What to do if they suspect or encounter potential victims.
- **Reporting Procedures**: How to report incidents to the appropriate authorities.

### Training Accessibility:
The training is available in multiple languages, including English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong. It can be accessed through the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) Learning Center.

### Compliance:
- **Poster Display**: Managers and owners must display a "Back of House" poster to ensure staff visibility.
- **Annual Training Requirement**: All required staff must retake the training every year.
- **Record Keeping**: Completed trainings should be documented with rosters showing employee names and dates of completion. These records are required for inspections.

### Exceptions:
The law includes exceptions for certain employees who do not have direct contact with guests, such as:
- Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms
- Employees working exclusively in a restaurant or providing catering services
- Employees without direct contact with guests or guest rooms

However, owners, operators, and managers of hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are still required to complete the training.

### Contact Information:
For any questions related to accessing the training or compliance issues, interested individuals can email health.safeharbor@state.mn.us.
