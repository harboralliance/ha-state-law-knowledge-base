### Overview of Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which mandates annual human trafficking training for employees directly employed by commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms. This requirement aims to increase awareness and enable staff to identify and respond effectively to potential human trafficking situations.

### Requirements
- **Annual Training:** Employees must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
- **Recurring Training:** All employees must be trained annually.
- **Certificate Requirement:** The approved training provider must issue a certificate upon completion, which must include the date taken and course title.
- **Signage:** Commercial lodging establishments are required to display specific human trafficking signage.

### How to Obtain Training
1. **Contact Approved Providers**: Search for training options listed on the Office of the Attorney General's website or contact them directly for more information.
2. **Online Platforms**: Many courses are available online, providing flexibility and convenience.
3. **In-Person Sessions**: Some courses may offer in-person sessions as well.

### Free Training Options
- **Office of the Attorney General**: Offers "Human Trafficking Hotel Training" (22 minutes) for free.
- **Etactics**: Provides "Human Trafficking – Texas" (26 minutes) and "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry" (25 minutes in English; 25 minutes in Spanish) for free, with options for online or in-person delivery.

### Paid Training Options
- **Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking**: Offers "Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training" (45 Minutes - 1 hour) for $25.95.
- **Extended Stay America**: Provides a 30-minute course for free via their internal website, accessible only to ESA associates.
- **Marriott International**: Offers "Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry," available to Marriott associates (60 or 80 minutes for free).
- **ECPAT-USA**: Provides "Your Role in Preventing Human Trafficking: Recognize the Signs" (30 minutes) for free.
- **Refugee Services of Texas**: Offers "Survivor Services/HT101" (60 minutes) for free, available in-person.
- **PBJ Learning**: Offers a 26-minute course on "Human Trafficking Essentials: Texas Hospitality Awareness HB 390" for $24.99.
- **Compliance Learning Solutions**: Provides "Combating Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry" (45 Minutes) for $25.95.
