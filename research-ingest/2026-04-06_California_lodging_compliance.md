### Analysis of Human Trafficking Requirements and Model Notices in California

#### Overview
This report provides a detailed overview of human trafficking requirements, training obligations, and model notices for businesses and establishments in California, as per Senate Bill 1193 and other relevant legislation. It includes key points on the notice content, posting locations, languages required, and penalties for non-compliance.

### Key Points

1. **Human Trafficking Legislation**:
   - **Senate Bill 1193**: This law mandates that specified businesses post a human trafficking model notice informing the public and victims about hotline numbers for help or reporting unlawful activity.
   - **Additional Requirements (SB 225, AB 260)**: 
     - SB 225 requires the notice to include a specified number that victims can text for services and support.
     - AB 260 extends the requirement to hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns.

2. **Posting Requirements**:
   - Businesses are required to post the notice in English and Spanish.
   - For businesses located in specific counties subject to federal language assistance provisions under the Voting Rights Act, a third language is also required (e.g., Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese).

3. **Model Notice Content**:
   - The model notice includes instructions on how victims can receive help, report unlawful activity, and contact local law enforcement.
   - It provides hotline numbers for victims to seek assistance and text-based support services.

4. **Penalties for Non-Compliance**:
   - Failure to post the notice as required can result in fines or other penalties imposed by the California Attorney General's Office.

### Compliance Steps
Businesses must ensure they comply with these requirements by:
1. Posting the correct model notice in all necessary languages.
2. Verifying that the posted notices are placed in a visible and accessible location within the establishment.
3. Ensuring the notice is updated regularly to reflect any changes in hotline numbers or services.

### Resources

### Conclusion
Compliance with human trafficking requirements is crucial for businesses in California. By adhering to the model notice and posting it in all required languages, establishments can help protect their customers and comply with state law.
