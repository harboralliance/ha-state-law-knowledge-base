The Texas Attorney General's Office has established requirements for commercial lodging establishments to provide annual human trafficking training to their employees. This training is designed to educate staff on recognizing signs of human trafficking, the roles they can play in reporting suspected cases, and compliance with signage requirements. The law applies only to establishments with 10 or more rooms but encourages all lodging businesses to participate.

### Key Requirements:
- **Annual Training**: Employees must complete training not later than the 90th day after being hired.
- **Completion Certificate**: Approved providers issue a certificate of completion, which must include the date taken and course title. Operators must retain records for at least three years.
- **Signage Requirement**: Commercial lodging establishments must display specific human trafficking signage.
