### Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging in Texas

The Texas Legislature has passed House Bill 390 (87th Regular Session), which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. This annual training requirement only applies to commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, but it is encouraged for all lodging establishments in the State of Texas regardless of size.

#### Training Requirements

The approved training must cover several key areas:

1. **Identification of Human Trafficking**: Staff should learn how to recognize signs that an individual may be a victim of human trafficking.
2. **Reporting Mechanisms**: Employees need to understand who to contact if they suspect human trafficking and the proper procedures for reporting it, including calling 911 in emergencies.
3. **Employee Protection**: The training must ensure that employees are not disciplined or retaliated against for making good faith reports of suspected acts of human trafficking.

#### Training Signage

The OAG requires that commercial lodging establishments display specific human trafficking signage. This signage should be placed in visible locations to inform guests about the availability of services and resources for those who may need assistance.

#### Compliance Records

Operators of commercial lodging establishments must maintain records showing compliance with Section 114.0051, Texas Business and Commerce Code. These records must be available for review by the OAG, local, state, and federal law enforcement upon request.

### Conclusion

The requirement to undergo human trafficking training is an important step in protecting vulnerable individuals and ensuring that commercial lodging establishments play a role in combating this heinous crime. By providing comprehensive training, employees will be better equipped to identify signs of human trafficking and report it appropriately.
