### Overview

This response provides a comprehensive overview of the requirements and information related to human trafficking awareness training for employees of motels and hotels in California. The key points are sourced from three official California government documents:

1. **SOURCE 1: Summary of Training Requirement** (SB 970)
   - Requires hotel and motel employers to provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness.
   - Applies to employees likely to interact with victims of human trafficking, defined as those working in positions such as front desk, housekeeping, maintenance, or security.
   - Training must be completed by January 1, 2020.

2. **SOURCE 2: Human Trafficking FAQs** (California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking)
   - Confirms the requirement for training on human trafficking awareness, aligning with SOURCE 1.
   - Provides additional resources including contact numbers for hotlines such as the National Human Trafficking Resource Center.

3. **SOURCE 3: Model Notice for Posting** (Office of the Attorney General)
   - Details are consistent with previous information but emphasizes that posting requirements include language in English and Spanish, along with additional languages based on specific counties covered by federal Voting Rights Act provisions.

### Compliance and Enforcement

- **SOURCE 1** indicates that employers must provide initial training to employees employed as of July 1, 2019, by January 1, 2020. Training must also be provided to new employees within six months of their employment.
- **SOURCE 3** specifies posting mandates and language requirements for businesses located in counties subject to federal Voting Rights Act provisions.

### Additional Support and Resources

Both SOURCES 2 and 3 provide contact information for hotlines, such as the National Human Trafficking Resource Center (1-888-373-7888), which is a critical resource for anyone seeking assistance or reporting suspected human trafficking incidents.

### Conclusion

Compliance with California's human trafficking awareness training requirement involves providing 20 minutes of interactive classroom training to employees who may interact with victims of human trafficking. This must be completed by January 1, 2020, and should include new hires within six months. Posting model notices in specified languages is also required for businesses operating in certain counties.

For further support, individuals can contact the National Human Trafficking Resource Center or other resources provided through these documents.
