### Overview of the Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals

#### Purpose:
The Minnesota law mandates that hotels, motels, and short-term rentals provide training to all on-site employees regarding sex trafficking prevention and response. This training aims to equip staff with knowledge and skills necessary to identify signs of trafficking, report potential incidents, and ensure a safe environment for guests.

### Training Requirements:

1. **Who Must Take the Training:**
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees.
   - Exemptions include:
     - Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms.
     - Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant or catering services with no direct guest contact.
   
2. **Timing of Training:**
   - New employees must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake the training annually.

3. **Displaying the Poster:**
   - A specific back-of-house poster is required to be displayed in a location visible to all staff.

4. **Ongoing Awareness Campaign:**
   - Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are encouraged to conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees.

### Training Content:

The training package includes:
- A virtual presentation (video with guided exercises).
- A Training Companion Guide (PDF) as a resource.
- A Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF).
- Posters set (back of house and other locations).

### Accessibility and Languages:
- The training is available in English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong starting late fall 2023/early 2024.
- Contact `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us` if you need materials in additional languages.

### Compliance:
- Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals must ensure compliance with the law.
- Licensees are responsible for paying the costs of compliance (MDH does not provide assistance with printing posters).

### Questions and Answers:

1. **Do managers/owners and employees have to take the training every year?**
   - Yes, it is an annual requirement tied to licensure.

2. **Can we replace the MDH training with our own training materials?**
   - No, it cannot be replaced. In limited situations, a part may be replaced but only with prior review and approval by MDH.

3. **Are contracted workers, such as contracted cleaners, subject to the training?**
   - Yes, they are still required to take the training.

4. **Do whole house vacation rental properties subject to compliance with the statute?**
   - Yes, MDH licenses whole home vacation rentals as hotels/motels in most cases, subjecting them to following the statute.

### Contact Information:
For questions or assistance accessing the training, contact `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.

### Training Package Overview:
- **Duration:** 45-50 minutes (with potential for longer viewing of translated versions).
- **Package Components:**
  - Virtual presentation
  - Training Companion Guide (PDF)
  - Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)
  - Posters set

By providing this mandatory training, hotels and motels play a crucial role in preventing sex trafficking within their establishments.
