**Minnesota Sex Trafficking Prevention Training Requirements for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals**

The Minnesota legislature has enacted laws requiring specific training for staff in hotels, motels, and short-term rentals to help prevent sex trafficking. This includes both managers/owners and on-site employees.

### Key Points of the Legislation

1. **Training Requirement**:
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
   - New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
   - All staff must retake the training every year.

2. **Exceptions to Training Requirements**:
   - Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms.
   - Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant, providing catering services, or both (if they have no direct contact with guests or guest rooms).
   
3. **Displaying the Poster**:
   - The back of house poster is required to be displayed in all locations where staff are regularly present.

4. **Ongoing Awareness Campaigns**:
   - Managers/owners and employees must conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees regarding sex trafficking.

### Training Package

The training package includes:

- A virtual presentation (video with guided exercises).
- A Training Companion Guide.
- A Manager & Owner Training Toolkit.
- A poster set that includes back of house (required to display) along with other locations like housekeeping, restaurant/bar, front desk/lobby, and security.

### Accessing the Training

- **Managers and Owners**: Complete a pre-training survey before accessing the training package via MDH Learning Center.
- **Employees**: Access the employee training in their preferred language (English, Spanish/Español, Somali, Hmong).

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. **Do I still need to take the Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training if I already completed another training on sex trafficking?**
   - Yes, you need to complete this specific training as it is designed specifically for the Minnesota context.

2. **Is there any fee for the mandatory training package?**
   - No, the training is free.

3. **Is MDH providing assistance with costs of compliance such as printing posters?**
   - No, MDH does not provide financial assistance beyond the free training package. Licensees must cover the cost themselves.

4. **Are training materials available in other languages besides English?**
   - Training materials will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024. Contact MDH for other language needs.

5. **Do managers/owners and employees need to take the training every year?**
   - Yes, it is an annual requirement tied to licensure.
