### Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging in Texas

In response to House Bill 390, the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas requires certain commercial lodging establishments to provide annual human trafficking training for their employees. This legislation aims to increase awareness and help prevent human trafficking within the hospitality industry. Below are some resources and details on approved training programs.

#### Training Requirements
- **Applicability**: Applies only to commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms, but is encouraged for all lodging establishments regardless of size.
- **Frequency**: Annual training is required.
- **Documentation**: Operators must maintain records of employee training completion and make these records available upon request from the OAG, local, state, and federal law enforcement.
