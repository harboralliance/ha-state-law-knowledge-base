Based on the information provided, here are key points about commercial lodging training for human trafficking in Texas:

1. The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390 (87th Regular Session) requiring annual human trafficking training for employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms.

2. The training is intended to help staff identify and respond to potential signs of human trafficking in the hospitality industry. 

3. Training must be completed by new employees within 90 days of hire, and all existing employees must receive annual training.

4. The approved training videos include:
- "Human Trafficking Hotel Training" from the Office of the Attorney General (free online)
- "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry" from Etactics (free online or in-person)
- Various other organizations like Inhospitable to Human Trafficking, Extended Stay America, Marriott International, ECPAT-USA, etc.

5. The training must be approved by the Office of the Attorney General and delivered through an approved provider.

6. Training providers are required to provide a certificate of completion for each employee who completes the course.

7. Operators must retain records showing compliance with the law and make these records available for inspection.

The goal is to raise awareness about human trafficking risks in the hospitality industry and equip staff with the knowledge to recognize and respond appropriately if they encounter potential victims.
