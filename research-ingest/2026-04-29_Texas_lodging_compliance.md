### Overview of Human Trafficking Training for Commercial Lodging in Texas

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for workers directly employed by commercial lodging establishments. This training aims to provide opportunities for human trafficking to be reported to law enforcement by requiring human trafficking training for workers at these establishments.

### Key Requirements

1. **Training Frequency**: 
   - Annual training requirement for employees at commercial lodging establishments.
   
2. **Language and Format**:
   - Training must be available in English and Spanish, with both options meeting the OAG requirements.
   - Courses can be delivered online or in-person.

3. **Employee Rights**:
   - Employees are protected from discipline, retaliation, or other discrimination for making a good faith report of suspected human trafficking.

4. **Training Content**:
   - Training covers identifying and responding to human trafficking in the hospitality industry.
   - It includes information on how and why human trafficking occurs in the hospitality industry, specifically addressing commercial lodging's role in sexual trafficking.
   - Guidance is provided on reporting and responding to human trafficking incidents, including contacting appropriate entities like the National Human Trafficking Hotline.

5. **Certification**:
   - Approved training providers must provide a certificate of completion to every employee who completes the training.
   - The certificate must include the date the course was taken and the title of the course.
