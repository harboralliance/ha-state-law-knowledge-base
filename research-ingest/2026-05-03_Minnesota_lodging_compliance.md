### Key Points from the Training Package for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals in Minnesota

#### Training Requirements:
- **All Owners, Managers, and On-Site Employees Must Complete:** 
  - The required training.
  - Exceptions apply (e.g., employees under 16 unless they clean rooms).
  
- **New Employees:**
  - Must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
  
- **Annual Requirement:**
  - All staff must retake this training every year.

#### Training Content:
- **Duration:** 45 to 50 minutes (including translated versions)
- **Components:**
  - Virtual Presentation with guided exercises
  - Training Companion Guide
  - Manager & Owner Training Toolkit
  - Poster Set (Back of House, Housekeeping, Restaurant/Bar, Front desk/lobby, Security)

#### Display Requirements:
- **Back of House Poster:** Must be displayed in a location where all staff will see it.

#### Awareness Campaign:
- Managers and owners must conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees.

#### Compliance Costs:
- Licensees are required to pay the costs of compliance.
- MDH does not provide assistance with printing posters or other costs.

### Important Exemptions:
- **Employees Under 16:** Unless they clean rooms.
- **Restaurant Staff Only:** No direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

### Additional Information:
- Training is available in multiple languages including Spanish, Somali, and Hmong starting late fall 2023/early 2024. Requests for other language materials can be made to health.safeharbor@state.mn.us.
- The law covers whole house vacation rental properties if they are licensed as hotels/motels.

#### Contact Information:
For any questions or assistance accessing the training, contact:
**health.safeharbor@state.mn.us**

This training package is designed to empower hotel staff with knowledge and tools to prevent and respond to sex trafficking effectively.
