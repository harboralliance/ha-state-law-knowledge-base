### Overview of Approved Human Trafficking Training Videos for Commercial Lodging

The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390 in the 87th Regular Session, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for the hospitality industry. This training aims to provide additional opportunities for human trafficking to be reported to law enforcement by requiring employees directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments to undergo human trafficking training.

### Key Requirements:
1. **Annual Training Requirement**: Commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms are required to provide annual human trafficking training.
2. **Training Content**: The training must cover identifying individuals at risk of human trafficking, guidance on reporting suspected cases, and information on the required human trafficking signage for commercial lodging establishments.
3. **Certification**: Employees must be certified upon completing the training.
4. **Training Provider Approval**: Only approved training providers can offer the training.
