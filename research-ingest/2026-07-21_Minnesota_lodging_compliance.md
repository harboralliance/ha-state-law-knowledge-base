The provided text discusses a training program for hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff in Minnesota to help prevent and respond to human trafficking, specifically sex trafficking within lodging facilities. Here's a summary of key points:

### Training Requirements:
- **Who Must Take the Training:** 
  - All owners, managers, and on-site employees (with limited exceptions).
  - New employees must complete it within 90 days of hire.
  - Required staff must retake the training annually.
  
- **Training Content:**
  - Includes a virtual presentation with guided exercises.
  - A Training Companion Guide as a resource.
  - A Manager & Owner Training Toolkit for compliance.
  - Posters to be displayed in various locations within the facility.

### Training Package:
- The package includes:
  - Virtual Presentation
  - Training Companion Guide (PDF)
  - Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)
  - Back of House Poster set (required to display)
  
- Training Duration: Approximately 45-50 minutes, with potential for longer viewing times if translated versions are used.

### Access and Assistance:
- Managers/Owners can access the training through MDH Learning Center.
- Employees can access the training in multiple languages: English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):
1. **Do I need to take this training if I already took sex trafficking training?**
   - Yes, you still need to complete the Minnesota-specific training.

2. **Are resorts, bed and breakfasts, vacation shares, short-term rentals required to provide the training?**
   - Only those holding a hotel license are required.

3. **Does the law apply to tribal nations?**
   - No, but MDH encourages participation.

4. **Are any employees exempt from the training?**
   - Yes, exemptions include certain under-16 employees and those working exclusively in restaurants or providing catering services without direct guest contact.

5. **How can I check if I hold a correct license?**
   - Contact the Food, Pool, and Lodging section at MDH.

6. **Does MDH provide financial assistance for compliance with the law?**
   - No additional financial assistance is provided beyond the free training package.

7. **Are materials available in other languages?**
   - Training will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

### Key Takeaways:
- The training aims to equip hotel staff with knowledge about identifying and responding to potential cases of human trafficking.
- Compliance is mandatory for licensed facilities and involves annual retaking of the training.
- All employees, including managers and owners, are required to participate, except for specific exemptions.

This comprehensive approach helps to create a safer environment in lodging facilities by ensuring that staff are equipped with the necessary knowledge and skills to prevent and respond effectively to human trafficking.
