The Minnesota Sex Trafficking Prevention Training Requirement mandates that hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff receive specific training to help prevent sex trafficking. Key aspects include:

1. **Training Requirements**: 
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the training.
   - New hires must take it within 90 days of hiring.
   - Annual refresher training is required for all staff.

2. **Content Covered**:
   - Identifying signs of sex trafficking
   - Understanding legal requirements and responsibilities
   - Knowing how to respond in various situations

3. **Training Materials**:
   - Virtual presentation with guided exercises
   - Training Companion Guide
   - Manager & Owner Toolkit
   - Required back-of-house poster

4. **Displaying the Poster**:
   - The back-of-house poster must be displayed in a location where all staff can see it.

5. **Exceptions and Exemptions**:
   - Employees under 16 (unless they clean rooms)
   - Those working exclusively in restaurants
   - Employees with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms

6. **Non-Profit Compliance**:
   - Training is required only if the facility holds a hotel license.

7. **Costs and Assistance**:
   - The training package is free.
   - Licensees are responsible for printing posters, etc.

8. **Language Availability**:
   - Training materials will be available in English, Spanish, Hmong, and Somali starting late fall 2023/early 2024.

9. **Contact Information**:
   - For questions or assistance accessing the training, contact health.safeharbor@state.mn.us

The law aims to enhance awareness and preparedness among hotel staff to better identify and respond to potential instances of sex trafficking.
