Based on the provided sources, here is a summary of key information related to human trafficking training for commercial lodging establishments in Texas:

1. Legislation: 
- Passed House Bill 390 (2017) requiring annual human trafficking training for employees at commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms.
- The requirement is voluntary for smaller establishments.

2. Training Requirements:
- New employees must complete training within 90 days of hire.
- All employees must receive annual training.
- Employees may not be disciplined, retaliated against, or discriminated against for making a good faith report of suspected human trafficking.

3. Content:
Training should cover:
- Identification of human trafficking in the hospitality industry
- Role of commercial lodging establishments in addressing sex trafficking
- Different types of hotel contract workers vulnerable to labor trafficking
- Reporting and responding to human trafficking incidents

4. Approved Training Resources:
- Various organizations have developed approved training videos that meet the requirements.
- Some courses are free, while others require a fee or subscription.

5. Training Providers:
- The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) has an online portal with approved training resources.
- Other providers include Harbor Alliance, Unbound Now, Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking, Extended Stay America, Marriott International, ECPAT-USA, Refugee Services of Texas, PBJ Learning, and Compliance Learning Solutions.

6. Reporting Requirements:
- Employees must report suspicious activity related to human trafficking.
- Operators must maintain records showing compliance with the law.

7. Signage Requirement:
Training should include information on required human trafficking signage for commercial lodging establishments.

This summary provides an overview of the requirements and resources available for human trafficking training in the hospitality industry in Texas, as mandated by state law and implemented through approved training programs.
