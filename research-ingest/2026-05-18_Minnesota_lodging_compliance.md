The Minnesota Legislature passed a law that requires hotel, motel, and short-term rental owners, managers, and on-site employees to complete mandatory training on preventing and responding to sex trafficking within 90 days of hire. This requirement applies to all hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota, with a few exceptions for only parts of the package if applicable.

The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training includes:
- A virtual presentation – a video with guided exercises
- Training Companion Guide (PDF)
- Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF) to comply with the law
- Poster set (Back of House is required to display)

To complete the training, all employees must retake it every year. The law states that licensees are required to pay the costs of compliance, such as printing posters.

The Minnesota Department of Health developed this training package in partnership with Hospitality Minnesota. Managers and owners can access the training through the MDH Learning Center and will be asked to complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package.

Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant, providing catering services, or both are exempt from the training if they do not have direct contact with guests or guest rooms, but these exemptions do not apply to owners, operators, and managers.
