### Overview of Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals

The prevention and response to sex trafficking in hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are critical aspects of community safety and awareness. According to Minnesota law (Section 240A), these establishments must provide specific training to their staff. Below is a detailed overview of the requirements and details of this training.

#### Training Requirements

1. **Scope**:
   - This training applies to all hotel, motel, and short-term rental owners, managers, and on-site employees.
   - New employees must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake the training every year.

2. **Exceptions**:
   - Employees under 16 years old (unless they clean rooms).
   - Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant, providing catering services, or both.
   - Employees with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

3. **Display of Poster**:
   - Hotels and motels must display the required back-of-house poster prominently within their establishment.

4. **Awareness Campaigns**:
   - Managers and owners are encouraged to conduct ongoing awareness campaigns for employees about the signs and risks of sex trafficking.

#### Content of the Training

The Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training Package includes:

1. **Virtual Presentation**:
   - A video with guided exercises designed to educate staff on recognizing and responding to signs of sex trafficking.

2. **Training Companion Guide (PDF)**:
   - A resource for viewing the virtual presentation, which provides detailed information and examples.

3. **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)**:
   - Comprehensive resources tailored for managers and owners to help them comply with the training requirements.

4. **Poster Set**:
   - Multiple posters designed for display in different areas of the hotel or motel (e.g., back-of-house, housekeeping, restaurant/bar, front desk/lobby, security).

#### Access to Training

- **Managers and Owners**: Can access the training through the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) Learning Center. They will be required to complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the package.
- **Employees**: The employee training materials are available in multiple languages including English, Spanish/Español, Somali, and Hmong.

#### Additional Information

1. **Compliance Costs**:
   - Licensees are required to pay for the costs of compliance, which includes displaying the posters.

2. **Training Language Availability**:
   - Starting late fall 2023/early 2024, the training package will be offered in Spanish, Hmong, and Somali in addition to English. If other languages are needed, contact health.safeharbor@state.mn.us for assistance.

3. **Revisions and Updates**:
   - MDH provides updates to the training package by November 1 of each year.

4. **Non-Exempt Individuals**:
   - Owners, operators, and managers who do not have direct contact with guests are still required to take the training.

5. **Seasonal Employees**:
   - Seasonal employees must receive training within 90 days of starting work.

6. **Contracted Workers**:
   - Contracted workers, such as contracted cleaners, must also complete the training.

#### Conclusion

Compliance with the Minnesota law on sex trafficking prevention training is essential for hotel, motel, and short-term rental owners, managers, and employees to ensure their establishments are safe from this form of exploitation. Proper training can help identify signs of sex trafficking early and respond effectively, saving lives and fostering a safer community.

For more detailed information and support in accessing the training materials, contact health.safeharbor@state.mn.us or visit the Minnesota Department of Health's website.
