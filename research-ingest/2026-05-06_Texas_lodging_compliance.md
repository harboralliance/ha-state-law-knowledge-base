This text describes a program by the Texas Attorney General to provide training on human trafficking for workers in commercial lodging establishments. Specifically, it outlines:

1. The requirement under HB 390 that certain commercial lodging employers provide annual human trafficking training to their employees.

2. The content requirements for approved training programs, including information on identifying and responding to human trafficking, the role of hospitality industry staff in reporting trafficking, and the required display of anti-trafficking signage. 

3. A list of approved training videos from various organizations that meet these requirements, along with details such as course length, language, cost, delivery method, and contact information.

4. The OAG's role in approving and recommending these training programs for lodging employers to use.

The overall goal is to increase awareness and reporting of human trafficking by hospitality workers, which the Attorney General believes will help address this important social issue.
