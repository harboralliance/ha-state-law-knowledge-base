### Summary of Laws and Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals in Minnesota

#### Legal Requirements:
1. **Training Requirement**: 
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
   - New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake this training every year.

2. **Exceptions**:
   - Employees under 16 unless they clean rooms.
   - Employees who work exclusively in a restaurant, providing catering services, or both.
   - No direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

3. **Displaying the Poster**: 
   - Back of house poster must be displayed in all staff visible locations.

4. **Awareness Campaign**:
   - Managers and owners need to conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for their employees.

#### Training Content:
- The training package includes a virtual presentation, a Training Companion Guide (PDF), Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF), and a poster set.
- It is crucial for all hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota, with very few exceptions.

#### Access to Training:
- Managers and owners can access the training through the MDH Learning Center. 
- Employees need to complete a brief pre-training survey before accessing the training package.
  
#### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):
1. **Do I still need to take this training if I've already taken sex trafficking training?**
   - Yes, you must complete the Minnesota Lodging Sex Trafficking Prevention and Response Training as it is specific to Minnesota.

2. **Are resorts, bed and breakfasts, vacation shares, short-term rentals required to provide the training?**
   - Only those holding a hotel license are required.

3. **Does the law apply to tribal nations?**
   - No, but MDH encourages tribal nations to use the materials.

4. **What if I'm unsure about my licensing status?**
   - Contact health.foodlodging@state.mn.us or 651-201-4500 for clarification.

5. **Does MDH charge a fee for the mandatory training package?**
   - No, it is free.

6. **Do managers/owners and employees have to take the training every year?**
   - Yes, it is an annual requirement.

7. **Can we replace MDH training with our own materials?**
   - No, only limited exceptions are allowed after prior approval by MDH.

8. **If a hotel, motel or short-term rental owner/operator/manager does not have contact with guests, are they required to take the training?**
   - Yes, they must still complete the training.

9. **Does the law exempt seasonal employees?**
   - Not explicitly, so they must receive the training within 90 days of starting work.

10. **If an employee is temporary and only employed for two months, do they need to complete the training?**
    - Technically no, but MDH encourages it.

11. **Are contracted workers subject to the training?**
    - Yes, they must take the training.

12. **Do whole house vacation rental properties subject to compliance with the statute?**
    - Yes, as they are licensed as hotels/motels.

#### Contact Information:
- For questions and assistance, contact health.safeharbor@state.mn.us.

### Conclusion
The laws in Minnesota require all hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff to undergo mandatory sex trafficking prevention and response training annually. Non-compliance can lead to legal repercussions. Training must include specific content provided by the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) and be displayed as required. Compliance is crucial for protecting employees and preventing sexual exploitation in these establishments.
