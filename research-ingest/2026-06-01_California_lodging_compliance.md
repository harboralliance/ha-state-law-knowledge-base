## Summary of Requirements and Information

### Key Points:

1. **Training Requirement**: Hotel and motel employers must provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness to each employee who is likely to interact with or come into contact with victims of human trafficking.
   - **Effective Date**: January 1, 2020
   - **New Employees**: Training must also be provided within six months of employment.

2. **Training Content**: The training should cover the basics of identifying and responding to potential human trafficking scenarios, as well as resources available for victims.

3. **Notice Requirement**: Businesses required to post notices about the human trafficking hotline numbers must also provide a model notice with specific language requirements in English and Spanish. For businesses in certain counties subject to federal Voting Rights Act provisions, posting in a third language may be necessary.

### Detailed Information:

1. **Training Compliance**:
   - Hotel and motel employers need to ensure that their employees who interact with or come into contact with potential victims of human trafficking receive adequate training.
   - The training should cover basic human trafficking awareness, identification, and response procedures.
   - Employers must provide this training by January 1, 2020, for all employees employed as of July 1, 2019, and to new employees within six months of employment.

2. **Posting Requirement**:
   - Businesses subject to posting requirements (including hotels, motels, bed and breakfast inns) must post a model notice informing the public and victims about human trafficking hotline numbers.
   - The notice must be available in English and Spanish.
   - For businesses located in specific counties with language assistance provisions under the federal Voting Rights Act, posting in an additional third language may be required.

### Model Notice Information:
- **Availability**: The mandated model notice can be downloaded from the Office of Attorney General’s website.
- **Language Options**: Available in English, Spanish, and multiple other languages (Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, Cambodian, Chinese - Simplified/Traditional, Eastern Armenian, Farsi, Haitian Creole, Hindi, Hmong, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Laotian, Pashto, Polish, Punjabi, Romanian, Russian, Tagalog, Thai, Vietnamese).

### Additional Resources:
- **National Human Trafficking Resource Center**: Hotline: 1-888-373-7888 or Text: Be Free (233733)
- **California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST)**: Contact information provided on the website.

### Summary of Compliance:
To comply with the requirement, hotels and motels should ensure that all relevant employees receive appropriate training by January 1, 2020. Additionally, they must post the model notice in English and Spanish (and potentially another language if required) in a visible location within their establishments.

This information is crucial for businesses to understand and implement compliance measures, ensuring adherence to both state laws regarding human trafficking awareness and notice posting requirements.
