Based on the information provided in the sources, here are some key points about human trafficking training for commercial lodging establishments in Texas:

1. Requirement: House Bill 390 requires annual human trafficking training for employees of commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms. The law is encouraged for all lodging establishments regardless of size.

2. Training Content: Required training must cover:
- How human trafficking occurs in the hospitality industry
- Commercial lodging's role in addressing sex trafficking 
- Identifying different types of hotel contract workers vulnerable to labor trafficking
- Staff roles in reporting and responding to human trafficking

3. Approval Process: Training must be approved by the Office of the Attorney General (OAG). Approved videos are listed on the OAG website.

4. Sample Training Programs:
- Office of the Attorney General: "Human Trafficking Hotel Training" (22 mins, free online)
- Unbound Now: "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry" (25 mins each in English and Spanish, free online/in-person)

5. Delivery Methods: Many approved courses are available online for a fee. Some require registration or contact with the training organization.

6. Training Frequency: Employees must complete initial training within 90 days of hire, followed by annual training.

7. Signage Requirement: Approved training providers include information on required human trafficking signage for lodging establishments.

8. Reporting Requirements: Training must include guidance on contacting appropriate entities for reporting suspected human trafficking.

This legislation aims to increase awareness and reporting of human trafficking among hotel employees in Texas.
