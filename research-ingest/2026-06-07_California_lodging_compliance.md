## Summary

### Required Training for Hotel and Motel Employees in California:
**Training Requirement:** Yes  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2020  
**Duration of Training:** At least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training.  
**Target Group:** Employees likely to interact with victims of human trafficking (those working in areas like front desk, cleaning, and maintenance).  

### Posting Requirement:
**Notice Posting Requirement:** Yes  
**Effective Date:** April 1, 2013 for specified businesses including hotels and motels.  
**Languages Required:** English and Spanish. In certain counties, a third language may be required based on federal Voting Rights Act provisions.  
**Contact Information:** Telephone hotline numbers to seek help or report unlawful activity.

### Enforcement:
Failure to comply with posting requirements can result in fines. Failure to provide training could lead to disciplinary action against the employer, including the revocation of business licenses.

### Resources:
- **Model Notice:** Available on the California Attorney General’s Office website for downloading.
- **Training Materials:** The Office provides various language versions of model notices and additional resources.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Training Requirement
The new law (SB 970) mandates that by January 1, 2020, all hotel and motel employers provide training to their employees who are likely to interact with victims of human trafficking. This includes front desk staff, cleaning personnel, and maintenance workers.

- **Training Duration:** Each employee must receive at least 20 minutes of interactive training.
- **Frequency:** Initial training is required for all existing employees as of July 1, 2019. New employees also need this training within six months of starting their employment in roles likely to interact with victims.
- **Language:** Training can be provided in English or another language if required by federal law.

### Posting Requirement
In addition to providing training, employers must post a model notice informing the public and victims of human trafficking about available help resources. The notice must be posted in English and Spanish on all premises where hotel/motel employees interact with the public.

- **Posting Date:** April 1, 2013.
- **Languages Required:** The notice must be in both English and Spanish. In specific counties subject to federal Voting Rights Act provisions, a third language may also be required.
- **Contact Information:** The notice must include telephone hotline numbers for victims to seek help or report unlawful activity.

### Enforcement
Failure to comply with either the training or posting requirements can result in legal consequences:

- **Training Failure:** Employers may face disciplinary action against individual employees and potential revocation of business licenses.
- **Posting Failure:** Fines can be imposed for failure to post the model notice correctly.

### Resources and Support
- **Model Notice Template:** Available on the California Attorney General’s Office website for download.
- **Additional Training Materials:** The Office provides various language versions of model notices and additional resources, including:
  - Arabic, Brazilian (Portuguese), Cambodian, Chinese (Simplified/Traditional), Eastern Armenian, Farsi, Haitian Creole, Hindi, Hmong, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Laotian, Pashto, Polish, Punjabi, Romanian, Russian, Tagalog, Thai, and Vietnamese model notices.

These resources are intended to assist businesses in compliance with the new legislation and provide victims of human trafficking with easier access to support services.
