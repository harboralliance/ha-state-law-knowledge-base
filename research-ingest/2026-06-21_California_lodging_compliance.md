## Summary and Key Points

### Awareness Training Requirement for Motels and Hotels (Section 1)
- **Requirement**: Employees of motels and hotels must receive at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training on human trafficking awareness.
- **Timing**: This requirement applies to employees employed as of July 1, 2019. New hires who will be interacting with victims of human trafficking within six months of their employment are also required to receive this training.

### Posting Requirement for Model Notice (Section 2)
- **Model Notice Content**: Hotels, motels, and bed and breakfasts are required to post a model notice that includes telephone hotline numbers for help or reporting unlawful activity.
- **Posting Location**: The notice must be posted in English and Spanish. For businesses located in specific counties subject to federal language assistance provisions of the Voting Rights Act, posting in a third language is also required.

### Training and Posting Mandates (Section 3)
- **Penalties for Non-compliance**: Failure to post the model notice or provide required training results in penalties.
- **Downloadable Notice**: The mandated notice can be downloaded from the website provided.

### Additional Languages (Section 4)
- **Support for Multilingual Businesses**: Businesses must post notices in English and Spanish, with additional languages required in certain counties as specified by federal law.

## Detailed Breakdown

1. **Awareness Training Requirement**:
    - The requirement applies to all employees of motels and hotels who are likely to interact or come into contact with victims of human trafficking.
    - Employees employed as of July 1, 2019, must receive this training by January 1, 2020.
    - New hires within six months of employment must also receive the training.

2. **Posting Requirement**:
    - Hotels, motels, and bed and breakfasts are required to post a model notice that includes hotline numbers for help or reporting human trafficking.
    - The notice must be posted in English and Spanish.
    - In counties subject to federal language assistance provisions of the Voting Rights Act, additional languages (such as Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, Cambodian, etc.) may also need to be posted.

3. **Training and Posting Mandates**:
    - Businesses failing to comply with posting or training requirements will face penalties.
    - A downloadable model notice is provided on the website for businesses to use.

4. **Additional Languages**:
    - Support for multilingual businesses includes English, Spanish, and additional languages specified in certain counties as per federal law.

## Conclusion
The requirement for hotels and motels to provide human trafficking awareness training and post a model notice is designed to enhance employee knowledge and public awareness of potential cases of human trafficking. Non-compliance with these requirements can result in penalties.
