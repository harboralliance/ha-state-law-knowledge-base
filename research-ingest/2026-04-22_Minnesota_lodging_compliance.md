Based on the provided information, hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota are required to provide training to their employees on sex trafficking prevention and response. The training is mandated by state law and includes the following requirements:

1. All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
2. New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
3. All required staff must retake this training every year.

The training package typically consists of:
- A virtual presentation (video with guided exercises)
- Training Companion Guide
- Manager & Owner Training Toolkit
- Posters set

To comply, hotels and motels should display the required back-of-house poster and conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees. The training is available in multiple languages, including English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.

For more detailed information on who is exempt from the training or how to access it, individuals can contact health.safeharbor@state.mn.us.

The Minnesota Department of Health provides this training as a free resource and does not charge for compliance with the law. However, licensees are responsible for any additional costs associated with displaying posters or other materials, which must be borne by the licensee according to the state law.

Overall, the goal of this legislation is to educate hotel staff about the signs of sex trafficking and how they can help prevent it, thereby contributing to community safety and well-being.
