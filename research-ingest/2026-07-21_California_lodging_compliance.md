Based on the information provided in the documents, here are some key points about human trafficking and what organizations can do to help survivors:

1. Human trafficking is illegal under both criminal and civil law.

2. CRD (California Right's Defense) enforces California's human trafficking civil rights law.

3. CRD receives and investigates complaints of human trafficking.

4. Civil remedies may be available for survivors of trafficking. More information can be found at the "Human Trafficking FAQs" document linked on their website.

5. To learn more about how CRD can assist trafficking survivors, including potential civil remedies, review that document.

6. Additional resources to contact if in immediate danger or need help:
   - National Human Trafficking Resource Center: 1-888-373-7888
   - California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST): 1-888-KEY-2FRE(EDOM)

7. Motels/hotels in California are required by law to provide human trafficking awareness training to employees who interact with victims, effective January 1, 2020.

8. The California Attorney General's office provides resources for victims of trafficking:
   - Victims' Services Unit: (877) 433-9069
   - National Human Trafficking Hotline: (888) 373-7888

9. There are model notices that businesses must post informing the public and survivors of human trafficking how to seek help or report unlawful activity.

10. The State of California has enacted legislation (SB 1193) requiring certain businesses to post a human trafficking notice.

These resources and requirements aim to raise awareness, provide assistance, and protect victims of human trafficking in California.
