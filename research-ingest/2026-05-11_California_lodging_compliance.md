### Summary

The provided sources outline requirements for hotel and motel employers in California regarding human trafficking awareness training and posting model notices informing the public about human trafficking resources. 

#### Key Points:

1. **Training Requirement:**
   - Hotel and motel employers are required to provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training on human trafficking awareness to employees who interact with potential victims.
   - This training must be completed by July 1, 2019, for those hired before that date. New hires must complete the training within six months of their hire date.

2. **Posting Requirement:**
   - Businesses required to post the model notice include hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns.
   - The mandated notice is available on the website for downloading in multiple languages (English, Spanish, and additional languages as needed).
   - Each business must post the notice in English and Spanish. For businesses located in specific counties, a third language posting may also be required.

3. **Penalties for Non-Compliance:**
   - Failure to post the mandated notice can result in fines and penalties.

4. **Resources:**
   - The model notice is available on various government websites.
   - Victims of human trafficking can contact hotlines for assistance or report unlawful activity.

### References

2. **Source 3:**

### Conclusion

California mandates that hotel and motel employers provide human trafficking awareness training to their employees and post a model notice informing the public about human trafficking resources. Non-compliance can result in fines and penalties. Resources for assistance are available through various government agencies and hotlines.
