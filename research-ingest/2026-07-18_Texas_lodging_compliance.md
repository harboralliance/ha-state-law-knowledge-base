The training videos listed on the Texas Attorney General's website provide resources for commercial lodging establishments to ensure they are aware of and prepared to address human trafficking within their facilities. These trainings cover various aspects including identifying vulnerable workers, recognizing signs of trafficking, and reporting incidents.

For example, the "Human Trafficking Hotel Training" course by the Office of the Attorney General is a 22-minute online training that is free of charge. It provides an overview of human trafficking in the hospitality industry and guides staff on how to report suspected cases. Another option is the "Texas Commercial Lodging Human Trafficking Awareness and Response" course from National Course Portal, which offers English and Spanish versions and requires a one-time payment for individual learners or bulk discounts for groups.

These resources are crucial for maintaining compliance with state laws that require annual human trafficking training for employees of establishments with 10 or more rooms. They help ensure that workers in the hospitality industry can recognize and report suspected instances of trafficking, thereby protecting both the workers and the establishment from legal consequences.
