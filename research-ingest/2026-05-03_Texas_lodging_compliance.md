The information provided on the Texas Attorney General's website outlines a comprehensive program for commercial lodging establishments to combat human trafficking. The key components of this program include:

1. **Training Requirement**:
   - All commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms must provide annual human trafficking training to their employees.
   - Smaller establishments are encouraged but not mandated.
   - Training must be completed by new employees within the first 90 days of employment and annually thereafter.

2. **Approved Training**:
   - The Attorney General has approved several training videos that meet the requirements set forth by House Bill 390.
   - These videos cover various aspects of identifying, responding to, and preventing human trafficking in the hospitality industry.

3. **Training Organization Details**:
   - Each approved organization provides contact information for more specific course inquiries or additional details on their training methods (online, in-person, etc.).

4. **Signage Requirement**:
   - Commercial lodging establishments are required to display certain human trafficking signage within their premises.
   - The exact signage requirements can be found in the provided guidelines.

5. **Penalties for Non-Compliance**:
   - Employees who make a good faith report of suspected human trafficking cannot be disciplined, retaliated against, or discriminated against.
   - The owner/operator must maintain records showing compliance with the training requirement and make these records available upon request from the Attorney General or law enforcement agencies.

6. **General Information**:
   - For general inquiries about the program, email [email protected] is provided.
   - Specific questions about course content should be directed to the respective training organization.

This comprehensive program aims to educate employees and create a safer environment for guests by empowering them with knowledge on recognizing and responding to human trafficking situations.
