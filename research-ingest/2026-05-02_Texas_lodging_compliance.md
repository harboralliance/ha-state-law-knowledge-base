### Summary of Texas Human Trafficking Training Requirements and Resources

#### Overview:
The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, requiring the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for workers directly employed by certain commercial lodging establishments. This training is mandatory for establishments with 10 or more rooms but is encouraged for all lodging businesses. The training aims to increase awareness and improve response mechanisms regarding human trafficking.

#### Mandatory Requirements:
- **Annual Training**: Employees must complete the approved training within 90 days of hire and annually thereafter.
- **Training Content**: Must cover identifying individuals at risk, reporting procedures, the role of employees in responding to suspected trafficking, and required signage.
- **Discrimination Protection**: Employees cannot be disciplined for making good faith reports of suspected human trafficking.

#### Approved Training Providers:
The OAG provides a list of approved training videos. These include:

1. **Office of the Attorney General**
   - "Human Trafficking Hotel Training" (22 minutes, free, online)
   
2. **Etactics**
   - "Human Trafficking – Texas" (26 minutes, $1/user/month, online)

3. **Unbound Now**
   - "Identifying and Responding to Human Trafficking: Commercial Lodging & Hospitality Industry" (25 minutes, English/Spanish, free, online/in-person)
   
4. **Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking**
   - "Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training" (45-60 minutes, $25.95/user, online/in-person)

5. **Extended Stay America**
   - "Human Trafficking Training" (30 minutes, free, internal website)
   
6. **Marriott International**
   - "Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry" (60/80 minutes, free to Marriott associates)
   
7. **ECPAT-USA**
   - "Your Role in Preventing Human Trafficking: Recognize the Signs" (30 minutes, English/Spanish, free, online)

8. **Refugee Services of Texas**
   - "Survivor Services/HT101" (60 minutes, free, in-person)
   
9. **PBJ Learning**
   - "Human Trafficking Essentials: Texas Hospitality Awareness HB 390" (26 minutes, $24.99, online)

10. **Compliance Learning Solutions**
    - "Combating Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry" (45 minutes, $25.95, online)

### Key Points to Remember:
- **Compliance**: All commercial lodging establishments must comply with these requirements.
- **Training Content**: Essential topics include identification of trafficking victims, reporting mechanisms, employee roles in response, and anti-discrimination policies.
- **Resources**: The OAG provides free online resources, while paid courses offer more comprehensive training.

This overview ensures compliance with the new legislation and helps lodging establishments protect their staff and customers.
