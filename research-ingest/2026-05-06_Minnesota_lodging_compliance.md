### Overview

The training described pertains to preventing and responding to sex trafficking within hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota. This legislation requires specific actions from hotel staff to help identify signs of trafficking and know how to respond appropriately.

### Key Requirements:

1. **All Employees**:
   - Must complete the required training.
   - New employees must take it within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake this training every year.

2. **Training Content**:
   - Includes a virtual presentation and accompanying training companion guide.
   - Available in multiple languages: English, Spanish, Somali, and Hmong.

3. **Displaying the Poster**:
   - A mandatory back-of-house poster must be displayed where all staff can see it.

4. **Ongoing Awareness Campaign**:
   - Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are encouraged to conduct an ongoing awareness campaign for employees regarding sex trafficking.

5. **Accessing Training**:
   - Managers and owners access training through the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) Learning Center.
   - Employees can complete the training in their preferred language.

### Exceptions:

- Exemptions apply to certain types of staff who do not have direct contact with guests, such as employees under 16 unless they clean rooms or those working exclusively in restaurants providing catering services.
  
### Training Frequency and Legal Compliance:

The law mandates that this training is completed annually. Updates to the training package will be provided by November 1st each year.

### Costs and Support:

- The MN Department of Health (MDH) provides the free training package, but licensees are responsible for the costs associated with printing and displaying posters.
  
- MDH does not provide financial assistance beyond offering the training materials.

### Contact and Assistance:

For questions or assistance accessing the training, contact `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.

### Training Package Components:

The training package includes:
- A virtual presentation
- A Training Companion Guide
- Manager & Owner Training Toolkit

### Conclusion:

This legislation highlights the critical role hotels and motel staff play in preventing sex trafficking. Compliance with these requirements is mandatory for all licensed facilities, and MDH provides comprehensive resources to support this effort.
