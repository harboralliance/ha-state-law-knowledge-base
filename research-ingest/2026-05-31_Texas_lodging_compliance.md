The Office of the Attorney General (OAG) in Texas has approved several human trafficking training videos for commercial lodging establishments. These training resources are designed to educate employees on identifying and responding to potential instances of human trafficking within their establishments.

### Key Points:
1. **Requirement**: House Bill 390 requires certain commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms to provide annual human trafficking training to their employees.
2. **Approval Process**: The OAG must approve any training program before it can be used in compliance with the law.
3. **Resources Available**:
   - **Etactics**: "Human Trafficking – Texas" (English), $1/user/month
   - **National Course Portal**: "Texas Commercial Lodging Human Trafficking Awareness and Response" (English & Spanish)
   - **Harbor Alliance, Inc.**: "Harbor Alliance Human Trafficking Prevention Training"
   - **Unbound Now**: Two versions each for English and Spanish
   - **Inhospitable to Human Trafficking**: "Inhospitable to Human Trafficking Training" ($25.95)
   - **Extended Stay America**: "Human Trafficking Training" (Free online via internal website)
   - **Marriott International**: "Recognize and Respond: Addressing Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry" (Free for Marriott associates)
   - **ECPAT-USA**: "Your Role in Preventing Human Trafficking: Recognize the Signs"
   - **Refugee Services of Texas**: "Survivor Services/HT101" (In-person training)
   - **PBJ Learning**: "Human Trafficking Essentials: Texas Hospitality Awareness HB 390"
   - **Compliance Learning Solutions**: "Combating Human Trafficking in the Hospitality Industry"

### Training Content:
- **Identification of Human Trafficking**: Each video includes detailed instructions on how to identify signs of human trafficking within the hospitality industry.
- **Reporting Procedures**: Clear guidelines on what actions employees should take when they suspect human trafficking and where to report it (e.g., National Human Trafficking Hotline, local law enforcement).
- **Employee Support Policies**: Information on how employers should treat employees who make good faith reports about potential trafficking incidents.

### Benefits:
- **Compliance with Law**: Ensures that commercial lodging establishments are in compliance with the requirements of House Bill 390.
- **Enhanced Security**: Educates employees to better recognize and respond to threats, potentially preventing instances of human trafficking.
- **Employee Protection**: Provides clear guidelines for reporting incidents without fear of retaliation.
