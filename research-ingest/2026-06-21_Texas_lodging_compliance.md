The **Office of the Attorney General (OAG)** in Texas requires hotels and commercial lodging establishments to provide human trafficking training annually. This training is mandatory for establishments with 10 or more rooms but is encouraged for all lodging businesses.

### Key Points:

- **Requirement:** All employees of commercial lodging establishments must receive annual human trafficking training.
- **Content:** The training covers recognizing the signs of human trafficking, reporting procedures, and the importance of employee safety and well-being.
- **Training Provider Approval:** Training providers must be approved by the OAG before they can offer their courses.
- **Certification:** Employees must complete a certified course to receive a certificate that shows they have been trained.
