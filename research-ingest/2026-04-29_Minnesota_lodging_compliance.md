The training for hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota to prevent sex trafficking is a legal requirement that all owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete. This training covers several key points:

1. **Training Requirements**: 
   - All new employees must complete the training within 90 days of hire.
   - All required staff must retake this training every year.

2. **Displaying the Back of House Poster**:
   - Owners and managers are required to display a specific back of house poster that includes important information about sex trafficking prevention and response.

3. **Conducting an Ongoing Awareness Campaign**:
   - Managers and owners need to conduct ongoing awareness campaigns for all employees to ensure they remain informed and can respond effectively in case of suspected sex trafficking incidents.

4. **Training Content**:
   - The training includes a virtual presentation with guided exercises, a Training Companion Guide, a Manager & Owner Training Toolkit, and a poster set.
   - It covers signs of sex trafficking, how to recognize it, and what actions to take if a suspicion arises.

5. **Exceptions**:
   - There are some exceptions for certain employees who do not have direct contact with guests or guest rooms (e.g., room cleaners under 16).

6. **Accessing the Training**:
   - Managers and owners can access the training through MDH’s Learning Center and will be asked to complete a brief pre-training survey.

7. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**:
   - There are numerous FAQs to help clarify any doubts about compliance requirements, exemptions, and what employees need to do post-training.

This comprehensive approach ensures that hotels and motels are equipped with the necessary knowledge and resources to play their crucial role in preventing sex trafficking within their establishments.
