### Overview:

The training package for hotels, motels, and short-term rentals in Minnesota to prevent and respond to sex trafficking is a critical initiative aimed at enhancing employee awareness and competence in identifying and addressing this serious issue.

#### Key Features:
1. **Virtual Presentation**: A video with guided exercises.
2. **Training Companion Guide (PDF)**: Provides additional resources for viewing the virtual presentation.
3. **Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF)**: Instructions on how to comply with the law effectively.
4. **Posters**:
   - Back of House (required to display)
   - Housekeeping
   - Restaurant/Bar
   - Front desk/lobby
   - Security

#### Compliance Requirements:
- All owners, managers, and on-site employees must complete the required training.
- New employees must take this training within 90 days of hire.
- All required staff must retake this training every year.

### Training Content:
The training package covers essential topics such as:
- Understanding sex trafficking
- Identifying the signs and indicators of sex trafficking
- Steps to take if a suspicion arises
- Reporting procedures

### Accessing the Training:
1. **Managers/Owners**: Complete a pre-training survey and access the training through MDH Learning Center.
2. **Employees**: Access training in English, Spanish, Somali, or Hmong based on their preference.

### FAQs:
1. **Who needs to take the training?**
   - All hotel, motel, and short-term rental staff who have contact with guests are required to take the training.

2. **Are there any exemptions?**
   - Employees under 16 (unless cleaning rooms), employees working exclusively in restaurants or bars, and those without direct contact with guests may be exempt.

3. **What if I’m unsure about my licensing?**
   - Contact MDH’s Food, Pool, and Lodging section at `health.foodlodging@state.mn.us` or 651-201-4500 for clarification.

4. **Does MDH provide financial assistance?**
   - No, MDH does not charge fees and does not assist with printing posters; licensees are responsible for the costs of compliance.

### Additional Resources:
- MDH offers materials in multiple languages starting late fall 2023/early 2024. If needed, contact `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.
- Regular updates to the training package will be provided by November 1 each year.

### Contact Information:
For any questions or assistance accessing the training, email `health.safeharbor@state.mn.us`.

This comprehensive training is a vital step in safeguarding Minnesota communities from sex trafficking.
