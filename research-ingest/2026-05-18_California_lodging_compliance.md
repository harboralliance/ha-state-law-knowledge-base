### Detailed Breakdown

#### Legal Requirements and Training

**SB 970 (2019)**:
- **Requirement**: Hotel and motel employers must provide at least 20 minutes of training regarding human trafficking awareness to employees likely to interact with victims.
- **Timing**:
  - Employees employed as of July 1, 2019.
  - New employees within six months of their employment.

This legislation aims to ensure that employees in the hospitality sector are aware of potential indicators of human trafficking and know how to respond appropriately.

#### Model Notice

**Senate Bill 1193 (2013)**:
- **Requirement**: Certain businesses must post a notice informing the public about human trafficking hotline numbers.
- **Businesses Covered**:
  - Hotels, motels, and bed and breakfast inns.
- **Posting Requirements**:
  - Notices must be posted in English and Spanish.
  - For businesses in specific counties, an additional language may be required based on language assistance provisions of the federal Voting Rights Act.

The notice should include information on how victims can seek help or report unlawful activity through hotline numbers.

#### Resources

The California Department of Justice provides various resources for compliance:
- **Multiple Language Versions**: English, Spanish, and additional languages as needed.
- **Hotline Numbers**:
  - National Human Trafficking Resource Center: (888) 373-7888 or TEXT: Be Free
  - California Coalition to Abolish Slavery and Trafficking (CAST): (888) KEY-2FRE(EDOM) or (888) 539-2373

These resources are designed to assist victims of trafficking and provide guidance on what to do in immediate danger.
