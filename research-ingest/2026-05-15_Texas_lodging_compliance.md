The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for commercial lodging establishments in order to provide additional opportunities for human trafficking to be reported to law enforcement. The training must be completed by new employees within 90 days of being hired and all employees must receive annual training. Approved training videos are available from various organizations, including the OAG itself, Etactics, Unbound Now, Businesses Ending Slavery and Trafficking, Extended Stay America, Marriott International, ECPAT-USA, Refugee Services of Texas, PBJ Learning, and Compliance Learning Solutions. The cost varies depending on the organization providing the training.
