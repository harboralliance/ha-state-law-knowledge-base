## Human Trafficking Training Resources for Commercial Lodging Establishments in Texas

### Overview:
The Texas Legislature passed House Bill 390, which requires the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) to approve training for the hospitality industry. This training is aimed at providing employees with the knowledge and skills needed to identify and respond to human trafficking. The requirement applies specifically to commercial lodging establishments with 10 or more rooms but is encouraged for all lodging establishments regardless of size.

### Key Requirements:
1. **Training Content**: Training must cover identifying red flags, recognizing human trafficking in the hospitality industry, reporting suspected cases, and understanding the role of hotel staff.
2. **Frequency**: Employees must receive annual training.
3. **Documentation**: Establishments must retain records of completed training for three years upon request by OAG or law enforcement.
4. **Certification**: Employees who complete the training must be issued a certificate.
