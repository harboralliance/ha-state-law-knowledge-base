### Summary

**General Requirement:**
Hotel and motel employers in California are required to provide 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness to employees who are likely to interact with victims of human trafficking.

**Effective Date:**
This requirement takes effect by January 1, 2020.

**Specific Requirements for Employees:**
- Employees employed as of July 1, 2019
- New employees within six months of their employment

**Exceptions:**
Employers who have provided this training to an employee on or before January 1, 2019 are not required to provide additional training.

**Training Content:**
The training must cover awareness and education about human trafficking and its impact on victims, as well as the resources available for assistance.

### Sources

- **SOURCE 1:** The California legislature passed Senate Bill 970 (SB 970), which requires hotel and motel employers to provide at least 20 minutes of classroom or other effective interactive training regarding human trafficking awareness.
  
- **SOURCE 2:** The California Attorney General's office provides detailed information on this requirement, including a model notice that businesses must post.

- **SOURCE 3:** The California Department of Justice offers additional resources and guidelines for compliance with the human trafficking awareness training requirement.

### Compliance

To comply with this requirement:

1. **Training Content:** Ensure that the training covers the following:
   - Understanding of human trafficking
   - Identification of victims of human trafficking
   - Awareness of resources available for assistance (e.g., national hotline numbers)
   
2. **Documentation:** Keep records of training provided, including dates and documentation of completion.

3. **Posting Requirements:** Ensure that a notice informing the public and employees about the human trafficking hotline is posted in English and Spanish, and in additional languages as required by law.

### Enforcement

Non-compliance with this requirement may result in fines and penalties as outlined in the relevant state laws.

**Enforcement Authority:**
- California Attorney General's office
- Local law enforcement agencies

### Conclusion

Hotel and motel employers in California must provide 20 minutes of human trafficking awareness training to employees who interact with victims, effective by January 1, 2020. Compliance involves providing the required training content, documenting it, and ensuring that a notice about the hotline is posted as per state regulations.
