### Minnesota Law on Sex Trafficking Prevention Training for Hotels, Motels, and Short-Term Rentals

#### Overview:
Minnesota has enacted laws requiring certain businesses, including hotels, motels, and short-term rentals, to provide sex trafficking prevention training to their employees. This training is designed to help prevent and respond to sex trafficking within these establishments.

#### Key Requirements:

1. **Who Must Take the Training:**
   - All owners, managers, and on-site employees of hotels, motels, and short-term rentals must complete the required training.
   - Some exceptions include:
     - Employees under 16 years old unless they are involved in room cleaning.
     - Employees who work exclusively in restaurants or food service establishments.
     - Those with no direct contact with guests or guest rooms.

2. **Timing of Training:**
   - New employees must complete the training within 90 days of their hire date.
   - All required staff members must retake the training every year.

3. **Training Content:**
   - The training covers recognizing signs of sex trafficking and how to respond appropriately.
   - It includes a virtual presentation with guided exercises, a Training Companion Guide, and other resources.

4. **Display of Poster:**
   - All employees must be trained in the use of a required back-of-house poster that displays information on identifying and responding to sex trafficking.

5. **Awareness Campaigns:**
   - Hotels, motels, and short-term rentals are encouraged to conduct ongoing awareness campaigns for all their employees regarding sex trafficking prevention.

#### Training Materials:

- The training materials include:
  - A virtual presentation.
  - A Training Companion Guide (PDF).
  - A Manager & Owner Training Toolkit (PDF).

#### Implementation Steps:

1. **Accessing the Training:**
   - Managers and owners can access the training through the MDH Learning Center.

2. **Employee Training:**
   - Employees should be trained in their preferred language:
     - English
     - Spanish
     - Somali
     - Hmong

3. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):**
   - The FAQs provide detailed information on exceptions, licensing requirements, and other relevant details.

4. **Training Completion:**
   - After completing the training, employees should fill out a roster of names along with the date of completion.
   - This roster should be kept on file for inspection but does not need to be submitted to MDH for compliance verification.

### Conclusion:
The Minnesota law on sex trafficking prevention training for hotels, motels, and short-term rentals is a critical step in combating sex trafficking within these establishments. By providing mandatory training to all relevant employees, these businesses can better recognize and respond to signs of sex trafficking, ultimately helping to prevent such crimes from occurring.
